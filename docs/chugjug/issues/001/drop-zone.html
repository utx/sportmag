Map guide · Chapter 7, Season 4: Override

Every match starts with the same question, asked while plummeting out of a flying bus: where do I land? Pick a busy spot and you'll find great loot and twelve people who want it too. Pick a quiet one and you'll live longer but might be holding a grey pistol when the circle closes. Below is the actual Chapter 7, Season 4 map, with our markers on it. Study it before your next drop.

Word Watch

Contestedadjective
Fought over. A contested landing spot is one lots of players head for at once.
Trajectorynoun
The path something follows as it moves through the air, like you after you jump off the bus.
Perimeternoun
The outside edge of an area. Landing on the perimeter of the map is usually safer.

Hot dropsFor the brave

  • Nightmare Neighborhood (north): brand new for Fortnitemares, with a horror-film boss guarding a Legendary weapon. Expect a crowd, and a very contested first minute.
  • Green Hill Zone (west): the season's headline area, inspired by a famous speedy hedgehog's world, with big loot and Rings that build up extra Overshield. Its boss left the Island in the Fortnitemares update, but it's still busy every single game.
  • The pizza restaurant (north, near the middle): animatronic robots lurk here. Beat one to possess its suit.

Smart dropsFor the clever

  • Stone Sanctum (south of centre): find the two Vault Keys hidden around the area to open the Temple Vault, packed with rare weapons and Sprites.
  • Reality's Reign and Geno's machine (dead centre): the story's beating heart. Central spots are great for reaching the circle, but every rotation passes through, so don't stay long.

Safe dropsFor the patient

The places we've marked in green, Chopped Shop (far west), Heatwave Harbor (south-west) and Cluster Coast (south-east), sit on the perimeter of the map, far from the centre and the big bosses. You'll usually have time to loot in peace. The catch: the storm may start on the other side of the Island, so keep an eye on the circle and leave early.

Tip 1
Read the bus line

Places right under the bus's path get crowded. Places a long glide away from it are quieter. Plan your trajectory before you jump.

Tip 2
Count the gliders

As you fall, look down and count how many gliders are heading to your spot. More than three? Change your mind in the air.

Tip 3
Console, then move

Grabbing an Override Console tells everyone where you are. Take it, then relocate quickly.

The Chug Jug pick

Heatwave Harbor. It's close enough to the southern forests to reach the circle, far enough from the crowds to loot properly, and the coast gives you somewhere to retreat if a squad shows up.
