Storm Warning

Nobody is giving away free V-Bucks. How to spot the scams, protect your account, and never lose your locker.

Stay safe · Read this one with a grown-up

Here's the most important page in the magazine, and it isn't about winning. Every day, people try to trick Fortnite players into handing over their accounts, usually with the promise of free V-Bucks. If someone steals your account, they can take everything in your locker. So let's make you scam-proof.

Word Watch

Scamnoun
A trick to cheat someone out of money or something valuable.
Gullibleadjective
Easily tricked into believing something. Scammers hunt for gullible people. Don't be one.
Authenticadjective
Real and genuine, not fake.
Vigilantadjective
Watchful and alert for danger.

The golden ruleThere are no free V-Bucks

Websites, videos and messages that promise "free V-Bucks generators" are scams, every single one. They want your username and password, or they want you to download something nasty. The only real ways to get V-Bucks are buying them in the game, using an authentic gift card from a proper shop, or earning them through the battle pass.

Scam or real?
"Free V-Bucks! Enter your login"

Scam. No real offer ever asks for your password.

Scam or real?
"I'm an Epic employee, send your code"

Scam. Epic staff will never ask for your password or your security codes.

Scam or real?
"Buy my rare account cheap!"

Scam, and against the rules. Buying or selling accounts can get them banned.

Scam or real?
"Download this aimbot"

Scam, and cheating. Cheats get accounts banned, and many are viruses in disguise.

Lock it downFour ways to protect your account

  1. Turn on two-factor authentication. Ask a grown-up to help you set it up in your Epic account settings. Then, even if someone guesses your password, they can't get in without a second code.
  2. Use a strong, secret password, and never use it for anything else.
  3. Never share your login, not even with a best friend. Friendships change; passwords should too.
  4. Ask about parental controls. Epic's parental controls let grown-ups set spending limits and other settings. It's not a punishment; it's a safety net.

Bought something by mistake?

Fortnite has a Cancel Purchase option for recent buys, and a limited number of Return Tickets for refunds. Some items, like certain passes, can't be returned, so check before you buy. If anything strange happens to your account, tell a grown-up straight away and contact Epic's official player support. Stay vigilant, and don't be gullible.
