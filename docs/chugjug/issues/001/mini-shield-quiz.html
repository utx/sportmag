Mini Shield Quiz

Ten questions on this issue. Each correct answer is worth 10 shield. Can you reach a full 100?

Answers are hidden. No peeking.

Ten questions, each worth 10 shield. Write down A, B, C or D, then check your answers. Can you reach a full 100?

  1. Who slotted the Cursed Cartridge into the Override console?
    1. Kado Thorne
    2. Geno
    3. Mali
    4. The bus driver
    AnswerB. Geno, this season's villain.
  2. Which location replaced The Battlewoods?
    1. Freaky Fields
    2. Stone Sanctum
    3. Nightmare Neighborhood
    4. Heatwave Harbor
    AnswerC. Nightmare Neighborhood.
  3. What does the Bonerattler SMG do when you aim down its sights?
    1. Fires pumpkins
    2. Heals you
    3. Reveals enemies through walls
    4. Turns you invisible
    AnswerC. It reveals enemies through walls.
  4. What does "ADS" stand for?
    1. Always Drop South
    2. Aim Down Sights
    3. Attack, Defend, Survive
    4. Auto Damage Shield
    AnswerB. Aim down sights.
  5. Which Sprite lets you dash, with charges that refill over time?
    1. Vampire
    2. Honey
    3. Dumpster Dive
    4. Spooky Dash
    AnswerD. Spooky Dash.
  6. In Ranked matches, Sprites…
    1. Are banned completely
    2. Appear, but their abilities are switched off
    3. Are twice as powerful
    4. Only appear on Tuesdays
    AnswerB. They appear, but with abilities disabled.
  7. Which classic outfit, first seen in Chapter 1 Season 4, was in the Item Shop this week?
    1. Vertex
    2. Geno
    3. Wrixel
    4. Nitemare Hack
    AnswerA. Vertex, from 2018.
  8. What do you and a friend both get if you gift them the Override battle pass?
    1. 1,000 V-Bucks
    2. The Peril Peak Mali style
    3. A Chug Jug
    4. Nothing
    AnswerB. The Peril Peak Mali style and accessories.
  9. Swizzy became the first player to do what?
    1. Win without building
    2. Win back-to-back FNCS titles
    3. Score 1,000 points
    4. Win solo and duos on the same day
    AnswerB. Win back-to-back FNCS titles.
  10. Word Watch: if you are vigilant, you are…
    1. Sleepy
    2. Easily tricked
    3. Watchful and alert
    4. Very fast
    AnswerC. Watchful and alert, especially for scams.

How full is your shield?

100: Full shield. You're ready for anything.
70–90: Big Shield Potion energy. Very impressive.
40–60: A couple of minis. Read Drop Zone again.
0–30: You're one-shot! Have another go.
