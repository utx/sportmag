Every word from this issue's Word Watch boxes, in one place. Drop three of them into conversation this week. Extra respect if you use one in the lobby.

Discontinueverb
To stop making or providing something. p.30

Impulsenoun

Never stopping or easing off, like wave after wave of Cube Monsters. p.30
Replenishverb

Restoreverb

Silhouettenoun
