Thank the bus driver

Welcome aboard! This is The Chug Jug: a magazine for Fortnite players who want to know what's new, where to land, what's worth buying and how the pros do it, explained properly and without the fluff.

Like a real Chug Jug, every issue should top you right up. You'll find the real map with our markers on it, honest Item Shop verdicts, a weapon tier list, the latest competitive news and the odd terrible pun. (Fine, lots of terrible puns.) You'll also spot Word Watch boxes. Tap a highlighted word to see what it means; they're all collected in the Jug of Words at the back.

One promise: we will never tell you that you need to buy anything. Fortnite is free, and the best things in it, like a clutch Victory Royale, cost nothing at all.

— The editors. Now jump.
