Shake it up

Bonerattlers, stake shotguns and a broom you can ride. Our tier list of the Fortnitemares loot pool, and the loadout we'd carry.

Loadout lab · Ratings are our opinion

Fortnitemares has shaken up the loot pool like a fizzy Chug Jug. A new SMG, a Legendary from a boss, a broom you can ride, and a pile of old favourites dug back up. So what's actually worth carrying? Here's our tier list. Remember: it's our opinion, and the best weapon is always the one you're good with.

Word Watch

Versatileadjective
Able to do lots of different jobs well. A versatile weapon works at many ranges.
Mobilitynoun
The ability to move around quickly and easily.
Arsenalnoun
A collection of weapons. (Also a football club in London, but that's a different magazine.)

Jargon busterWhat does "ADS" mean?

ADS stands for aim down sights: holding the aim button so your character lifts the weapon and looks through its sight. You move more slowly but shoot more accurately. The Bonerattler's special trick only works while you ADS, which is why it's so strong for checking a building before you go in.

Our pickThe loadout we'd carry

  1. Heavy Assault Rifle: your versatile main weapon for mid-range fights.
  2. Wood Stake Shotgun: for when someone builds right up to your face.
  3. Bonerattler SMG: close-range spray, plus wall-vision scouting.
  4. Witch Broom: mobility. Escapes, rotations and flying over the fog.
  5. Healing: Quack Snacks or whatever potions you find. Never fight on low health.

If you beat the Nightmare Neighborhood boss, Freddy's Nightmare is a powerful Legendary to add to your arsenal. (Scare warning: the boss is a horror-film villain, so it's fine to skip him.)

Fun fact: the slurp glow

Fortnite has a known bug this update: drink a Shield Potion and the shiny "slurp glow" effect can stay on your character for the rest of the match. Epic says it's fixing it in update 42.40. Until then, you'll sparkle. Embrace it.
