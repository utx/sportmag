The Touchline quiz

Twelve questions on this issue. Score ten or more and you may call yourself a pundit.

Answers are hidden. No peeking.

All the answers are somewhere in this issue. Grab a pencil, write down A, B, C or D for each one, then check your score at the end. No flicking back. Well, maybe a little.

  1. How quickly did Pablo Barrios score for Atlético against United?
    1. After 47 seconds
    2. After 5 minutes
    3. Just before half-time
    4. In stoppage time
    AnswerA. Sky Sports timed it at 47 seconds.
  2. Why was Leny Yoro sent off?
    1. Two yellow cards
    2. Arguing with the referee
    3. Denying an obvious goalscoring opportunity
    4. Handball on the line
    AnswerC. After a VAR review. Michael Carrick called it "very soft and slow".
  3. The Leeds v United rivalry is linked to which part of history?
    1. The Hundred Years' War
    2. The Wars of the Roses
    3. The Battle of Hastings
    4. The Gold Rush
    AnswerB. Lancashire's red rose against Yorkshire's white rose.
  4. In the Data desk chart, what is a "key pass"?
    1. Any pass longer than 30 metres
    2. A pass back to the goalkeeper
    3. A pass that leads straight to a teammate's shot
    4. A pass from a corner
    AnswerC. It counts even if the shot doesn't go in.
  5. How many Premier League assists did Bruno Fernandes make in 2025–26, a new record?
    1. 18
    2. 20
    3. 21
    4. 25
    AnswerC. 21, one more than Thierry Henry and Kevin De Bruyne.
  6. If 10% of players are top scorers and 10% are top creators, and the two had nothing to do with each other, what proportion would you expect to be both?
    1. 1%
    2. 10%
    3. 20%
    4. 50%
    AnswerA. 10% of 10% is 1%: one player in every hundred.
  7. Which front three had the most goals plus assists in our Face-off?
    1. Bayern Munich
    2. Chelsea
    3. Barcelona
    4. Real Madrid
    AnswerC. Barcelona: 24 goals and 16 assists.
  8. Which team holds the record for the best winning start to a La Liga season?
    1. Barcelona, 2013–14
    2. Real Madrid, 1968–69
    3. Atlético Madrid, 2013–14
    4. Sevilla, 1940–41
    AnswerB. Real Madrid won their first nine in 1968–69.
  9. Why will Vinícius Júnior miss El Clásico?
    1. He is injured
    2. He is on international duty
    3. He has a four-match ban
    4. He is resting
    AnswerC. Two games for the hair-pull and two for his reaction to the red card.
  10. By our count, how many major trophies have Sydney FC won in the A-League era?
    1. 9
    2. 11
    3. 12
    4. 15
    AnswerC. 12. The graphic said 11, but it missed one of their two Australia Cups.
  11. What was the score at half-time in AIK v Brommapojkarna?
    1. 0–0
    2. 1–5
    3. 3–3
    4. 5–5
    AnswerA. 0–0. Then came eleven goals.
  12. Word Watch: an outlier is…
    1. A player who has been sent off
    2. A dot that sits far away from all the others in a set of data
    3. The line that marks the edge of the pitch
    4. A team that never wins
    AnswerB. Like Bruno Fernandes, way out on the right of the chart.

How did you do?

11–12: Pundit. Television is waiting for you.
8–10: A creator and a scorer. You're in the magnificent 17.
5–7: Solid squad player. Have another look at the Data desk.
0–4: Did you leave at 1–5? Have another go. Stay until the final whistle this time.
