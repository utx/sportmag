Last orders for Override

Fortnite's final update of the season lands today, with two new Sprites and a free skin still up for grabs. Here's your end-of-season checklist.

Games desk · Chapter 7, Season 4: Override · update 42.40

Every Fortnite season ends the same way: a last burst of new stuff, a countdown, and then the map changes forever. Chapter 7, Season 4 ("Override") is nearly done. Its final big update, 42.40, arrives today, Thursday 15 October, and Fortnitemares, the Halloween event, runs until 1 November. That gives you about two weeks to tick off everything on your list. Here's how to make the most of them.

Update 42.40What's new today

The servers go down for the update at 4am in New York, which is 7pm Thursday in Sydney, and should be back about two hours later. If you were planning an evening session, plan dinner instead.

The big news is Sprites, the little helpers you can collect this season. Early patch notes say 42.40 adds two more, including a Honey Sprite that sends a swarm of bees after anyone who damages you. These are expected to be the last Sprites of the season, so if you want them in your collection, the deadline is the end of October. Leaked patch notes also mention more Match Overrides and new Loot Hacks, but Epic hasn't confirmed every detail yet, so treat those as rumours until you see them in-game.

New · today
Honey Sprite

Fight back by proxy: damage its owner and you'll have bees to deal with. Brilliant for punishing sneaky third-partiers.

Halloween
Pumpkin Sprite

Slowly grows a pumpkin head that protects you from headshots. Silly-looking, seriously useful.

Halloween
Vampire Sprite

Gives you siphon: health back when you deal damage, up to half of it at max level. Perfect for players who like to keep pushing.

Free stuffGrab Jack-A-Nape

This year's Fortnitemares free outfit is Jack-A-Nape, and every player can unlock it without spending a single V-Buck. You just need to complete the Fortnitemares event quests, which you'll find in the Quests tab. If you prioritise the quests you can finish in one match, you'll get through them much faster.

A note on the spooky stuff

Fortnitemares always includes some collaborations with horror films and games that are made for older teenagers and adults. We don't feature those in Touchline. If a skin or a boss looks too scary, it probably is, and that's fine: there's plenty to enjoy without it. As always, ask a grown-up before buying anything in the Item Shop.

The checklistBefore the season ends

  • Collect the new Honey Sprite (and any Halloween Sprites you're missing)
  • Finish the free Fortnitemares quests and unlock Jack-A-Nape
  • Try Nitemare Island: Kado's Curse in Fortnite Reload, which arrived on 8 October
  • Play a few Ranked matches: the Reload Ranked season reset with the Halloween update
  • Claim any battle pass rewards you've already earned before the season finale
  • Take a screenshot of your favourite spot on the map. It might not be there next season!
