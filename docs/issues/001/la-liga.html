La Ligap.24

Perfect Barça, furious Madrid

Barcelona have won all eight league games. Real Madrid won too, but Vinícius Júnior's afternoon went badly wrong, and El Clásico is only two weeks away.

Round-up · La Liga, Matchday 8

Barcelona 3–0 GetafeEight games, eight wins

Barcelona are making La Liga look easy. Against Getafe they were ahead inside two minutes when Anthony Gordon, who joined from Newcastle, scored his first goal for the club. Gabriel Jesus, once of Manchester City and Arsenal, made it 2–0 on the half-hour, and Jules Koundé added a third in the 73rd minute. And they did all of this without their leading scorer, Raphinha, who missed out with a minor thigh problem.

That's eight wins from eight league games, an immaculate start. Barça have scored 34 goals and let in just seven. If they keep this up, the rest of La Liga may as well start playing for second place.

Real Madrid 1–0 VillarrealVinícius sees red

Real Madrid won, but nobody was talking about the goal afterwards. Young winger Yan Diomande scored the only goal just after the hour, his first for the club. Then came the chaos. Vinícius Júnior thought he'd made it 2–0, and José Mourinho, now Madrid's coach and once Manchester United's, went sprinting down the touchline to celebrate. But the video referee had spotted something in the build-up: Vinícius had pulled the hair of Villarreal's Nathan Saliba. The goal was ruled out, and Vinícius was shown a straight red card.

He argued furiously as he left, a petulant exit that the referee mentioned in his match report. Later, a more contrite Vinícius posted an apology online: "Sorry to my team and my fans." Even Villarreal defender Juan Foyth said the red card was deserved, though he didn't think Vinícius had meant to hurt anyone.

Madrid hung on with ten men for the last quarter of an hour. Mourinho deserves credit for that: his half-time switches, including bringing on Marc Cucurella, helped close the game down. Mourinho has always loved a backs-to-the-wall win.

Countdown: El Clásico

Barcelona v Real Madrid · scheduled for Sunday 25 October. The biggest game in Spanish football is two weeks away, and the question already being asked in Madrid is whether Vinícius will be allowed to play. A ban for violent conduct could rule him out. Nothing has been decided yet. Before then, Barcelona travel to Real Betis on Saturday 17 October, and Betis are third, so it won't be easy.

Did you know?

El Clásico ("The Classic") has been played more than 250 times. Spain are also the reigning world champions, after beating Argentina 1–0 in extra time in the World Cup final in July, so plenty of players in this fixture now have winners' medals.
