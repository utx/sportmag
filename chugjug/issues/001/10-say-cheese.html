Ever pulled off an amazing play and wished you had a photo of it? You can. Fortnite's Replay Mode records your matches, so afterwards you can go back in, fly a camera anywhere you like, freeze time and take a screenshot that looks like a movie poster. It's how professional Fortnite artists make those amazing pictures. Here's how to do it.

Word Watch

Compositionnoun
How the things in a picture are arranged. Good composition makes a photo look balanced and exciting.
Perspectivenoun
The angle you see something from. A low perspective makes characters look heroic.
Silhouettenoun
The dark outline of something against a bright background.

The basicsHow Replay Mode works

  1. Play a match. Your recent matches are saved automatically on PC and consoles.
  2. Open your replays from the game's menus (look under your Career section).
  3. Pick a moment, then slow down or pause the playback.
  4. Switch to the drone camera and fly it wherever you want: low to the ground, behind a player, high above the action.
  5. Hide the on-screen display so there are no menus or health bars in your shot, then take your screenshot with your console's or PC's capture button.

Pro photographyFive tricks for poster-quality shots

Trick 1
Go low

Put the camera near the ground and look up. A low perspective makes any character look like a hero.

Trick 2
Rule of thirds

Imagine a noughts-and-crosses grid on the screen. Put your character on one of the lines, not dead centre. Instantly better composition.

Trick 3
Catch the action

Pause at the exact moment of a jump, an explosion or a glider deploy. Movement makes a picture exciting.

Trick 4
Use the light

Sunset and Fortnitemares fog make amazing backgrounds. Try a silhouette against the sky.

Trick 5
Tell a story

The best shots show something happening: a close escape, a Victory Royale, a squad celebrating.
