Before you spend a single V-Buck

Always ask a grown-up first. Sleep on it: if you still want it tomorrow, that's a good sign. And remember the shop rotates. Most items come back eventually, so you rarely have to buy something "before it's gone".

Skip it: the Halloween horror section

A big chunk of this week's shop is horror-movie guest characters. They're well made, but they're properly scary and built for older players. Our advice: scroll past and spend nothing. Your V-Bucks will still be there next week, and so will plenty of brilliant original outfits.

The Chug Jug rules of shopping

  1. Never make an impulse buy. Wait a day.
  2. Rarity isn't value. A plain Rare can cost more than a brilliant Epic.
  3. Buy what you like, not what's trending.
  4. Free stuff first: quests and the battle pass give you outfits without spending (see page 24).
