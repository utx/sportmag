Battle Royale gets the headlines, but Fortnite is really lots of games in one. Here's what's happening everywhere else.

Word Watch

Reload
Nitemare Island

Since 8 October, Reload has a spooky refreshed map called Nitemare Island: Kado's Curse, with Elite Kado Thorne calling the shots. Look out for Siphon loot, which heals you whenever you deal damage. Two more maps, Stranger Things and Squid Grounds, have joined the rotation too.

Horde Rush
The waves are back

Squad up against relentless waves of Cube Monsters, pick perks, buy and upgrade weapons, and take on Cursed Shrines if you're feeling brave. Beat the final boss to keep going in Endless Mode.

Blitz Royale
Fast and frantic

Short, speedy matches, now with the Bonerattler SMG and Freddy's Nightmare in the loot. Great when you only have ten minutes before dinner.

LEGO Fortnite
Balloon builders

You can now build Balloons in Quick Build mode even when Free Crafting is switched off. Handy. (And yes, the Batmobile no longer gets stuck.)

Fortnite OG
Back to 2018

Epic has been carefully restoring the original Chapter 1 map: Grandma's House is back to its old look, the geysers have returned to Loot Lake and Salty Springs, and the snow has been tidied up at Viking Village.

Festival
Main Stage fix

Jam Tracks in the Featured Rotation can be picked on the Main Stage again. Time to practise that expert-level guitar solo.

Goodbye, Rocket Racing

Rocket Racing, Fortnite's high-speed car game, was switched off on 1 October 2026. Epic announced back in March that it would be discontinued because not enough people were playing. If you bought car customisations, don't worry: you keep them, and you can still use them in other modes. Pour one out. (A Chug Jug, obviously.)
