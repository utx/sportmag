The battle pass is the best value in Fortnite: play, earn XP, and unlock outfits along the way. This season's pass is called Override. It costs 800 V-Bucks, or it's included if you have Fortnite Crew. The headline outfit is a famous blue hedgehog from another company's games, who unlocks the moment you buy the pass. Behind him is a strong line-up of Epic's own original characters.

Clever trickThe gifting bonus

If a friend doesn't have the pass yet, buying it for them as a gift unlocks the Peril Peak Mali style and matching accessories for both of you. It's a rare case where being generous earns you a reward too. (Gifting costs real V-Bucks, so it's definitely a grown-up conversation first.)

Free stuffOutfits that cost nothing

  • Jack-a-Nape: complete the Fortnitemares quests this month.
  • Gold Punk: given free with any Fortnite gift card redeemed during Fortnitemares.
  • Battle pass V-Bucks: work through the pass and it pays back some V-Bucks as rewards along the way.

Is the pass worth it?

If you play a few times a week, yes: it's the best value purchase in the game, and the season runs until 1 November, so there's still time to level up. If you only play now and then, you might not reach the good stuff. Be honest with yourself before you spend.
