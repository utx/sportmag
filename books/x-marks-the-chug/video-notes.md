# Video notes (from subtitles and contact-sheet OCR)

Perfect Score's videos have no captions (music + on-screen text). The publisher made contact sheets
(`sheets/<id>_NNN.jpg` in Drive: one frame every 2 s, 3x3 tiles, so sheet N covers
(N-1)*18 s to N*18-1 s, tile i (0-8, left-to-right, top-to-bottom) ≈ (N-1)*18 + 2i s).
OCR text below is what Drive read off each sheet. Rough times, to be pinned down by looking at the frames.

**Crop note:** a "USE CODE SCORE" creator-code banner is on nearly every frame. Crop it out (spending plug).
