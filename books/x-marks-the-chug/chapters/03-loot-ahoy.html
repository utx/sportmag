Chapter 3
Loot ahoy!

New island, new treasure chest of loot. This season you can eat magic fruit, swing three swords at once, snack on a tangerine, and fire a slingshot that hits as hard as a sniper rifle. Here's every new item, rated out of five Chug Jugs.

Our ratings are our own opinion, but we didn't make them up from nowhere. We watched two creators who tested everything on day one, TyluhFryz, who ranked every new item from worst to best, and Evolve Jake, who reviewed the whole loot pool, and we noted where they agreed and where they didn't.

Devil Fruits: the big new thing

In One Piece, a Devil Fruit gives whoever eats it an amazing power, but they can never swim again! In Fortnite, there are two of them, and they're both Mythic, the rarest kind of loot:

The Gum-Gum Fruit (purple, with swirls) turns you into Luffy. Your body becomes rubber, so you can punch from far away and launch yourself through the air.
The Flame-Flame Fruit (orange, with flames) turns you into Ace, Luffy's big brother. You get fire powers instead.

Here's how they work:

Devil Fruit rules
1
They appear during the match, and they're marked on your map. Race for them!
2
Eat one and you transform into Luffy or Ace.
3
It's undroppable: it stays with you for the rest of the match.
4
One at a time. Try a second and the game says: "Probably shouldn't eat another one right now..."
✕L

And when anyone in the match eats one, a banner pops up on everybody's screen: "Devil Fruit has been consumed." So now everyone knows there's a rubber pirate on the loose.

One Piece powers

TyluhFryz ranked it number one, the best new item in the game, and the only item he'd say you must always pick up. As Luffy, you can punch people from a long way away with your stretchy arms (TyluhFryz guessed 20 to 30 metres), and the punches even smash through walls. Add the Gum-Gum Rocket (Chapter 2) for travel, and it's an attack item and a travel item in one.

Chug Jug tip: TyluhFryz thinks something this strong will probably be made weaker in an update. Enjoy it while it lasts!

Gum-Gum punch! Luffy stretches across the beach near The Chest. (Frame: EveryDay FN)

Turns you into Ace, with fire powers: fiery shots that hit instantly at any distance, plus a big charged fire attack that's very strong up close. Both creators think it's the weaker of the two fruits. TyluhFryz found that each shot does only a little damage, and that it's very loud, so everyone knows where you are. Still exciting, and still Mythic!

Zoro is the swordsman in Luffy's crew, and he fights with three swords, one in each hand and one in his mouth! In Fortnite you get slashes up close and a series of quick dashes forward (TyluhFryz counted four or five before you need a rest). Secret trick: a special charged attack can block bullets. Fun, but TyluhFryz thinks most players will still choose an Overdrive Grenade for getting around.

Usopp is the crew's sharpshooter, and his slingshot is like a pocket sniper rifle. It's silent, so people won't know where you are, and it hits hard. Both creators were impressed. The catch? It's tricky to aim, especially at players who are moving. TyluhFryz called it an item for really good players. Practise, practise, practise.

Nami, the crew's navigator, grows tangerine trees in One Piece. Eat one of hers and you get 25 shield plus a little health, almost instantly, and it gives you a speed boost in the water too. Evolve Jake thinks it's the best healing item of the season because it's so quick to use in the middle of a fight.

Back from the vault

Lots of old favourites have returned for Treasure Hunters: the Sentinel Pump Shotgun, the Auto Shotgun, the Hammer Assault Rifle, the Machine SMG and Tactical SMG, the Sidearm Pistol, the Flint-Knock Pistol, the Zipline Deployer, the Overdrive Grenade, Slurp Juice and some fish snacks. How we checked: experts disagree

Even people who play Fortnite for a living don't always agree. Look at these:

The Bundlebuss: "maybe the best rifle" (TyluhFryz) vs "on the weaker side" (Evolve Jake).

Nami's Tangerine: both say you get 25 shield, but one says a stack holds 5 tangerines and the other says 6. And how much extra health? 5, or 10?

Usopp's Slingshot: one creator guessed about 80 damage, the other "almost 100", and a website said 77.

Why? On day one, everyone is guessing from a few hours of play, and numbers can change with every update. So we didn't print exact numbers we couldn't confirm. When experts disagree, the smartest thing to do is try it yourself, and keep an open mind.

Real treasure: why sailors needed tangerines

Nami's tangerines heal you in Fortnite. Amazingly, in real life, fruit like this once saved thousands of sailors' lives.

Hundreds of years ago, on long voyages, sailors ate mostly dried biscuits and salted meat, with no fresh fruit or vegetables for months. Many of them caught a terrible illness called scurvy. Their gums bled, their teeth wobbled, cuts wouldn't heal, and they felt exhausted. Historians think that, for hundreds of years, more sailors died of scurvy than in battles.

In 1747, a Scottish ship's doctor called James Lind tried an experiment. He took twelve sailors with scurvy and gave pairs of them different things to eat or drink. The two who got oranges and lemons got better much faster than everyone else. It was one of the first fair tests in the history of medicine.

Today we know why: citrus fruit is full of vitamin C, and scurvy is caused by not getting enough of it. A couple of oranges, a kiwi fruit or a handful of strawberries gives you plenty. So next time you have a mandarin in your lunchbox, you're eating like a well-prepared pirate.

Do the maths

Each Nami's Tangerine gives 25 shield. Your shield can go up to 100.

1. You have no shield at all. How many tangerines do you need to reach 100?

2. You have 40 shield. How many tangerines to reach (at least) 100? Will any be wasted?

Answers
1: 100 ÷ 25 = 4 tangerines
2: You need 60 more. 2 tangerines = 50 (not enough), so 3 tangerines = 75, and 15 shield is wasted.
