Chapter 5
Sprite ho!

Sprites are the little creatures that ride around on your back and give you a special power. They've been in Fortnite all of Chapter 7, and Treasure Hunters brings a whole new crew, dressed for the high seas. There's a Sprite that's half banana and half skeleton, a Sprite that's a treasure chest with a cat's face, and a Mythic skull that grins with far too many teeth.

This chapter introduces every new Sprite, explains the special versions (Fortune, Cursed and, coming soon, Gold), and shows you where they hide. Our main guide is another video by Perfect Score, who set out to find every new Sprite and recorded the whole hunt. The powers below are the game's own descriptions, checked against what appeared on screen in the video and against two written guides.

Sprites in 30 seconds

Equip one and it rides on your back, giving you its power.

Level it up by playing with it, from Level 1 to Level 5. Level 5 is called Mastered, and most powers get stronger at every level.

Extract it to keep it. A Sprite you find in a match only goes into your collection once you've extracted it. If you're eliminated first, it's gone.

Mastery Monday: every Monday this season, Sprites earn double Sprite XP and double Sprite Dust.

Meet the crew

Here they are, from the most common to the rarest. Each one has a Chug Jug rating out of five. That part is our opinion, based on how useful we think the power is for a treasure hunter, so feel free to argue with it.

Each Sprite's card in the game also shows a number of Sprite Dust, and it goes up with rarity: Rare cards showed 500, Epic 700, Legendary 900 and Mythic 1,200.

Rare

"Reveals Sprites nearby and grants more items when opening Buried Chests." Its detection range grows every time it levels up. A little navigator in a captain's hat with a compass on its tummy, and the perfect partner for the Wanted Poster hunt in Chapter 6.

A parrot in a pirate hat. It makes you reload faster, with a chance that reloading gives you your ammo back for free. Both get better with every level. Quietly brilliant.

Half banana, half skeleton: a spooky pirate take on Peely, Fortnite's famous banana. It marks eliminations on your map, over a wider area as it levels up. That shows you where fights have just happened, which is handy for avoiding them, or for sneaking over to pick up the loot afterwards.

A fish peeping out of a barrel. You use less stamina when you sprint through water, and at max level it gives you the Slap effect. Good on a sea-soaked island, but not exciting.

Epic

A treasure chest with a cat's face and an eyepatch. It can give you extra loot from chests, and it cancels fall damage, so you can jump off cliffs and ships without losing health. Two great powers in one. Our favourite.

A walking treasure map, with a red X for one eye. "Reveals Treasure Maps and Wanted Posters. Spawns a Treasure Map at Max Level!" Made for this book, really.

A grumpy-looking fish wearing an orange swimming ring. It slowly heals you while you're in water, and heals more at higher levels. Crossbones Isle is surrounded by sea, so that's a lot of free healing.

A hooded squid with glowing pink eyes. Jump into a fishing hole and it fishes it for you, with some bonus loot. You get more loot as it levels up. Fun, but you'll need to find fishing holes.

Legendary

A blue Sprite in a white cap and shades, based on the rapper A$AP Rocky. "Creates a small shockwave on landing, knocking back enemies." The knockback gets stronger with each level. It can drop when you defeat the A$AP Rocky boss at The $.$. Rocky (Chapter 7), which is exactly where Perfect Score found his.

In One Piece, Transponder Snails are snails that work as telephones. This one gives you extra materials whenever you pick some up (wood, stone and metal), and any teammates who also have a Transponder Snail equipped get some extra too. Better in a squad than on your own.

Mythic

A sleepy purple Sprite covered in stars, like a pillowcase. It gives you a random item every time it levels up, and Mythic loot at max level. A treasure chest that follows you around.

A grinning pirate skull. "Opening Chest grants increased pickaxe speed and movement speed." Open a chest, then dig and run faster. Perfect Score called it the rarest of the new Sprites; he found his inside a glowing Sprite Chest upstairs in a wooden house at Cursed Cavern.

Coming soon: Lil' Uzi Vert Jr.

A Legendary Sprite based on the rapper Lil Uzi Vert, who is the Captain on Saturday 7 November (Chapter 7). According to the game files, restoring your shields creates a Chug Splash effect. It wasn't available at launch, so we haven't rated it yet. We'll update this page when it arrives.

Where to find them

Perfect Score's on-screen note at the start of his video says it plainly: "Finding the Sprites is random." No Sprite has one fixed home. But you can give yourself lots of chances:

1. Sprite Chests. Follow a Wanted Poster's Treasure Map and dig one up (Chapter 6), or find one sitting out in the open. A Sprite Chest always has a Sprite inside.

2. Ordinary chests. Sometimes a normal chest has a Sprite in it too, though not often.

3. Bosses. The A$AP Rocky boss can drop Lil' Flacko.

4. Other players. When a player with a Sprite equipped is eliminated, their Sprite drops on the ground.

Keep what you catch

Remember: a Sprite isn't yours until you extract it. Head to an Extraction Site before somebody takes it from you. Perfect Score also noticed something surprising at the end of his video: after he won the match, he quickly picked up the Sprites his opponents had dropped, and the game extracted those as well. He said "apparently" when he told viewers, and we haven't been able to check it, so try it yourself and see.

Victory Royale, and a beach full of Sprites to grab. (Frame: Perfect Score)

The Mastery track

In the lobby, the Treasure tab has a Sprite Mastery track, with a new page each week. Master 40 Sprites and you unlock the Navigator's Nosedive, an umbrella glider. Careful, though: mastering 40 Sprites means levelling each one all the way up to Level 5. Simply finding 40 isn't enough. Mastery Mondays, with double Sprite XP, are the best days to level up.

Real world: what do "better odds" mean?

Cursed Sprites promise a better chance of finding rare variants. What does that actually mean?

Imagine a bag with 10 marbles: 6 blue, 3 purple and 1 gold. Close your eyes and pick one. There are 10 marbles you could pick, and only 1 of them is gold, so your chance of gold is 1 in 10. Mathematicians write that as a fraction, 1/10, or as a percentage, 10%.

Now add 1 more gold marble. There are 11 marbles and 2 are gold, so your chance is now 2 in 11, about 18%. That's what "better odds" means: more of the chances are the ones you want.

But here's the important part: better odds are never a promise. With 2 golds in 11, you'll still pick a blue or a purple most of the time. Real scientists, weather forecasters and sports analysts think like this every day. When the forecast says "70% chance of rain", it doesn't mean it will definitely rain. It means it's more likely than not, so take an umbrella. A Navigator's Nosedive would do.

How we checked

How many new Sprites are there? It depends who you ask! Epic's announcement listed 11. Perfect Score's video found a 12th, the Transponder Snail, and the game files have a 13th, Lil' Uzi Vert Jr., which arrives later. Guides counting variants say 33 or 35. Nobody is lying: they're counting different things. (See the maths box below.)

Is Cursed out yet? One guide says Cursed versions are in the game; another says they're still "coming soon". We say what we can confirm: not at launch.

When does Gold arrive? Most reports say Thursday 12 November, one says the 14th. Check this page again after the update.

Do the maths

One guide counts the Sprites like this. 10 of the new Sprites come in three versions (Base, Fortune and Cursed). Lil' Uzi Vert Jr. also comes in three. Transponder Snail and Sweet Dreams come in just one version each.

1. How many entries is that altogether?

2. The Mastery track needs 40 mastered Sprites. If you master 3 Sprites a week, how many weeks will it take? (Round up!)

Answers
1: 10 × 3 = 30, then 30 + 3 + 1 + 1 = 35 entries
2: 40 ÷ 3 = 13 and a bit, so 14 weeks. That's longer than the whole mini-season!
