What's a mini-season?

A normal Fortnite season lasts about three months. A mini-season is a short, packed one, more like a summer holiday than a whole school term. Treasure Hunters started on Sunday 1 November 2026, and it's only a few weeks long, which is why everything happens so fast: there's something new every single week.

Word Watch

Voyagenoun
A long journey, especially by sea. Word origin: old French voiage, from the Latin viaticum, "supplies for a journey" (via means "road").
Buccaneernoun
A pirate, especially one in the Caribbean in the 1600s. Word origin: the French boucanier, someone who cooked meat on a boucan, a wooden grill. The first buccaneers were hunters who smoked meat before they took up piracy. Barbecue pirates!
Crewnoun
The people who work together on a ship (or a team). Word origin: old French creue, "a growth", meaning extra soldiers added to an army.

Real treasure: when is Saturday?

Fortnite's big moments usually happen at the same moment all over the world. Epic often gives the time for New York, and Sydney is a very long way from New York.

Right now, Sydney is 16 hours ahead of New York. So if an event started at 9 am on a Saturday in New York, it would be 1 am on Sunday in Sydney! For Australian players, a "Saturday" event can arrive on Sunday.

And here's a twist: on the very day Treasure Hunters began, 1 November, the clocks in New York went back an hour, because their summer time ended. Sydney's summer time had started a month earlier. The day before, the gap was 15 hours; from launch day on, it was 16. Two cities, two hemispheres, two different summers.

Try it: If something starts at 8 pm on Friday in New York, what day and time is it in Sydney? (Answer at the bottom of the page.)
