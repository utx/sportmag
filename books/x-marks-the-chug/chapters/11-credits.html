About this book

X Marks the Chug is an unofficial fan book made by The Chug Jug, a fan magazine about Fortnite. It's a guide to the Treasure Hunters mini-season, put together from YouTube creators' videos, Epic's own announcements and news write-ups, with the real-world history, science, maths and words added for this book. It isn't written, approved or endorsed by Epic Games, or by any of the creators whose videos it draws on.

It's free, and it will never ask you to spend money. If you ever want to buy anything in Fortnite, ask a grown-up first.

Version

This book describes Fortnite Battle Royale in Chapter 7: Treasure Hunters (version 43.00), as it was at the start of November 2026. Fortnite changes all the time, and an update is due on 12 November, so some details may have changed by the time you read this.
