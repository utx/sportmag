Confirmed: what we know for sure

These facts come from Epic itself: from the patch notes, the game's own words, or what we've seen on screen.

✅ Something big happens on Saturday 5 December. Epic's patch notes said: "keep Saturday, December 5, open. Time is on your side… for now." It's the end of Chapter 7.

✅ There are six Map Fragments, one a week (Chapter 6). The Treasures tab counts them: 1/6, 2/6...

✅ All six unlock a map with a riddle. The X Marks the Zero Point Sliver loading screen is described as "Map up front, ancient pirate riddle on the back."

✅ Captain X is "the greatest treasure hunter in any reality." That's the game's own description of the outfit.

✅ More captains are coming on 7, 14 and 21 November (Chapter 7).

The clues

Clue 1: The Chest talks. Before the season started, the creator ItsRadr visited the giant chest on the old island and found that a mysterious voice spoke from it, saying something different each day. One day it talked about a power beyond imagination, and "the power to save reality."

Clue 2: The trailer. Epic's season trailer shows every crew racing towards the giant chest. The creator PUNK'D SIDE pointed out that the X on the treasure map marks the exact spot of the chest, and that the voice at the end of the trailer, talking about "my treasure chest", promises: "Lucky for you, I know how to share." Hmm. Can a pirate be trusted?

Clue 3: The word "reality". It's in Captain X's description, and in the chest's voice lines. In Fortnite's story, the whole island is a kind of reality, and the "Zero Point" (from the loading screen's name) has been at the centre of Fortnite's biggest mysteries for years. Are Captain X and the Zero Point connected? We think it's a very good clue.

Rumours: maybe, maybe not

These are things people are saying, but Epic hasn't confirmed. Treat them like a weather forecast: interesting, but not a promise.

❓ Chapter 8 starts on 5 December. Lots of people expect it, and one creator pointed out that another Fortnite pass has been moved to that exact date. Very likely, but not official.

❓ The season might end before 5 December. One guide noted that mini-seasons usually last 28 to 30 days, which would mean the end of November. Epic's teaser points to 5 December, though.

❓ Captain X is a famous character in disguise. Some fans have guessed it's Midas, a golden-touch villain from earlier Fortnite seasons, because the trailer shows a gold car. It's only a fan theory!

❓ Old locations are coming back. PUNK'D SIDE spotted that parts of the new island look just like places from Chapter 6, and thinks the story will explain why.

How we checked: spotting clickbait

While researching this book, we watched lots of videos. Some of their titles promised exciting things that the video didn't really deliver. One promised viewers could "get free V-Bucks"; when we listened carefully, it was really a guess that Epic might give some players a refund one day. Another title promised "free rewards", but the video was mostly about a trailer.

That's called clickbait: a title designed to make you click, not to tell you what's inside. Here's how to spot it:

1. Words like FREE, SECRET or INSANE in capital letters.
2. Promises of V-Bucks, free skins or giveaways. (Real free V-Bucks don't exist outside the game. Never type your login into a website that promises them.)
3. The answer arrives in the last few seconds, after lots of "like and subscribe".
And the best test of all: does another source say the same thing? That's what we did for every fact in this book.

Word Watch

To guess about something without having all the facts. Word origin: Latin specula, "a watchtower". Speculating is like peering from a crow's nest, trying to see what's coming.

To show that something is definitely true. Word origin: Latin confirmare, "to make firm".

Real treasure: the maths of a calendar

Why does November have 30 days, but December has 31? Blame the ancient Romans! Our calendar comes from one designed for Julius Caesar over 2,000 years ago (yes, the same Caesar from the cipher in Chapter 6). The word "calendar" comes from the Latin kalendae, the first day of each Roman month, which was when people had to pay their debts.

A handy rhyme helps you remember the months: "Thirty days has September, April, June and November. All the rest have thirty-one, except February alone..."

Or try the knuckle trick: make two fists side by side and count the months across your knuckles and the dips between them. January is the first knuckle, February the dip, March the next knuckle, and so on. Every month on a knuckle has 31 days!

Do the maths

1. Treasure Hunters started on Sunday 1 November. November has 30 days. How many days is it from 1 November to 5 December?

2. How many whole weeks is that, and how many days left over?

3. 5 December is a Saturday. What day of the week will 25 December (Christmas Day) be?

Answers
1: 29 more days in November + 5 = 34 days
2: 34 = 4 weeks (28 days) and 6 days
3: 20 days later = 2 weeks and 6 days, so it's a Friday
