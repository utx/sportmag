Crossbones Isle." Alternatives: *Shiver Me Shields*, *Yo-Ho-Chug-Jug*.

## Fun + educational features

- Word Watch in every chapter (4–6 words) with a **Word Origin** line (buccaneer ← French *boucan*,
  plunder, privateer, navigator, doubloon, cenote, rampart, galleon, mutiny, bounty).
- **Real Treasure** box (one real-world fact) and **Do the Maths** box (answer upside down) per chapter.
- **How we checked** box: sources disagreeing, auto-caption bloopers, spotting clickbait titles.
- Treasure hunt through the book: a hidden X with a letter in each chapter spells a secret word
  (answer in the credits). Captain's Log checklist (Sprites, locations, fragments). Spot-the-difference
  from two frames. Pirate cipher in ch. 6.
- Make & do: design-your-own Sprite card, write your Captain's Log, draw your own island on a grid.
- Rules: game first, max one real-world box per chapter, history checked against proper sources
  (Royal Museums Greenwich, Britannica), real pirates were criminals (said lightly), no violence beyond
  cartoon eliminations, bright-9-year-old reading level.

How to use them:
1. Read the `.srt` subtitles for tips, written in our own words, credited per creator; short credited quotes only.
   Check every name against fortnite-api (auto-captions mishear names).
2. Take frames at timestamps chosen from the subtitles. Crop out facecams, logos and subscribe buttons.
   Credit the creator in photos.json + credits ("not endorsed by the creator"). Use Epic API art for clean

4. Leave out spending pressure, "players are disappointed" complaining, bad language.
