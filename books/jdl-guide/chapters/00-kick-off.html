Your first touch of the JDL
How to use this book

Every great footballer has a first touch. Not just the first time the ball hits their boot, but the first day at a new club, the first training session with new team-mates, and the first match in a new shirt. If you're holding this book, you've just reached one of those moments. You've made it into the Junior Development League, the JDL.

That's a big deal. You were picked because a coach saw something in you. This book is about what happens next.

What's inside

The first half is about the league: where the JDL came from, why nobody keeps score, exactly how the Under 10 rules work, what your season looks like, and a guide to nineteen JDL clubs, ranked. The second half is about you: ball skills, football brain, fitness, food, mindset, and getting on with coaches and team-mates. Finally, you'll meet six Australian stars who were once new players too.

Word Watch

The process of growing and getting better over time. The "D" in JDL.

What you could become in the future if you keep working at it.

How to read it

You don't have to read it front to back. Read the rules before your first game and the mindset chapter before a big match. Look out for these boxes:

  • Word Watch boxes explain useful words. They're all collected in the Word Bank at the back.
  • Try this boxes are things to practise, on your own or with a friend.
  • Coach's corner boxes are little pieces of advice to remember.

The small print (it's important)

This book is an independent guide. It isn't made or approved by Football NSW or any club. The rules, dates and club details come from Football NSW's own published documents for the 2026 season and were checked on 13 October 2026. Rules can change from season to season, and your club's coach always has the final word on how your team trains and plays. If something in this book doesn't match what your club tells you, listen to your club.

The club rankings in Chapter 4 are our opinion. In the JDL, match results aren't published, so nobody can rank clubs by wins and losses. We explain exactly how we decided.

A note for grown-ups

This book is written for Under 10 players and works for ages 8 to 12. The health chapters follow Australian government guidelines and are about energy and enjoyment, never weight or appearance. Nothing in this book suggests paying for extra training: Football NSW's own guidance is that a good JDL program already gives most players enough.
