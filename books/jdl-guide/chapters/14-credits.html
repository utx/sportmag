About this book

The Complete Guide to JDL Football in NSW is an independent guide for new players. It is not made, approved or endorsed by Football NSW, Football Australia, or any club named in it. No club logos are used. The club rankings in Chapter 4 are the authors' opinion, explained in that chapter.

Version

Rules, dates, badges and club details describe the 2026 season as published by Football NSW, and were checked on 13 October 2026. Football NSW updates its rules and dates every season, so check with your club for the latest. Where Football NSW's own documents gave slightly different numbers (for example, the size of the Under 10 penalty area), we used the figure in its 2026 Club Information Document.

Where the facts came from

The JDL and its rules: Football NSW, 2026 JDL Game Leader Handbook; 2026 Junior Development Leagues Club Information Document (Mixed and Girls); Junior Development Cup April 2026 Competition Rules and Regulations; the Junior Development Leagues page on footballnsw.com.au; Football NSW media releases on the Skill Acquisition Program (2011 and 2012).

Club badges and leagues: Football NSW, 2026 Declaration of Leagues (29 September 2025), which includes the 2026 Mixed and Girls JDL ratings; Football NSW, "Girls Junior Development League continues improvement through Club Standards and Benchmarking Framework" (1 October 2026), for the newest Girls JDL ratings; Football NSW's JDL club directory.

The Junior Development Cup: Football NSW, "Young Talent Shines at Football NSW's Junior Development Cup" (21 April 2026), including the quotes from Paul Jackson and Enzo Palumbo.

Clubs: club websites, especially Sydney Uni SFC (susfc.com.au), including its Training DNA, Performance Support, About Us and JDL pages, quoted in Chapter 4, Eastern Suburbs FA (esfa.com.au), Southern Phoenix FC and Macarthur Football Association; Wikipedia articles on the clubs, cross-checked against Football NSW's league lists.

Players: Wikipedia biographies of Johnny Warren, Clare Wheeler, Clare Hunt, Amy Sayer, Miloš Degenek and Michael Thwaite; Sydney Uni SFC's list of former players.

Health: Australian Government Department of Health, Australian 24-Hour Movement Guidelines for Children (5–12 years) and Young People (13–17 years); NSW Health, Eating for Junior Sport; ACT Government, "Half time snacks for active kids" (based on Sports Dietitians Australia advice); a 2022 systematic review and meta-analysis of the FIFA 11+ Kids warm-up program.

Mindset: the growth mindset research of Professor Carol Dweck of Stanford University.

Play it safe

  • Always wear shin guards, and tell an adult about any injury, especially a knock to the head.
  • Drink water, wear sunscreen, and rest when you're tired.
  • Be kind online. Never post photos of other players without permission.
