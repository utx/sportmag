Chapter 4
Extract! Extract! Extract!

Less than a minute into my first match, I found two Trick or Treat Sprites in a row. And then I started shouting, "No, no! Extract, extract, extract, extract!" Why the panic? Because finding a Sprite is only half the job. Until you extract it, it isn't really yours.

Why extracting matters

When you pick up a Sprite, it rides around with you for the rest of the match. But if somebody eliminates you before you've extracted it, it's gone. Lost forever. All that pumpkin smashing for nothing.

Extracting a Sprite sends it safely back to your collection, so it stays yours whatever happens next. It's like putting your pocket money in the bank instead of carrying it around in your pocket, where it might fall out.

The best words in the game: "Added to collection". This one also showed a big XP bonus for extracting.

Three ways to extract

  1. An Extraction Site. These are special places on the map. Go there with your Sprites and extract them. Easy, but they can be far away, and other players know where they are too.
  2. A Portable Extractor. An item you carry and use anywhere. This is the one I rely on most. It's in my item wheel in the screenshot below, next to some other Sprite-hunting gear.
  3. Win the match. Get a Victory Royale and everything you're carrying is saved automatically. The most satisfying way, and also the hardest.

My item wheel. Look for the Portable Extractor (top right), plus a Lucky Locator (left), a stack of thirteen Cheat Code Locators and an Extraction Accelerator. More on the locators in Chapter 5.

The one-a-day problem

You can buy a Portable Extractor with Sprite Dust, the stuff you earn by playing. But in the video, I was grumbling about a restriction: you can only buy one a day. "If I've got the dust for it, let me buy as many as I want!" So use yours wisely. Save it for a precious Sprite, not one you've already got.

Learn from my mistakes

I made three mistakes in the video, so you don't have to.

  • Mistake 1: thinking it worked when it didn't. At one point I celebrated, then realised: "Oh, it didn't extract." Extracting takes a few seconds. Watch the screen and wait for "Added to collection" before you run off.
  • Mistake 2: running out of extractors. Later on I found a brand-new Sprite and shouted, "No, no, no, I need an extractor!" Always know how you're going to save your Sprite before you go hunting.
  • Mistake 3: extracting in the open. While you're extracting, you're busy, which makes you vulnerable. Hide behind a rock or inside a building first. (In one game, the guy chasing me had no idea what was happening, and I got away with it. Don't count on that.)

Pro Tip: Already got that Sprite? Don't waste your Portable Extractor on it. In the video I kept saying, "Not the one I needed, but it did work. I've got that one already." Only extract the new ones.
