Chapter 2
Pumpkin patrol

If you remember one thing from this whole book, make it this: the pumpkins are the way. I said it in the video and I'll say it again here. The pumpkins are the way to get the new Trick or Treat Sprites.

When I say "pumpkins", I mean Pumpkin Chests. They aren't the orange pumpkins you see sitting around in fields. They're big, dark, scary-looking jack-o'-lanterns with a jagged grin, and they glow with a luminous purple flame. When you get close, a button prompt appears that says SMASH.

That's what you're hunting for. A Pumpkin Chest, glowing and grinning, with the SMASH prompt showing.

What's inside?

Smash a Pumpkin Chest and it bursts open in a shower of sparks. Inside, every single time, you get a weapon and a random Sprite. Not "maybe a Sprite". A Sprite. That's what makes pumpkins so much better than ordinary chests for Sprite hunting. The only question is which Sprite, and whether it's a Trick or Treat one.

Boom. The pumpkin bursts, and the loot flies out.

The area around Nightmare Neighborhood and Freaky Fields is especially dense with them. The trouble is that everybody else knows that too, so those spots are contested. You'll have company.

The pattern I noticed

About sixteen minutes into the video, I said something that turned out to be really important: "I feel like the pumpkins are close by each other, man. When you find one, you find a lot."

That's a pattern, and the map backs it up. Pumpkin Chests come in clusters, so as soon as you've smashed one, stop and look around before you run off. There's very often a second one within sight, and sometimes a third.

Pro Tip: Pumpkin Chests can only be smashed once per match. If you arrive and there's no pumpkin where you expected one, somebody probably got there first. In one game I was literally saying, "Let's just get lucky that nobody looted." Get there early, or go somewhere quieter.

Why do some matches have more?

In one game I found pumpkins everywhere. In the next few, hardly any. Part of that is other players smashing them first, and part of it is simply where the Battle Bus flew and where the storm went. Don't give up after one quiet match. Like I said in the video: "They were literally everywhere." Sometimes.
