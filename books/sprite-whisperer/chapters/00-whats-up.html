Chat, I found the meta

What's up, everybody, and welcome back! Today we are talking about the brand-new Trick or Treat Sprites, and let me tell you something: I went crazy. I came up with a strategy, tried it out, and I was getting a new Sprite almost every single time. Not once. Not twice. Three times in a row, chat. Three!

Now, I know what you're thinking. "Every YouTuber says their strategy is amazing." Fair. You should be sceptical. That's why this book doesn't just tell you what I did. It shows you, with screenshots straight from my matches, and then it does something most guides never do: it asks whether the strategy actually works, or whether I just got lucky. (Spoiler: the answer is more interesting than you'd think.)

Word Watch

Scepticaladjective
Not believing something straight away; wanting to see proof first. A sceptical player asks, "Does that trick really work?"
Metanoun
Short for "the most effective tactics available". In gaming, the meta is whatever the best players are doing right now.
Strategynoun
A plan for reaching a goal. Not one lucky move, but a whole way of doing things.
Variantnoun
A slightly different version of something. A Trick or Treat Sprite is a spooky variant of a normal Sprite.

The small print (it's important)

This book covers Chapter 7, Season 4: Override, with the Fortnitemares update (version 42.30) on the Island. Fortnite changes all the time, and an update arrived on 15 October 2026, so a few details may have changed by the time you read this. If the game doesn't match the book, the game has moved on, not your eyes.

This is an unofficial fan book. It isn't made by Epic Games or by the creator whose video it's based on. It's written in the style of that video, as a guide for one keen reader.

A note for grown-ups

Fortnite carries an age rating on every platform, so check the one on yours. Nothing in this book involves spending money: Pumpkin Chests are free to open, and Sprite Dust is earned by playing. The Fortnitemares event includes some guest characters from other companies; we mention them by name, but don't show their official art. (One of them, a pink guest Sprite, rides on the player's back in many of the gameplay screenshots.)
