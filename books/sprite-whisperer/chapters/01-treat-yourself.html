Chapter 1
Treat yourself

Okay, first things first. What even is a Trick or Treat Sprite, and why was I yelling about them for twenty minutes straight?

You already know Sprites: the little creatures that ride on your back and give you a power. Vampire heals you when you deal damage. Spooky Dash lets you dash through the air. X-Ray marks enemies near you. For Fortnitemares, Epic dressed them up for Halloween. Every Trick or Treat Sprite is a spooky variant of a normal Sprite, with a pumpkin-orange costume and a glowing grin.

My very first one of the morning: a Trick or Treat Vampire Sprite, fresh out of a pumpkin. Read the card: "Chance to find candy when opening containers."

What's the difference?

Here's the thing a lot of people don't realise. A Trick or Treat Sprite keeps its normal power, exactly the same as the regular version. Then it adds one extra perk on top, and every Trick or Treat Sprite shares it:

The Trick or Treat bonus

Chance to find candy when opening containers. Open a chest, an ammo box or anything else you can loot, and sometimes candy pops out as well. Candy heals you, so a Trick or Treat Sprite is like carrying a tiny, very cute snack machine.

So is a Trick or Treat Vampire better than a normal Vampire? A little, yes, thanks to the candy. But let's be honest, chat: the real reason everybody wants them is that they look awesome and they're novel. There are 24 of them to collect, and collectors gonna collect.

The Trick or Treat Spooky Dash. It's literally a jack-o'-lantern, and it's one of the most wanted Sprites on the Island.

Three things to know before you hunt

  1. They're random. When a Sprite drops, the game picks which one it is. You can't choose. That's why the hunt is half skill and half luck, and why Chapter 3 is going to get a bit scientific.
  2. They're fragile until you save them. If you get eliminated while carrying a Sprite you haven't extracted, it's gone. Chapter 4 is all about not letting that happen. (It happened to me. I'm still not over it.)
  3. Once you extract one, it's yours. An extracted Sprite goes into your collection, the Sprite Garden, and it's permanent. Guides say Trick or Treat Sprites stay in your collection even after Fortnitemares ends.

Pro Tip: Look at the little info card when a Sprite drops. It tells you the Sprite's name, its level and its power, and the colourful stripe tells you it's a special variant. If it says Trick or Treat, you've hit the jackpot.

The Trick or Treat Spooky Dash is one of the most coveted Sprites in the whole game right now. Want to know where I found mine? Turn the page. It's pumpkin time.
