Chapter 6
The haul

Okay, chat, the moment of truth. One morning. A few matches. A lot of pumpkins. At the end of the video I went back to the lobby, opened my Sprite collection, and scrolled through everything I'd found. I'm not going to lie: it was exhilarating.

Here's the haul. These are real screenshots from my collection screen, straight after the session.

The Trick or Treat collection

Vampire. The first one I found, less than a minute in. Heals you when you damage an enemy.

Spooky Dash. The jack-o'-lantern one. Jump in the air to dash, phasing through some objects.

X-Ray. Marks enemies around you every so often. I said I'd lose my mind if I got this one. I got it.

Overshield. Gives you an overshield that grows as it levels up.

Adventure. Upgrades a random item in your inventory every time it levels up.

Birthday. Chests sometimes give you a slice of cake. Cake and candy. Delicious.

Crown, mastered. Of course. This one only levels up when you win, so it's the proudest one in my collection.

I also found Trick or Treat versions of four guest Sprites: Sonic, Blinky, The Deer and Tails. They belong to other companies, so we haven't shown their pictures here. That's at least eleven different Trick or Treat Sprites, in one morning, out of 24. Not bad, chat. Not bad at all.

The Sprite Whisperer's rules

  1. The pumpkins are the way. Every Pumpkin Chest drops a Sprite.
  2. Where there's one, there's more. Pumpkins come in clusters. Look around.
  3. Spin if you like. The 360 is fun. Just remember it's a ritual, not a rule.
  4. Extract the new ones. A Sprite isn't yours until it says "Added to collection".
  5. Go to its home. Hunting one Sprite in particular? Search its favourite area.
  6. Persevere. Some matches are pumpkin parties. Some are pumpkin deserts. Keep going.

Collecting all 24 won't happen in a single morning. It takes patience, and you'll have to persevere through a few disappointing matches. But every pumpkin is a fresh chance. So drop in, find a pumpkin, slide, spin, smash, and maybe, just maybe, you'll hear yourself yelling the words every Sprite Whisperer lives for: "Let's GO!"
