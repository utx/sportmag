Bonus routes

Pumpkins are my number-one method, no question. But a true Sprite Whisperer has more than one trick up their sleeve. Here are the other ways I hunted Sprites in the video, plus a secret about where each Sprite likes to turn up.

Cheat Codes

No, not actual cheating! Cheat Codes are an official part of the game this season. They're glowing panels hidden around the Island, often stuck on the side of something, like the digger in my screenshot. Each one shows a short sequence of arrows. Walk up, interact with it, and enter the arrows in exactly the right order. Get it right and you're rewarded with a random Sprite. Get it wrong and you get nothing, so concentrate.

A Cheat Code on the side of a digger. See the arrows? That's the sequence you have to copy.

Code cracked! This one gave me a Gold Storm Scout Sprite, plus 1,000 XP. A few seconds later, another code nearby coughed up a Trick or Treat Sprite.

In the video, I was going from code to code shouting, "Code right here! Lock in, lock in, lock in!" That's what you need for Cheat Codes: total concentration. The arrows only make sense if you copy them in the right sequence.

The locator gizmos

Two gizmos in my item wheel make Sprite hunting much easier:

  • Cheat Code Locator. Creates a Cheat Code somewhere nearby, inside the safe zone, and shows you where it is. Cheat Codes give you Sprites, so this is basically a Sprite-finding machine. I had thirteen of them, chat. Thirteen!
  • Lucky Locator. Buries a Sprite Chest nearby, and it's guaranteed to have a Sprite you haven't found yet. That's a collector's dream: no duplicates.

Pro Tip: Use a Cheat Code Locator when you're between pumpkin clusters. Instead of wandering about hoping to find something, you get a guaranteed target.

Every Sprite has a home

Here's a secret that most players don't know. Each Trick or Treat Sprite has a favourite area of the map, almost like an animal's habitat. Smash pumpkins, open rare chests or crack Cheat Codes in that area, and that's where guides say it's most likely to turn up.

The guest Sprites have homes too. Sonic, Tails and Shadow hang out at Green Hill Zone. Crash Bandicoot likes Stone Sanctum, The Deer likes Sunken Shores, Jackrabbit likes Freaky Fields and Nightmare Neighborhood, and Morgana likes Chopped Shop and Golden Grove. Blinky is the strangest: you'll find it around the Pac-Man islands at night, by defeating ghosts.

Homes, not cages

These areas are where a Sprite is most likely to show up, not the only place. Here's proof: my Trick or Treat Spooky Dash popped out of a pumpkin near Stone Sanctum, not Nightmare Neighborhood. So if you're desperate for one particular Sprite, go to its home area. But keep smashing every pumpkin you see, wherever you are. You never know.
