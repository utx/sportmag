About this book

Stud Wars is an unofficial fan book about LEGO Star Wars sets, made for one family to read on a Kindle. It isn't for sale, and it isn't made, approved or endorsed by the LEGO Group or by Lucasfilm.

LEGO® is a trademark of the LEGO Group, which does not sponsor, authorise or endorse this book. Star Wars™ and related names are trademarks of Lucasfilm Ltd.

Which sets are in it

The book covers every set in Brickset's LEGO Star Wars database (plus the Star Wars BrickHeadz) whose launch date had passed and whose expected retirement date had not yet arrived on 5 November 2026. That gives 74 sets. We left out free gifts with purchase and bundles of other sets. Shops may still have older sets, and some of these may sell out early.

Where the facts come from

Brickset (brickset.com): set details (pieces, minifigures, ages, sizes, US prices, launch and expected retirement dates), fans' ratings, and the staff reviews quoted throughout. Most of the reviews quoted were written by Brickset's reviewer CapnRex101; the 2017 UCS Millennium Falcon review was by Huw. Where a set had no staff review, we quoted Brickset members' reviews and Brickset's news stories about the set. SMART Play facts come from LEGO's announcement of 5 January 2026 and Brickset's reports of January and June 2026.

Rebrickable (rebrickable.com): the list of minifigures in each set.

Prices are LEGO's recommended retail prices in the United States, in US dollars, as listed by Brickset. Ratings out of five bricks, and the Play, Display and Value scores, are our own opinions, based on those reviews.
