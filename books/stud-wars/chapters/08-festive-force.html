Chapter 8
The festive force

Star Wars isn't exactly a Christmas story (there's no snow on Tatooine), but LEGO has made a Star Wars Advent Calendar every year since 2011. It's a long-running tradition, and this year it has a new twist. There's also a seasonal set that everyone seems to love.

Word Watch

Traditionnoun
Something people do the same way year after year, like a Christmas tree or a birthday cake.
Seasonaladjective
Only around for one time of year. Seasonal sets usually vanish after Christmas.
Festiveadjective
Cheerful and celebratory, especially at Christmas.
Countdownnoun
Counting backwards to a big moment. An Advent Calendar is a countdown to Christmas, one door a day.
Charmnoun
A quality that makes people like something, even if it isn't perfect.

Twenty-four doors, each with a small build or a minifigure. This year's surprise is tiny microscale animals from the Star Wars galaxy, plus minifigures of the Mandalorian, Grogu and Moff Gideon in Christmas jumpers.

Win: A Brickset member called it "a great and refreshing advent" thanks to the new micro animal builds.

Win: Another member loved the minifigures: the Christmas-jumper trio, Carson Teva, a Jawa and a festive pit droid.

Watch out: A few of the little builds are "bad", according to the first member. It's only on sale until Christmas, so get in early.

An AT-AT walker made of LEGO "gingerbread", decorated with icing stripes and sweets, with a gingerbread Darth Vader minifigure. Lift the roof and there's a cosy lounge with a fireplace inside.

Win: Brickset's reviewer called it "an absolute delight" and said the shape was "surprisingly accurate, considering its size".

Win: Even the price "feels reasonable", and the Vader minifigure is "brilliant". Fans give it 4.4 out of 5 from 151 ratings.

Watch out: The head doesn't move, and a driver can't fit in with the roof closed. That somehow adds to its charm.

Best for: putting under the Christmas tree. Our favourite festive set.
