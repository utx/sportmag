Chapter 9
The Stud Wars awards

Seventy-four sets reviewed. Now it's time to hand out some trophies, crunch some numbers and help you find the right set. (And name and shame a tank.)

Word Watch

Averagenoun
The typical amount: add up all the numbers, then divide by how many there are.
Rationoun
A comparison of two amounts, like price compared with pieces. "Cents per piece" is a ratio.
Outliernoun
A result that is very different from all the others. On a chart, it's the one sticking way out.
Bargainnoun
Something that is good value: you get more than you'd expect for the price.
Accoladenoun
An award or praise given for doing something well.

Set of the year: 75454 Imperial Remnant AT-AT with INT-4. A giant walker, a starfighter, seven figures and a 4.5 fan score. If you could have just one set from this book (and a very generous grown-up), this is it.

Best swoosh: 75432 V-19 Torrent. Light, tough, fairly priced, and in the reviewer's words it "really demands to be flown around the room".

Best army builder: 75372 Clone Trooper & Battle Droid Battle Pack. Nine figures, both sides of the war, "essentially faultless". Get it before it retires.

Best small set: 75436 The Mandalorian & Grogu's Speeder Bike. Under US$10 for the two most famous characters of the moment.

Best character: 75434 K-2SO. Possibly "the best Star Wars character statue to date".

Best festive set: 40806 Gingerbread AT-AT Walker. The most delightful set in the book. Brickset's reviewer would be happy for it to "become a series".

Best minifigures: 75437 Cobb Vanth's Speeder. "About as detailed as LEGO figures can be." (The Death Star has 40 minifigures, but it also costs US$999.99.)

The wobbly tank award: 75413 Republic Juggernaut. Our only one-brick set. Fragile, unfinished and very expensive. Sorry, Ki-Adi-Mundi.

The five-brick club

Eleven sets scored five out of five from us: the AT-AT with INT-4, the V-19 Torrent, the Clone Trooper & Battle Droid pack, K-2SO, R2-D2, Boba Fett, the little Venator, the UCS Jango's Starship, the UCS Venator, the UCS Millennium Falcon and the Gingerbread AT-AT. Notice how few of them are new SMART Play or movie sets? Sometimes the older sets are the best ones.
