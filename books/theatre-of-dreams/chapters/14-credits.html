About this book

Theatre of Dreams is an unofficial fan book made by Touchline, a weekly football magazine. It isn't made, approved or endorsed by Manchester United. It is written from a United fan's point of view, and it says so loudly, especially in Chapters 11 and 12. The opinions are ours. The facts underneath them have been checked.

Where the facts came from

Every score, date, statistic and quote in this book was checked against published reports. The main sources were: Manchester United's official website (manutd.com) and its match archive; BBC Sport; Sky Sports; ESPN; UEFA.com; the Premier League's website (premierleague.com); the National Football Museum; contemporary reports from Reuters and the Associated Press; and the match records at 11v11.com. We used Wikipedia only as a starting point, and checked what it said elsewhere.

Quotes are given in the words reported at the time. Where we couldn't find a reliable record of exactly what someone said, we described what they meant without using quotation marks. Where a famous story is probably, but not certainly, true (the lost dog in Chapter 6, the phone call in Chapter 3), we have said so.

The story of Manchester City's charges in Chapter 11 is based on the Premier League's statement and the reporting of the independent commission's verdict in September 2026, and on reports in October 2026 about City's appeal. City deny wrongdoing and are appealing.

This book describes events up to the middle of October 2026.

A note on Chapter 7

The Munich air disaster and the Hillsborough disaster are real tragedies, and the families of the people who died are still alive today. We have tried to write about them with care. If anything in this book has upset you, talk to a grown-up about it.
