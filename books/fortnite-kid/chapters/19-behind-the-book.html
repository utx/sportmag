About this book

The Fortnite Kid is an unofficial fan novel made by The Chug Jug, a fan magazine about Fortnite. It is a made-up story. Billy, Kai, Mira, Pip and everyone else in it are invented characters, and nobody has ever really fallen into a video game (we checked).

It isn't made, approved or endorsed by Epic Games. It's free, and it will never ask you to spend money. If you ever want to buy anything in Fortnite, ask a grown-up first.

What's real, and what's made up

The story is invented, but the Island it happens on is as real as we could make it. It's set in Chapter 7, Season 4: Override, during Fortnitemares 2026, the weekend after the v42.40 update of Thursday 15 October 2026.

Real (in the game in October 2026):

  • The Trick or Treat Sprite Power Hour. Epic's update notes put it at 2 p.m. US Eastern time on Saturday 17 October, which is 5 a.m. on Sunday 18 October in Sydney.
  • The things that arrived in v42.40: the Honey Sprite (made by a fan in Epic's Design a Sprite contest), the Hunting Rifle and Gatekeeper Shotgun Loot Hacks, the faster Sweeper Shotgun, and the giant pirate chest east of Sunken Shores. So did the Witch Broom being vaulted, and the Super Shot Override disappearing.
  • Geno, his Machine, the Cursed Cartridge that started Fortnitemares, and the storm growing above the Machine.
  • Pumpkin Chests (a weapon and a Sprite every time), Cheat Codes with arrow sequences, Extraction Sites and crates, Portable Extractors (one a day), Loot Hacks bought with Sprite Dust, and the Sprite Garden.
  • Override Consoles guarded by NITE Units, the Overshield and Squad Extra Life Overrides, and squads being marked as Hackers.
  • Rings at Green Hill Zone, the fog at Nightmare Neighborhood, the duck-roofed diner in Golden Grove, the fans and cooling pipes of Reality's Reign, Hop Drops that make you float, and the Override 'Brella, which you earn by winning a match this season.

Made up for the story: the Trick or Treat Billy Sprite and its card, players being pulled into the game, everything that happens inside the lobby, being able to dash through a NITE Unit or ride inside a cooling pipe, Geno's face in the storm, and the purple light under the pirate chest's lid. (Although, honestly, who knows what's in that chest?)

The pictures

The illustrations were drawn for this book by The Chug Jug, by hand, in code. Many of them recreate key moments from a real Fortnitemares gameplay video, a YouTube creator's guide to finding the new Trick or Treat Sprites: the first pumpkin smash, the Extraction Crate landing, the Cheat Code on the digger, the Victory Royale banner and the READY screen in the lobby. We didn't use any screenshots. Every picture is our own cartoon, and Billy, Kai and Mira have taken the place of the player in the video.

The cover: the background is a fan-made "Fortnite: Infinity War" poster, sent in by the publisher and used with permission (Graphic: used with permission). The lettering was added on top by The Chug Jug, and the poster's own title and logo are covered up.

Pip is our drawing of the Trick or Treat Spooky Dash Sprite, which belongs to Epic Games. We haven't drawn any characters that belong to other companies, even though some of them (like the guests at Green Hill Zone and Nightmare Neighborhood) live on the Island this season.

Where the facts came from

Epic Games' v42.40 update notes; Beebom and Fragster's write-ups of the v42.40 update; Beebom's guides to Pumpkin Chests and Trick or Treat Sprites; our earlier books Tier Pressure and The Sprite Whisperer; and the gameplay video. Facts checked on 16 October 2026. Fortnite changes all the time, so some details may already be different by the time you read this.

Play it safe

  • In the real game, teaming in Solos is against the rules, and you can be banned for it. Billy only gets away with it because he's technically a Sprite. You are not a Sprite.
  • Nobody is giving away free V-Bucks. If a website, video or message offers them, it's a scam.
  • Never share your password or your account with anyone, not even a friend.
  • This book never recommends cheats, hacks or glitches. (Cheat Codes, Loot Hacks and Hackers are official parts of the game this season.)
  • Getting up at 5 a.m. for a Power Hour is a decision best discussed with a grown-up first. Ideally not by hiding on the kitchen floor with an oven timer.
  • Take breaks. The Island will still be there after breakfast.
