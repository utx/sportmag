# Shiver Me Shields: book plan (Chug Jug fan book #2 for the Treasure Hunters mini-season)

Working title **Shiver Me Shields**: "15 tips to survive Crossbones Isle". Same pipeline as
`books/sprite-whisperer/` and `books/x-marks-the-chug/` (book.json + chapters/*.html + img/, `build/book.sh`).

Planned 3 Nov 2026. Different job from *X Marks the Chug*: that book is *what's on the island*; this one is
*how to play it smart*. Like *The Sprite Whisperer*, it turns one creator's video into a book in the creator's
voice (marked unofficial, not endorsed), with each tip explained, shown and checked.

## The source video

Evolve Jake, *15 Tips Every Fortnite Player Needs To Know In Treasure Hunters (Fortnite Zero Build Tips)*,
`WL9G72-aO0I`, 13:14, posted 3 Nov 2026. English captions are clean (his own speech). In Drive:
`Chug Jug research/Treasure Hunters videos/` (.en.srt and .en-orig.srt; video uploading).
The tips are for **Zero Build** (no building), which suits the reader. Webcam on screen: crop it out.
Left out: his creator code plug.
