About this book

Shiver Me Shields is an unofficial fan book made by The Chug Jug, a fan magazine about Fortnite. It's built around one YouTube video, Evolve Jake's 15 Tips Every Fortnite Player Needs To Know In Treasure Hunters, with the explanations, checks, challenges, true stories, Word Watch boxes and quiz added for this book. Quotes in italics are Jake's own words from the video. The book isn't written, approved or endorsed by Evolve Jake or by Epic Games.

It's free, and it never asks you to spend money. If you ever want to buy anything in Fortnite, ask a grown-up first.

Version

This book describes Fortnite Battle Royale (Zero Build) in Chapter 7: Treasure Hunters, version 43.00, as it was in early November 2026. An update is due on 12 November, and some details may change.

Where the facts came from

The tips: Evolve Jake's video (and his earlier video, Everything You Need To Know About Fortnite Treasure Hunters). Every "Chug Jug check" says whether we found the same thing elsewhere.

Game checks: Epic's Treasure Hunters announcement on the Fortnite Communities site (1 November 2026); the Fortnite Wiki (Pocket Shockwave Grenade); Beebom, Games.gg, GameRant and Destructoid (the frog statues and the waterfall cave); TyluhFryz and TG Plays (videos).

True stories: the National Park Service (the saguaro cactus); Wikipedia and Ocean-current histories (the Friendly Floatees); Australian Geographic and the University of Western Australia (the water-holding frog); Vatican News (the Swiss Guard); the Royal Australian Navy's history pages (knots and the chip log); the Official Charts Company and NZ folk-song histories (the Wellerman); and general history and science references for tangerines, barrels, locks, fireworks and wormholes.

Play it safe

  • Nobody is giving away free V-Bucks. If a website, video or message offers them, it's a scam.
  • Never share your password or your account with anyone, not even a friend.
  • This book never recommends cheats, hacks or glitches. Every trick in it is part of the game.
  • Take breaks. Crossbones Isle will still be there after dinner.
