Chapter 4
Ships to swoosh

"Swoosh" is the noise a spaceship makes when you fly it round the lounge room, and it's also a word that sounds like what it means. That's called onomatopoeia (say it: on-oh-mat-oh-PEE-ah). "Swooshability" isn't in the dictionary, but every LEGO fan knows what it means: can you hold it in one hand and fly it without bits falling off?

Many starships are in other chapters (the X-wings, the Razor Crest, the V-19). These six are the other ships built for play, from a five-dollar paper bag to a Star Destroyer nearly half a metre long.

Word Watch

Onomatopoeianoun
A word that sounds like the noise it describes, like swoosh, pew-pew, crash or buzz.
Fuselagenoun
The main body of a plane or spaceship, where the pilot and passengers sit.
Aerodynamicadjective
Shaped to move smoothly through the air. (In space there's no air, but Star Wars ships still look aerodynamic, because it looks cool.)
Canopynoun
The clear cover over a cockpit.
Liverynoun
The special colours and pattern painted on a vehicle, like a team's colours on a racing car.

Word Watch: story words

Look out for these in the "From the screen" boxes.

Looking dangerous or threatening.

Darth Vader's TIE Advanced, in miniature, in a paper bag. The perfect pocket-money set.

Win: 68 pieces for US$4.99, and Vader's ship is instantly recognisable.

Watch out: No minifigure, and Brickset fans give it 3.4 out of 5.

From the animated series Rebuild the Galaxy, where Star Wars gets mixed up: a Snowspeeder with flaming hot-rod livery, plus a matching speeder bike and three minifigures, including Darth Dev and Sig Greebling.

Win: Brickset's reviewer said the new canopy "looks excellent" and the shape is a big step up for LEGO Snowspeeders.

Win: Two vehicles in one set, and a clip that holds the bike doubles as lightsaber storage.

Watch out: "Way too expensive". The reviewer would only buy it at about 30% off.

The Rebel U-wing from Andor and Rogue One, with Cassian Andor, K-2SO, Dedra Meero and an ISB agent. The wings swing forward and back, and there are spring-loaded shooters at the front.

Win: Brickset's reviewer found the outside just as detailed as the bigger 2016 U-wing, and the price "seems reasonable".

Win: Four minifigures, three of them likely to stay exclusive to this set.

Watch out: Not much space inside the passenger bay.

Jango Fett's ship, the Slave I, from Attack of the Clones, in blue and green, with Jango, young Boba Fett and the Kaminoan Lama Su. A maintenance cart doubles as a display stand.

Win: Brickset's reviewer thought it "looks even better" than the 2021 Boba Fett's Starship, with more functions.

Win: Lama Su and Jango are especially good minifigures.

Watch out: The stud shooters don't rotate, and it "simply feels too expensive for a model of this size".

An Imperial Star Destroyer, 46 centimetres long, with an interior you can open up and seven minifigures, including Darth Vader and Cal Kestis from the Jedi video games.

Win: Brickset's reviewer found "plenty of improvements" over the 2014 version, "far more than I expected", with a tidier, more detailed outside.

Win: Inside there's a bridge, a hologram room and a medical bay.

Watch out: The reviewer found the price "expensive", "but once discounted, I would recommend" it.

Another Rebuild the Galaxy set: a black-and-red, dark-side Millennium Falcon with a mini superlaser and six odd minifigures, including Darth Jar Jar and Jedi Vader.

Win: The minifigures are "superb", says Brickset's reviewer, and the inside is decent.

Watch out: It's basically the 2019 Millennium Falcon in different colours. The reviewer said "this is not a set I can recommend at any price really, other than for the minifigures."
