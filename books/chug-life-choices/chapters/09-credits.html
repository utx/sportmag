About this book

Chug Life Choices is an unofficial fan book made by The Chug Jug, a fan magazine about Fortnite. It isn't made, approved or endorsed by Epic Games. It's free, and it will never ask you to spend money. If you ever want to buy anything in Fortnite, ask a grown-up first.

Version

Real game details describe Fortnite Battle Royale in Chapter 7, Season 4: Override, during Fortnitemares, as the Island was on 18 October 2026 (game version 42.40). Midas' Masterpiece was removed from the game on 15 October 2026, which is why dilemma #3 imagines it coming back. The season ends at the start of November.

Where the facts came from

Items, weapons, Sprites, Overrides and vehicles: our earlier book Tier Pressure (which drew on Epic's update notes, the Fortnite Wiki and guides from Dexerto, Beebom and GameRant), and Beebom's notes on the v42.40 update.

Places: our earlier book Where We Droppin'?, and the Fortnite Wiki (fortnite.weirdgloop.org).

Off the Island boxes: general reference sources on Buridan's ass and Aristotle; the Swiss Army knife and Karl Elsener; echolocation in bats and magnetic navigation in loggerhead turtles; the International Space Station and Australia's Antarctic stations; the peacock mantis shrimp, tardigrades in space (2007) and octopus camouflage; Francis Galton's ox-weighing competition (1906); Edward Lear, Lewis Carroll and portmanteau words; Walter Mischel's marshmallow experiments and later studies that repeated them; and Carol Dweck's research on growth mindset.

The would-you-rather questions, the arguments for each side and the Chug Jug verdicts are our own. You're allowed to disagree with every one of them.

Play it safe

  • Never share your password or your account with anyone, not even a friend.
  • This book never recommends cheats, hacks or glitches.
  • Take breaks. The Island will still be there after dinner.
