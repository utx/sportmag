Previously, on the Island…

If you've been away from the Island for a few weeks, you might want to sit down. A lot has happened. There's a villain who wants to hack reality, a cursed video-game cartridge, a town that appeared out of nowhere, a gun that ties balloons to people, and a pumpkin rocket you can ride like a horse.

Here's everything you need to know, in about the time it takes to drink a Chug Jug.

The story so far

This is Chapter 7, Season 4: Override. It kicked off on 20 August, and the villain is Geno, a very shiny, very smug character who calls himself "Perfection, embodied." He has built a giant machine in the middle of the Island, and his plan is to hack reality itself.

To fight back, the Island got Override Consoles: seven machines dotted around the map. Switch one on and you change the rules of the whole match. Some make everyone faster. Some turn you invisible while you sprint. Some are just ridiculous. (The catch: activating one tells the entire lobby exactly where you are. Good luck.)

Then, on 1 October, Geno jammed a Cursed Cartridge into his machine, and the Island went spooky. Welcome to Fortnitemares. Fog rolls between the trees. The sky turned purple. Two brand-new places appeared: Nightmare Neighborhood in the north and Freaky Fields, with a gigantic pumpkin you can climb. Pumpkin Chests popped up everywhere. A few guests from horror films turned up too, but they're far too scary for this book, so you won't see them here.

Balloon Popper Assault Rifle

The silliest weapon in the game, and we love it. It's an Exotic: a normal assault rifle with a superpower bolted on. The 1st, 12th and 24th shots of every magazine explode into balloons, which tie themselves to anyone nearby. One balloon makes you floaty. Two make you floatier. Three lift you clean off the ground, legs dangling, while everyone laughs. It's not the strongest rifle (a normal Legendary Assault Rifle hits harder), but nothing beats it for comedy. Find it in vaults, special rifts, or by winning the Duck Race at Heatwave Harbor. Yes, there's a duck race.

Bonerattler SMG

The star of Fortnitemares. Aim down its sights and a purple flashlight switches on that shows enemies' skeletons through walls, as long as they're close. Nobody can hide behind a door from you ever again. It's also the fastest-damaging normal weapon in the game. Spooky and brilliant.

8-Bit Shotgun

A fully automatic shotgun that holds ten shells, reloads them all at once, and keeps score on your screen with combo multipliers, like an old arcade game. Miss a shot? You get another go almost straight away.

Easy Ride Pumpkin Launcher

It fires a grinning jack-o'-lantern rocket that starts off slowly. Slowly enough for you to jump on and ride it, before it speeds up. It's a tribute to the rocket-riding days of Fortnite's very first seasons. Look for it in the chest on top of the giant pumpkin at Freaky Fields.

Wood Stake Shotgun

Most shotguns spray pellets. This one fires a single wooden stake. Land a headshot and it does a whopping 177 damage. Miss, though, and you've missed completely. High risk, high reward, very Fortnitemares.

Witch Broom

Hop on and it blasts you high into the sky, smashing anything in the way, then lets you glide wherever you like. Press fire and you drop straight down, with no fall damage. Perfect for escaping a fight that's going badly. Just don't fly slowly over a crowd. Everyone can see you up there.

Midas' Masterpiece

A solid gold pistol that once belonged to Midas, one of Fortnite's most famous characters. One headshot does 313 damage, enough to eliminate anybody, even with full health and shield. The catch: it holds only seven bullets, and then it vanishes. There's usually only one per match, and it arrives in a loot drop that the whole lobby can see on the map. So expect company.

1-Up Token and Slap Candy Corn

The 1-Up Token is an extra life, just like in old arcade games. Get eliminated while you're carrying one and you come straight back through a rift, with all your loot and 100 health. Slap Candy Corn heals you 30 a piece in under a second and lets you sprint for 20 seconds without getting tired. It comes in stacks of six. Some pros say they'd rather carry it than a Chug Jug. (We're trying not to take that personally.)

Top 5 silliest things on the Island right now

  1. Floating opponents. Three balloons from a Balloon Popper, and your enemy drifts into the sky like a lost party balloon.
  2. Riding a pumpkin. The Easy Ride Pumpkin Launcher lets you surf a flying jack-o'-lantern across the map.
  3. Duck snacks. Quack Snacks are crunchy duck-shaped treats ("The snack that powers your back!") that heal you AND level up your Sprite.
  4. Hiding in a bin. The Dumpster Dive Sprite lets you jump into hiding spots and find extra snacks inside. Glamorous.
  5. The Duck Race. There's an actual duck race at Heatwave Harbor, and the prize is an Exotic weapon.

True story: the guns change all the time

Epic swaps weapons in and out of the game every few weeks. When a weapon is taken out, players say it's been vaulted. When it comes back, it's been unvaulted. This season, Epic vaulted the Drum Gun to make room for the Bonerattler, so if you can't find your old favourite, it's probably locked away somewhere.

Uh-oh. Geno's latest move.

And speaking of things locked away… we have bad news. Geno has stolen the Cursed Cartridge, stuffed it inside a secret vault, and locked the door with a 13-letter code.

Luckily, Geno is terrible at remembering passwords. So he scribbled each letter on a scrap of paper and hid one in every chapter from here to chapter 14. He thinks nobody will ever find them all.

Here's how you find them. Every chapter has a Vault Quest. Solve it, then turn the page: the answer is on the next page, along with your vault letter. Write the letter in the box with the same number as the chapter. Copy this card onto a piece of paper so you can fill it in as you go:

Fill all 13 boxes, and in chapter 15 you get to open the vault. Oh, and look out for Geno himself. He pops up in every chapter with a Worst Take: a terrible opinion that sounds clever but isn't. Your job is to roast it.
