Chapter 14
How champions win

The biggest event in competitive Fortnite is the FNCS Global Championship. This year it was played in duos (pairs) on 26 and 27 September, with 50 teams from around the world battling over 12 matches for a $2 million prize pool. The winners took home $400,000 and a trophy called the Axe of Champions.

After 12 matches, the winners were just two points ahead of second place. And here's the weird bit: they didn't even get the most eliminations. So how did they win?

How to fib with a chart

Charts are great for showing numbers at a glance. But a chart can be misleading even when every number on it is true. The sneakiest trick is to start the side of the chart at a number bigger than zero. These two charts show exactly the same scores:

Same numbers, different story
First place 525 points, second place 523 points

On the sneaky chart, first place looks like it crushed second place. On the honest one, you can see the truth: it was incredibly close. Whenever you see a chart, check where the side starts!

Vault Quest 14

Imagine a tournament where a Victory Royale is worth 10 points, finishing in the top 10 (without winning) is worth 5 points, and each elimination is worth 1 point. Over three games, Team A gets one Victory Royale with 4 elims, then finishes outside the top 10 twice, with 1 elim each time. How many points does Team A score?

Turn the page for the answer and your vault letter.

Answer: Vault Quest 14

16 points.

How to crack it: game 1: 10 for the win + 4 elims = 14. Games 2 and 3: no placement points, just 1 elim each = 2. Total: 14 + 2 = 16. (Fun fact: a team that finished in the top 10 all three times with 2 elims each would get 7 + 7 + 7 = 21, and beat them. Consistency wins again!)
