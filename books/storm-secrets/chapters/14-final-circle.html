Chapter 14
The Final Circle

This is it. The storm is closing, the circle is tiny, and only the sharpest brains are left standing. Ten challenges stand between you and a Victory Royale, and then the vault. Each one uses a secret from earlier in the book, and they get harder as the circle shrinks.

Grab a pencil. Take your time; there's no timer on this storm. Write down your answers, then check them in How to Think It Through. Every correct answer means you survive one more circle.

Circle 5 · Epic · 30 XP

Places: Stone Sanctum, Lifty Lodge, Heatwave Harbor, Freaky Fields.
Sprites: Spooky Dash, Vampire, Dumpster Dive, Honey.

  1. Peely landed at Lifty Lodge.
  2. Whoever landed at Heatwave Harbor found the Vampire Sprite.

  5. Whoever landed at Freaky Fields did not find Dumpster Dive.

Open the vault

The storm has closed. The Island is quiet. In front of you stands Geno's vault: a huge steel door with a keypad, 13 letters long.

Get out your Vault Code Card. You should have one letter from the Vault Quest in every chapter, from 1 to 13. If any boxes are empty, go back and crack those quests first. (No peeking at the walkthroughs. Well, not yet.)

Read your letters from box 1 to box 13. The gaps between the groups are the gaps between words. Does it say something? (If it's gibberish, one of your answers might be off. Check which word looks wrong and redo that chapter's Vault Quest.)

When you think you've got it, type it into the keypad, and turn to the very end of the Walkthroughs to find out if the door swings open.

Count your XP

Add up the XP from every quest you cracked by yourself, plus 20 for every Geno take you roasted. Cracked the vault? Add the 240 XP bonus. Then check your rank:

Bronze 0 · Silver 300 · Gold 750 · Platinum 1,200 · Diamond 1,650 · Elite 2,100 · Champion 2,500 · Unreal 2,850
