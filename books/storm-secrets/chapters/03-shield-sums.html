Chapter 3
Heal like a pro

Look at the bottom of your screen in any match and you'll see two bars. Health goes up to 100. Shield sits on top of it, also up to 100, and soaks up damage first. Epic could have picked any number for the maximum. They picked 100, and that makes your health bar much more useful than it looks, once you know how to read it. Pros do. And they know one more secret: the order you heal in can win or lose a fight.

Word Drop

Your health bar is secretly a fraction

One bar, three ways to say it
Because the bar goes up to 100, the number IS the percentage

If you've got 50 shield, your bar is half full: ½. With 25, it's a quarter: ¼. And because the bar goes up to 100, the number of points is also the percentage. 75 shield is 75%. Different ways of writing the same thing are called equivalent.

The Romans, a tax and the number 100

The word per cent comes from Latin, the language of ancient Rome: per centum means "for each hundred". The Romans loved counting in hundredths. When Emperor Augustus needed money for his army, more than 2,000 years ago, he brought in a tax on things sold at auction: one hundredth of the price. It was called the centesima rerum venalium, "the hundredth of things for sale". Today we'd call it a 1% tax. (A later emperor, Tiberius, cut it to half a per cent. People liked him a bit more after that.)

The % sign took a long time to appear. Italian merchants wrote per cento, and over hundreds of years they wrote it shorter and squigglier until it turned into the symbol we use now.

True story: the Egyptians' weird fraction rule

About 3,500 years ago, an Egyptian scribe named Ahmes copied out a scroll full of number puzzles. It's called the Rhind Mathematical Papyrus, and you can see it in the British Museum. The Egyptians had a strange rule: they liked fractions with 1 on top (½, 1⁄3, ¼, 1⁄5…), called unit fractions. To write ¾, they'd write ½ + ¼ instead. Every fraction had to be built out of different unit fractions, like building a wall out of blocks. Try it yourself in Quest 3.7.
