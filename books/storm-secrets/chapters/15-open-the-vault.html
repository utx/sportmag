Chapter 15
Open the vault

The storm has closed. The Island is quiet. And in front of you stands Geno's vault: a huge steel door with a keypad, 13 letters long.

Get out your Vault Code Card. You should have a letter in every box, from 2 to 14. Any empty boxes? Go back and crack those Vault Quests first. We'll wait.

Now read your letters from box 2 to box 14. The gaps between the groups are the gaps between words. Does it say something? If it looks like gibberish, one of your letters might be wrong. Find the word that looks odd and check that chapter's Vault Quest again.

Got it? Type it into the keypad…

…and turn the page.

Does the vault open?

The 13 letters are:

G E N O · N E E D S · A · N A P

The code is GENO NEEDS A NAP.

The keypad beeps. Somewhere deep inside the door, something goes clunk. The vault swings slowly open, and there, glowing on a little stand, is the Cursed Cartridge. Stuck to it is a sticky note in Geno's handwriting:

"Password reminder: GENO NEEDS A NAP. Do NOT tell anyone. Especially not that Chug Jug book."

It turns out "Perfection, embodied" gets very grumpy when he stays up too late plotting. You grab the cartridge. Somewhere across the Island, a villain yawns.

You cracked it!

Look at everything you did on the way. You outsmarted the storm, healed like a pro, beat the ghosts at their own game, counted triangles, made perfect callouts, cracked a Roman cipher AND a vault lock, busted a pile of myths, spotted a sneaky chart, and roasted Geno thirteen times.

Not bad for one book. Now go and drop somewhere. Geno has a nap to take.
