Caesar's secret letters

More than 2,000 years ago, the Roman general Julius Caesar needed to send orders that his enemies couldn't read, even if they intercepted them. His trick was simple: he swapped every letter for the letter three places later in the alphabet. A became D, B became E, and so on. At the end of the alphabet you wrap back round, so X becomes A. It's still called the Caesar cipher.

The Caesar wheel
Outer ring: your message.
Inner ring: the secret letter.

To encrypt, go from outside to inside.
To decrypt, go from inside to outside.

Example: CAT → FDW

Dots and dashes

Some codes aren't secret at all. In the 1840s, inventors sent messages down long electric wires as short and long beeps: Morse code. Here's the whole alphabet:

The Morse code alphabet

Real or fake? SOS means "Save Our Souls"

Fake! The famous distress signal, three dots, three dashes, three dots, doesn't stand for anything at all. It was picked because it's easy to send and impossible to mistake, even if you're panicking.

True story: the secret code-breakers

During the Second World War, thousands of people worked in total secret at a country house in England called Bletchley Park, cracking enemy codes. Most of them were women. They used clever maths, giant machines and ideas first worked out by three brilliant Polish code-breakers. They weren't allowed to tell anyone what they'd done, not even their families, for about 30 years.

Geno's vault has a three-digit lock, and all three digits are different. A spy left you these clues about four wrong guesses. Crack the code, then add its three digits together.

Turn the page for the answer and your vault letter.

Answer: Vault Quest 10

The code is 469, and 4 + 6 + 9 = 19.

How to crack it:

  1. "0 8 7: nothing is right." So 0, 8 and 7 are all out.
  2. "9 8 6: two are right, both in the wrong place." 8 is out, so 9 and 6 must both be in the code. 9 isn't first, and 6 isn't last.
  3. "0 9 3: one is right, in the wrong place." We know 9 is in the code, so that's the one, and 3 is out. 9 isn't in the middle either.
  4. So 9 isn't first and isn't in the middle: it must be last.
  5. "4 3 7: one is right, in the right place." 3 and 7 are out, so 4 is first.
  6. That leaves 6 in the middle: 469.

Vault letter: S (the 19th letter of the alphabet). Write it in box 10.
