The perfect drop

Every match starts the same way. A blue bus, held up by a hot-air balloon (nobody has ever explained how), flies across the Island. You thank the bus driver, because you have manners. Then you leap out of the back and plummet towards the ground.

Where you land decides almost everything. So let's get it right.

Hot drop or quiet drop?

A hot drop is a busy landing spot with lots of loot and lots of people who want it. A quiet drop is somewhere peaceful, where you can loot in peace but might end up with a grey pistol when the storm arrives. Here's the cheat sheet for right now:

Chug Jug tip: Watch where the bus is flying. Spots right under its path get busy. Spots far from the path are quieter, but you'll have to glide further to reach them.

The banana question

Here's a puzzle. You and a squad-mate jump at exactly the same moment. They're wearing the heaviest, chunkiest armoured outfit in their locker. You're dressed as a banana. Who hits the ground first?

In Fortnite, you land together. And in real life? For about 2,000 years, most people thought the heavy thing would win. It took a trip to the Moon to settle it properly.

True story: the hammer and the feather

On 2 August 1971, at the end of his last walk on the Moon, astronaut David Scott stood in front of a TV camera holding a hammer in one hand and a falcon feather in the other. On the Moon there's no air at all. He let go of both at the same moment, and they fell side by side and landed together. "How about that!" cheered Mission Control. On Earth, the feather would have floated down slowly, because air pushes against it. Take the air away, and heavy and light things fall exactly the same.

IRL Challenge: the paper race. Take two identical sheets of paper. Scrunch one into a tight ball and leave the other flat. They weigh exactly the same. Drop them from the same height at the same time. Which lands first? Now you know: it's the air, not the weight.

True story: the first daredevil

On 22 October 1797, a Frenchman named André-Jacques Garnerin went up about a kilometre over Paris in a hydrogen balloon, sitting in a basket under a folded parachute. Then he cut himself loose. His parachute had no hole in the top, so air kept spilling out of the sides and he swung wildly from side to side all the way down, while a crowd watched. He landed shaken but safe: the first real parachute jump in history. Two years later, his wife Jeanne-Geneviève became the first woman to do it. Total daredevils.

Quest 2.1

Four players jump out of the Battle Bus: Jonesy, Peely, Fishstick and Brite Bomber. They land one at a time. Work out the order they landed in.

  • Peely landed immediately before Jonesy.
  • Brite Bomber landed some time after Jonesy.
  • Fishstick landed before Peely.

Who landed first, and who landed last?

Turn the page for the answer.

Answer: Quest 2.1

Fishstick, Peely, Jonesy, Brite Bomber. Fishstick landed first and Brite Bomber landed last.

How to crack it: Peely and Jonesy are glued together as a pair: Peely, then Jonesy. Brite Bomber comes somewhere after them, and Fishstick comes somewhere before them. With only four places, the only order that works is Fishstick, Peely, Jonesy, Brite Bomber.

Vault Quest 2

Your squad of 4 lands at Stone Sanctum. Two duos (pairs) and one solo player land there too. In the first minute, 2 players get knocked out (and nobody comes back). How many players are still at Stone Sanctum?

Turn the page for the answer and your vault letter.

Answer: Vault Quest 2

7 players.

How to crack it: your squad is 4. Two duos are 2 + 2 = 4 more. One solo makes 1 more. That's 4 + 4 + 1 = 9 players. Two get knocked out: 9 − 2 = 7.

Vault letter: G (the 7th letter of the alphabet). Write it in box 2.

Geno's Worst Take

"My armour is the heaviest on the Island. So when I jump out of the Battle Bus, I'll fall faster than everybody else, land first and get all the best loot. Perfection!"

Geno has made two mistakes. Can you roast them both?

Turn the page for the roast.

The roast

Mistake 1: heavy things don't fall faster. In Fortnite everyone falls at the same speed, and in real life it's the air that slows things down, not their weight. (Remember the hammer and the feather.)

Mistake 2: even if he did land first, that wouldn't guarantee the best loot. Where you land matters far more than when. Sorry, Geno.
