Chapter 4
Heal like a pro

Here's a secret the best players know: matches aren't won by the player who shoots best. They're won by the player who heals best. Win a fight with 12 health left and you're one stray bullet from the lobby. Win it, then calmly top yourself up to full, and you're ready for whatever comes next.

You've got two bars. Health goes up to 100. Shield sits on top, up to another 100, and takes the damage first. And because both bars go up to 100, the numbers are easy to read: 75 shield means your shield bar is three-quarters full.

The heal-order secret

Look at the catches in that table. A Small Shield Potion stops working once you reach 50 shield. A bandage stops working at 75 health. That means the order you heal in can make a big difference. Try this:

Quest 4.1 · Would you rather?

You have 0 shield, two Small Shield Potions and one Shield Potion. Would you rather…

Plan A: drink both small ones first, then the big one? or
Plan B: drink the big one first, then both small ones?

How much shield do you end up with in each plan?

Turn the page for the answer.

Answer: Quest 4.1

Plan A wins: 100 shield. Plan B only gets you 50.

How to crack it: in Plan A, the two small potions take you from 0 to 25 to 50. Then the big potion adds 50 more: 100. In Plan B, the big potion takes you straight to 50, and then the small potions don't work at all, because they stop at 50. You've wasted both of them! Pros always drink their minis first.

Vault Quest 4

You're carrying 20 pieces of Halloween candy. You eat 7⁄10 of them. How many pieces did you eat?

Turn the page for the answer and your vault letter.

Answer: Vault Quest 4

14 pieces.

How to crack it: split 20 into 10 equal groups and each group has 2 pieces. That's 1⁄10. You ate 7 of those groups: 7 × 2 = 14.

Vault letter: N (the 14th letter of the alphabet). Write it in box 4.

Geno's Worst Take

"My shield was 100. I took a hit and it went down by half. Then I drank something that made it go up by half. Down half, up half: I'm back to 100. Perfect maths, as usual."

What's Geno's shield really on?

Turn the page for the roast.

The roast

Geno's shield is on 75, not 100. Down by half from 100 leaves 50. But "up by half" means half of 50, which is only 25. And 50 + 25 = 75. The two halves are halves of different amounts, so they don't cancel out. Geno is walking around with a cracked shield and no idea.
