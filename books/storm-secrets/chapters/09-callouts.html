Callouts like a pro

"Enemy at D5! Pushing from the north-east!" In squads, a good callout can save the whole team. A bad one ("He's over there! No, THERE!") gets everyone eliminated. The secret is the grid. Your map is split into squares, each with a letter and a number, so you can say exactly where something is in two words.

Real or fake? Greenland is as big as Africa

Fake! On lots of world maps, Greenland looks about the same size as Africa. But in real life, Africa is about 14 times bigger. Flat maps of a round world have to stretch something, and the most common kind of world map (invented in 1569 for sailors) stretches the places near the North and South Poles, like Greenland. Even Australia is more than three times bigger than Greenland.

(a) Chopped Shop (b) Shaken Sanctuary (c) E2 (d) Reality's Reign

How to crack it: for a square like A3, go along to the letter first, then down to the number. For (d), north-east means up and to the right on the map. Reality's Reign (E4) is up and to the right of Stone Sanctum (D5). Heatwave Harbor is off to the left, in the west.
