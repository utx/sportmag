TIP12
Drain the temple
Stone Sanctum
STILL WORKSeven better

Stone Sanctum is the ancient temple near the middle of the Island, which took the place of Sinister Strip this season. It has about 32 chests, which is already plenty. But it also has a hidden room, and opening it is, in Jake's words, "actually pretty weird."

How to drain the temple
1
CLIMB THE TOWERS
There are three tall towers. Walk around the edge of the top section of each.
2
SMASH THE POT
Get close enough and a little pot appears. Break it to get a piece (a Temple Vault Key).
3
DO IT TWICE
Put the piece down at the bottom. You need two pieces in total.
4
WATCH IT DRAIN
The water drains away, revealing a secret room full of chests.

Inside, Jake found rare chests and Slurp Barrels (shoot or smash one and it gives you health and shield). His tip: if you land here, or pass through mid-game and nobody seems to have opened it yet, "you can really easily do this." And a funny detail: "There is three, and you only need two, for some reason." That gives you a spare if someone else gets to a tower first.

Then and now

Still works, and it's even better. Since the update of 17 September, one of the three rare chests in the vault has a chance of holding an Exotic weapon. The secret room also has Cheat Codes now. The Fortnite Wiki counts four pots, not three, but either way: you only need two.

TIP13
Start the Duck Race
Heatwave Harbor
CHANGEDbetter prize

Heatwave Harbor, the sunny city on two islands, has a Duck Race: start it, finish it, and you win a prize. When Jake made his video, the prize was always the same: "Every time I've done it, I've gotten a high rarity pump." A purple or gold Pump Shotgun, plus some extra loot, just for taking part in a race. Not bad at all.

Then and now

Changed, for the better. Since 17 September, finishing the Duck Race gives you one of four Exotic weapons instead. You don't get to choose which one:

Duck Race prizes now
Overpowered PumpStronger with every hit
Overclocked 8-BitTrick shots heal you
Balloon PopperTies balloons to enemies
Accelerant Drum GunFaster the longer you fire
Chug Jug tip: Every duck racer wants the same prize, so Heatwave Harbor can get busy. Win the race, then move on before someone comes to take your new toy.
TIP14
Find the duck in sunglasses
Cluster Coast
STILL WORKS

Cluster Coast, the fancy beach club in the south-east, has a hidden vault, and the key is a rubber duck wearing sunglasses. It turns up in a different spot each match, somewhere around the area, so you'll need to hunt for it. Take it to the bottom part of the building and the vault opens, with several rare chests and "a lot of gold" (Gold Bars, the money you spend at vending machines on better weapons and heals).

Jake points out that, unlike last season, there's no special Mythic weapon hidden here. It's still a quick, guaranteed way to fill your pockets with gold early in a match.

Then and now

Still works. The duck vault arrived at Cluster Coast last season, Jake found it still working in this one, and we haven't found any reports of it changing since.

TIP15
Go golden fishing
Golden spots + Midas Flopper
STILL WORKS

Fishing spots are the bubbling patches you see on rivers, lakes and the sea. Most are blue. But some are gold, and those always give you gold (Legendary) loot when you fish them with a fishing rod. Jake's habit: whenever you're looting a place with fishing spots, "check to see if they're gold, because if they are gold, it's worth fishing."

But only early on. In the middle or end of a match, standing still at the water's edge makes you an easy target, so Jake doesn't bother. At the start, when you don't have good loot yet, it's a brilliant shortcut.

The fish that turns everything gold

Golden spots also have a good chance of giving you a Midas Flopper, a golden fish named after Midas, the Fortnite character who turned everything he touched into gold. Eat it and it heals you, and it transforms every weapon in your inventory into its gold version.

The Midas Flopper trick
1
PICK YOUR FAVOURITES
Carry the weapons you actually like, even if they're green or blue.
2
EAT THE FISH
Every weapon in your inventory turns gold.
3
SHARE IT
In a team, collect your teammates' weapons first, eat the fish, then hand them back.

Jake's example: say you've fished up some gold Pump Shotguns, but you'd much rather use the 8-Bit Shotgun. Eat the Midas Flopper while you're holding the 8-Bit, and now you've got a gold 8-Bit. And in team modes, "have your teammates give you their weapons and transform them into gold as well."

Then and now

Still works. Golden fishing spots and the Midas Flopper are both still in the game. Look out for the Big Fish Override, too: if someone switches it on, every fishing spot on the Island turns gold for the rest of the match.
