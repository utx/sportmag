TIP12
Drain the temple
Stone Sanctum
EARLY GAMEeven better now

Stone Sanctum is the ancient temple near the centre of the Island, which replaced Sinister Strip at the start of the season. Behind it lies a maze in which walls and platforms materialise and vanish as you approach. It already holds about 32 chests, which is plenty. But Stone Sanctum's real treasure is underneath your feet: a flooded secret room, and a rather peculiar puzzle that opens it.

How to drain the temple
1
CLIMB THE TOWERS
Make your way to the top section of the temple's tall towers, and walk around the edge.
2
SMASH THE POT
Get close enough and a little pot appears. Break it to collect a Temple Vault Key.
3
DO IT TWICE
Place the key down at the bottom. You need two keys in total.
4
WATCH IT DRAIN
The water drains away, revealing a secret room full of chests.

The pots only appear once you're close, which is why so many players never notice them. Walking slowly around the edge of each tower's upper section is the most reliable way to make them show up. There are more pots than you need, so if another player has already smashed one, simply move on to the next tower.

Once two keys are in place, the water drains away and reveals the vault. Inside you'll find three Rare Chests, Slurp Barrels (break one and it restores both health and shield) and even Cheat Codes. If you land at Stone Sanctum, this should be your first job. If you're passing through mid-match and the vault still looks flooded, it's well worth a few minutes of your time.

Beyond the Island: real underground water palaces

Hidden rooms beneath temples full of water aren't just a game idea. Fifteen hundred years ago, the engineers of the city now called Istanbul built a vast underground reservoir known today as the Basilica Cistern, to store water for the city. Its roof is held up by 336 stone columns standing in the water, and visitors today walk on raised paths through the dim, echoing halls. Two of the columns rest on enormous carved stone heads of Medusa, a monster from Greek myth, placed upside down and sideways. Nobody knows for certain why.

Since launch day

The vault is even better than when the season began. Since 17 September, one of its three Rare Chests has had a chance of holding an Exotic weapon, and the room now contains Cheat Codes too.

TIP13
Start the Duck Race
Heatwave Harbor
EARLY GAMEbetter prize

Heatwave Harbor is the sunny, Miami-style city spread across two islands and joined by a bridge. It's a well-rounded place to land, with plenty of chests and water to escape into if things go wrong. But its best feature is a Duck Race. Start it, complete it, and you win a prize at the finish.

Right now, that prize is one of four Exotic weapons. You don't get to choose which one, and every one of them is excellent:

Duck Race prizes now
Overpowered PumpStronger with every hit
Overclocked 8-BitTrick shots heal you
Balloon PopperTies balloons to enemies
Accelerant Drum GunFaster the longer you fire
  • Overpowered Pump Shotgun: every hit makes the next shot stronger, up to 25 extra damage, as long as you keep landing shots.
  • Overclocked 8-Bit Shotgun: fully automatic, and hitting someone while you're in the air or sliding gives you health or shield back.
  • Balloon Popper Assault Rifle: some of its shots burst into balloons that tie themselves to your opponents and lift them off the ground. Ridiculous, and brilliant.
  • Accelerant Drum Gun: the longer you hold the trigger, the faster it fires and the faster you run.
Chug Jug tip: Everybody racing ducks wants the same prize, so Heatwave Harbor can become crowded. Win the race, then move on promptly before somebody arrives to take your new weapon from you.

Beyond the Island: real duck races

Real duck races exist too. Charities all over the world hold "duck derbies", in which hundreds or even thousands of numbered rubber ducks are tipped into a river at the same moment. People buy a duck to raise money, and whoever owns the first duck to drift across the finish line wins a prize. Nobody steers: the current decides everything, which makes it a lesson in pure chance.

Since launch day

At the start of the season, the Duck Race prize was a high-rarity Pump Shotgun. Since 17 September it has been an Exotic, which is a considerable upgrade.

TIP14
Find the duck in sunglasses
Cluster Coast
EARLY GAME

Cluster Coast, the fancy beach club in the south-east with a duck-shaped yacht, has a secret vault, and the key to it is a rubber duck wearing sunglasses. The duck turns up in a different place each match, somewhere around the area, so finding it requires a little detective work. Search the obvious spots first: around the pools and the fountain, and anywhere with water.

Once you have it, carry the duck to the lower part of the building, and the vault opens. Inside are several Rare Chests and a generous pile of Gold Bars, the money you spend at vending machines on better weapons, healing and pocket-item swaps. There's no special Mythic weapon here this season, but for a quick, guaranteed injection of gold early in a match, it's hard to beat.

Chug Jug tip: Cluster Coast is a long way from the middle of the map, and it's often quiet. That makes it a peaceful place to land, but keep an eye on the storm. Grab the duck, open the vault, spend your gold at a vending machine, and start moving towards the centre.

Since launch day

The duck vault first appeared at Cluster Coast last season, and it has stayed for this one. We haven't found any reports of it changing since launch day.

TIP15
Go golden fishing
Golden spots + Midas Flopper
EARLY GAME

Fishing spots are the bubbling, rippling patches you'll notice on rivers, lakes and the sea. Most of them are blue. But some are gold, and a golden fishing spot always gives you gold (Legendary) loot when you fish it with a fishing rod. So make it a habit: whenever you're looting a place near water, glance at the fishing spots. If one is gold, it's worth stopping to fish.

Timing matters, though. Fishing means standing still at the water's edge, often in the open, which leaves you vulnerable. Early in a match, when you don't yet have good loot and fewer players are nearby, a golden spot is a brilliant shortcut. Later, when the circle is small and every player is hunting, it's usually too dangerous to be worth it.

The fish that turns everything gold

Golden spots also have a good chance of giving you a Midas Flopper, a golden fish named after Midas, the Fortnite character who turned everything he touched to gold. Eat it and it heals you, and it transforms every weapon in your inventory into its gold, Legendary version.

The Midas Flopper trick
1
PICK YOUR FAVOURITES
Carry the weapons you actually like, even if they're green or blue.
2
EAT THE FISH
Every weapon in your inventory turns gold.
3
SHARE IT
In a team, collect your teammates' weapons first, eat the fish, then hand them back.

Here's an example. Suppose you've fished up a gold Pump Shotgun, but you'd much rather use the 8-Bit Shotgun you found earlier. Keep the 8-Bit in your inventory, eat the Midas Flopper, and you've got a gold 8-Bit as well. In team modes it's even more powerful: ask your teammates to drop their weapons for you, pick them up, eat the fish, and hand back a set of golden weapons. One fish, a whole squad upgraded.

Beyond the Island: the real King Midas

Fortnite's Midas is named after a king from ancient Greek mythology. In the legend, King Midas of Phrygia did a favour for the god Dionysus, who offered him any wish he liked. Consumed by avarice, Midas wished that everything he touched would turn to gold. At first he was delighted, turning twigs and stones into treasure. Then he sat down to eat, and his bread turned to gold in his hands, and his wine turned to gold on his lips. Starving, he begged Dionysus to take the gift away, and was told to wash in the River Pactolus. The story goes that the river's sands have glittered with gold ever since.

The moral? Be careful what you wish for. (Fortnite's Midas Flopper, luckily, only turns your weapons gold, not your snacks.)

Since launch day

Golden fishing spots and the Midas Flopper are both still in the game. Look out for the Big Fish Override, too: if somebody activates it at an Override Console, every fishing spot on the Island turns gold for the rest of the match.
