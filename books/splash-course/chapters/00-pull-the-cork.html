Fifteen tips. One big check-up.

A Chug Splash is the quickest heal in Fortnite: one throw and you're topped up. This book is the reading version. Fifteen quick tips, each one short enough to read while your squad is still choosing where to land.

The tips come from a YouTube video called 15 Tips Every Fortnite Player Needs To Know In Chapter 7 Season 4, made by a creator called Evolve Jake. Jake specialises in Zero Build, the mode where nobody can build walls or ramps, so every tip is about moving smartly, healing fast and finding good loot. He recorded the video in late August 2026, in the very first days of the Override season.

And that's where things get interesting.

Fortnite moves fast

Since Jake made his video, the Island has had four updates: on 3 September, on 17 September, the big Fortnitemares update on 1 October, and another on 15 October. Items have been taken out of the game (Fortnite calls this vaulting), prizes have changed, and one boss has packed his bags and left.

So some of Jake's tips are now outdated. That's not his fault. It's what happens to every guide, in every game, eventually. A good reader doesn't just believe a tip because a video said it. A good reader asks: is this still true?

So that's what we did. We took every one of the fifteen tips and tried to verify it, using the official update notes from Epic, the Fortnite Wiki, and guides written since. Then we gave each tip a stamp.

What the stamps mean
STILL WORKSAs true now as it was in the video
CHANGEDStill useful, but something is different now
GONEThe item or trick has left the game, for now

Here's a spoiler: eleven of the fifteen tips still work, two have changed, and two are gone. And even the "gone" tips teach you something, because the thinking behind them still works with whatever is in the game today.

Zero Build in thirty seconds

If you mostly play with building switched on, here's what's different in Zero Build:

  • No walls, no ramps. When someone shoots at you, you can't build a wall. You need real cover: rocks, trees, buildings.
  • More shield. To make up for it, everyone gets an extra Overshield on top of their normal health and shield. It recharges by itself if you stay out of trouble for a few seconds.
  • Pocket items. Every player carries one special item that never runs out. It just needs time to recharge between uses. (Tip 8 is all about choosing the right one.)
  • Mobility is king. Without ramps, the player who can move fastest usually wins. That's why so many of Jake's tips are about getting around.

How to read this book

  • Each tip starts with a card showing its number, the item, and its stamp. Underneath: what Jake said, why it works, and a Then and now box explaining what's changed.
  • Quotes are things Jake actually says in the video, lightly tidied up for reading.
  • Word Watch boxes explain the trickier words. They're all collected at the back, in A Splash of Words.
  • The quiz at the back has one question for every tip.

This book covers Chapter 7, Season 4: Override, after the update of 15 October 2026 (version 42.40). The season is due to end on 1 November, Australian time, and the next one will change things again. If the game doesn't match the book, the game has moved on.
