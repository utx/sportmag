A Chug Splash is the quickest heal in Fortnite. You don't sip it or wait for a progress bar to fill: you lob it, it bursts, and everyone in the splash is topped up in an instant. This book is designed to work the same way. Fifteen tips, each short enough to read while your squad is still arguing about where to land, and each one packed with something you can use in your very next match.

Every tip is about Zero Build, the mode where nobody can build a single wall, ramp or roof, so every tip is about moving smartly, healing efficiently and finding the best loot before anyone else does. And every tip is for this season: Chapter 7, Season 4, which Epic Games has called Override.

The briefing: Zero Build in two minutes

If you usually play with building switched on, Zero Build can feel strange at first. You hear gunfire, your thumb reaches for the wall button, and… nothing happens. Here is what's different, and why it matters for every tip in this book.

No walls, no ramps

In Build mode, the instant someone shoots at you, you can throw up a wall, then another, then a ramp, and suddenly you're safe in a little wooden tower. In Zero Build, you can't. When you're caught in the open, you are vulnerable, and the only cover is whatever the Island already provides: rocks, trees, buildings, hills, cars and the occasional giant pumpkin. Learning to read the land like a map of hiding places is the most important Zero Build skill there is.

More shield to make up for it

To compensate for the missing walls, every Zero Build player gets a bonus layer of protection called Overshield: 50 extra points that sit on top of your normal 100 health and 100 shield. Better still, it recharges by itself if you avoid taking damage for a few seconds. That one rule changes how fights work. If you can break away from a fight, hide for a moment and let your Overshield regenerate, you can come back stronger than you left.

A pocket item that never runs out

Every Zero Build player carries one special pocket item in its own slot. Use it, wait for it to recharge, and use it again, all match long. Choosing the right one is the subject of Tip 8.

Mobility is king

Without ramps, the player who can move fastest usually wins: fastest to the loot, fastest out of the storm, fastest into a fight while the enemy is still reloading. That's why a third of this book is about getting around.

A season that keeps changing

Fortnite never stands still. Every couple of weeks, Epic releases an update, a fresh revision of the game. Weapons are added and taken away (Fortnite calls taking something out of the game vaulting it), prizes change, bugs are fixed, and occasionally a boss packs up and leaves the Island altogether. Here is the season so far, in chronological order:

Because of all these changes, some tips work slightly differently now than they did on launch day. Whenever that's the case, you'll find a Since launch day box explaining exactly what changed and when, so you'll know more than most players about how the season has developed.
