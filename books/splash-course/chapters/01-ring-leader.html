Chapter 1
Heal fast. Come back stronger.

In Zero Build you can't hide behind a wall while you heal, so every free bit of health counts. Jake's first three tips are all about staying alive: one gives you free shield, one drops loot from the sky, and one gives you a whole extra life.

TIP1
Run through the Rings
Green Hill Zone
STILL WORKS

Green Hill Zone is the huge, grassy area borrowed from the Sonic the Hedgehog games, with loop-the-loops, chequered cliffs and, just like in Sonic, golden Rings floating everywhere.

Run through a Ring and two things happen. First, it heals you a little: health if you're hurt, shield if your health is full. Second, it gives you one extra point of Overshield, and it raises the most Overshield you can have by one as well. "That doesn't sound like a lot," Jake admits, "but it can add up somewhat quick." Ring after Ring, the bonus accumulates, all the way up to 50 extra.

Break your Overshield and the bonus resets. Rings grow back about 30 seconds after someone collects them.

Jake's advice is simple. If you land at Green Hill Zone, collect Rings to heal up quickly. If you're just passing through and you see some, grab them. And here's the clever bit: in his matches, players who had collected Rings dropped some of them when they took damage, just like Sonic does. Win a fight quickly and you can scoop up your opponent's Rings too.

Chug Jug maths: 50 Rings for +50 Overshield is one Overshield point per Ring. If you collect 50 Rings, then lose your Overshield in a fight, how many Rings do you need to get back to +50? (Answer: all 50 again. That's why it pays to stay out of trouble while it's full.)

Then and now

Still works. The Rings are still at Green Hill Zone and still stack up your Overshield. One thing has changed nearby: the boss who used to guard Green Hill Zone has left (more on him in Tip 10), so it's a little less dangerous than it was in Jake's video.

TIP2
Call in a llama
Llama Supply Drop
STILL WORKS

Every so often, a Llama Supply Drop floats down from the sky: a purple loot llama hanging under a balloon. Jake says they're packed with "high rarity loot", which means the good stuff: gold and purple weapons, healing, ammo and Gold Bars. Shoot the balloon and it comes down faster.

Even better, you don't have to wait for one. The Llama Supply Drop is also a gizmo: an item you carry and use whenever you like, which calls a llama down right where you're standing. Use it when you need to replenish your loot after a big fight.

And there's one more reason to love them, which leads straight to Tip 3: the llama can contain a 1-Up Token.

Where to get one: complete three Daily Quests on a Thursday, or buy one at an Extraction Site with Gold Bars. There's also a free one in a lobby code: see Tip 4.

Then and now

Still works. Llama Supply Drops are still in the game, and still one of the best ways to find a 1-Up Token.

TIP3
Never pass up a 1-Up
1-Up Token
STILL WORKS

Jake calls this one "an absolute game-changer", and he's right. Carry a 1-Up Token and, when you're eliminated, you aren't out of the match. You respawn, and you come back with your loot: your whole inventory, weapons and all.

That last part is what makes it special. Earlier this year, in Chapter 7 Season 1, Fortnite had a different respawn item that brought you back with nothing except the basic weapon everyone starts with. As Jake remembers, "it was kind of a struggle to make things work." With the 1-Up, you're back in the fight straight away, fully armed, so "it's a lot more likely that you'll be able to make something happen."

His rule: "Any time you see a 1-Up coin, you're going to want to get it." And the best place he found them? Llama Supply Drops.
