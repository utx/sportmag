Chapter 1
Heal fast. Come back stronger.

In Build mode, staying alive is partly about walls. In Zero Build, it's about resources: how much health, shield and Overshield you have, how quickly you can top them up, and whether you have a back-up plan if everything goes wrong. The three tips in this chapter cover all three. One gives you free shield that keeps on stacking, one drops a parcel of premium loot from the sky, and one gives you something even better than a heal: an entire extra life.

TIP1
Run through the Rings
Green Hill Zone
EARLY GAME

Green Hill Zone is one of the largest named locations Fortnite has ever built: a sprawling landscape of grassy hills, chequered cliffs, loop-the-loops and Boost Pads, borrowed from the Sonic the Hedgehog games. And, exactly as in Sonic, there are golden Rings floating everywhere.

Run through a Ring and two useful things happen at once. First, it heals you slightly: it restores health if you're injured, or shield if your health is already full. Second, and far more importantly, it adds one point of Overshield and raises the maximum Overshield you're allowed to have by one point as well. One point sounds trivial. But Rings come in long, glittering lines, and the bonus is cumulative: Ring after Ring, it accumulates, until it reaches a threshold of 50 extra. Remember that Zero Build already gives you 50 Overshield. A dedicated Ring collector can double it.

Break your Overshield and the bonus resets. Rings grow back about 30 seconds after someone collects them.

How to use the Rings

  • Land and collect. If you drop into Green Hill Zone, make Rings part of your looting route. Grab a weapon first (always a weapon first), then sweep through the nearest line of Rings to heal and build your bonus.
  • Never walk past them. Crossing Green Hill Zone mid-match? Detour through any Rings you see. Ten seconds of collecting can be worth more than a Shield Potion.
  • Look after your bonus. Here's the catch: if your Overshield is broken, the bonus resets, and you'll need to collect the Rings all over again. So once you've built up a big bonus, play a little more cautiously. Fight from cover, and back away to let your Overshield regenerate before it breaks completely.
  • Scavenge after a fight. Players who have collected Rings can spill some when they take damage, just like Sonic does when he's hit. Win a fight quickly and you may be able to scoop up your opponent's Rings as well as their loot.
Chug Jug maths: 50 Rings for +50 Overshield is a rate of one point per Ring. If you collect 50 Rings and then your Overshield breaks, how many do you need to collect to get back to +50? (All 50 again. That's why it pays to protect a full bonus.)

Since launch day

The Rings work exactly as they did on the first day of the season. One thing has changed nearby, though: Green Hill Zone used to be guarded by a boss, Dr. Eggman, who left the Island in the Fortnitemares update (there's more about him in Tip 10). That makes the area a little less dangerous than it was in August.

TIP2
Call in a llama
Llama Supply Drop
ANY TIME

Every so often during a match, a purple loot llama drifts down from the sky, dangling beneath a hot-air balloon. This is a Llama Supply Drop, and it is stuffed with high-rarity loot: purple and gold weapons, healing items, ammunition and Gold Bars. If you spot one descending nearby, it's worth a detour. Shoot the balloon and it will plummet to the ground faster, which means you reach it before your rivals do.

Even better, you don't have to wait for the sky to deliver one. The Llama Supply Drop also exists as a gizmo, an item you carry in your inventory and use whenever you like. Activate it and a llama is summoned to the exact spot where you're standing. It has a cooldown of five minutes, so you can't summon an endless parade of llamas, but one well-timed llama can transform a match.

When should you use it? The best moment is just after a big fight, when you've used up your heals and your ammunition is running low. A llama replenishes almost everything at once. Just make sure the area is clear first: a descending llama is visible from a long way off, and other players know exactly what's inside.

Where to get the gizmo: complete three Daily Quests on a Thursday, or buy one at an Extraction Site with Gold Bars. There's also a free one hidden in a lobby code: see Tip 4.

Beyond the Island: real llamas

Fortnite's loot llama is a piñata, but real llamas have been working for people for thousands of years. They were domesticated in the Andes mountains of South America, and the Inca Empire relied on enormous trains of llamas to carry goods along mountain roads too steep and narrow for carts. A healthy llama can carry roughly a quarter of its own body weight. Overload one and it will simply lie down and refuse to move, and if it's really annoyed, it may spit. (Llamas mostly spit at each other, though, not at people.)

So a llama that delivers supplies isn't such a strange idea after all. Fortnite just added a balloon.

Since launch day

No changes: Llama Supply Drops work exactly as they did in August, and they're still one of the best places to find the item in Tip 3.

TIP3
Never pass up a 1-Up
1-Up Token
ANY TIME

If you remember only one tip from this chapter, make it this one. Carry a 1-Up Token and, when you're eliminated, you aren't out of the match. You come back through a rift, and you come back with your entire inventory: every weapon, every heal, every bullet. The token doesn't even take up an inventory slot, so there's no reason ever to leave one behind.

That last detail, keeping your loot, is what makes the 1-Up such a game-changer. Earlier this year, in Chapter 7 Season 1, Fortnite had a different respawn item, and it brought you back with nothing except the basic weapon everyone starts with. In theory that was a second chance. In practice it meant reappearing, almost defenceless, with the person who had just eliminated you still nearby. With a 1-Up, you're straight back into the action, fully armed, with a genuine chance of turning the fight around.

The most reliable source of 1-Up Tokens is the Llama Supply Drop from Tip 2, which is why those two tips belong together. Summon a llama, open it, and there's a chance a 1-Up is waiting inside. Some special events during the season have also hidden them in ordinary chests.

Chug Jug tip: A 1-Up is a contingency, not a licence to be reckless. Once it's used, it's gone. The best players fight as if they don't have one, and treat the token as a pleasant surprise when things go wrong.

Beyond the Island: where "1-Up" comes from

The name is a little piece of video-game history. On old arcade machines, "1UP" was printed above the first player's score, and "2UP" above the second player's. Later, games began to use "1-Up" to mean one extra life, and the Super Mario games made it famous with a green mushroom that gives Mario another go. So when Fortnite's token says 1-Up, it's paying tribute to forty years of gaming.

Since launch day

No changes. The 1-Up Token works exactly as it did on launch day, and it's still one of the most valuable items on the Island.
