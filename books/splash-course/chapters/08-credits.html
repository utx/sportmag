About this book

Splash Course is an unofficial fan book made by The Chug Jug, a fan magazine about Fortnite. It's free, and it will never ask you to spend money. If you ever want to buy anything in Fortnite, ask a grown-up first.

It isn't made, approved or endorsed by Epic Games.

Version

This book describes Fortnite Battle Royale (Zero Build) in Chapter 7, Season 4: Override, after the update of 15 October 2026 (version 42.40). Every tip was checked on 19 October 2026. The season is due to end on 1 November 2026 (Australian time), so some details will change after that.

Pictures

Item and weapon pictures, the Island map, the location close-ups and the lobby background are by Epic Games / Fortnite, used under Epic's Fan Content Policy, via the Fortnite Wiki and fortnite-api.com. The tip cards, charts, step boards, the Cheat Code drawing, the Ring, the map markers and the cover design were made for this book by The Chug Jug.

We haven't shown official pictures of characters or items that belong to other companies: Sonic the Hedgehog and his Rings, Power Sneakers and Shadow Sprite (from SEGA's Sonic games), Dr. Eggman, and the Mega Buster (from Capcom's Mega Man games). We mention them by name only.

Where the facts came from

Item details, numbers and what changed when: Epic Games' official update notes for version 42.00 (20 August 2026); the Fortnite Wiki pages for Rings, the Llama Supply Drop, Lobby Hacks, Loot Hacks, Cheat Codes, Pocket Items, the Pocket Crash Pad Jr., the Mega Buster, the Sonic Power Sneakers, the Shadow Sprite, the Overdrive Grenade, the Bass Boost, the Chug Splash, the Oni Shotgun, the Midas Flopper, Stone Sanctum and Heatwave Harbor; and our earlier books Tier Pressure and The Sprite Whisperer.

Tips and tactics: our own research, cross-checked against guides from Game Rant, Games.gg, GosuGamers, AllThingsHow and The Spike, and YouTube creator guides including Evolve Jake's "15 Tips Every Fortnite Player Needs To Know In Chapter 7 Season 4".

The 15 October update (Mega Buster fix, new Loot Hacks): Fragster, "Fortnite v42.40 Adds New Sprites and Loot Hacks". Only one detailed report was available when we checked, so treat the Mega Buster fix as reported rather than confirmed.

Beyond the Island: the word meanings are from standard German and French dictionaries; the llama, Konami Code, Basilica Cistern, duck derby, reaction-time, football-scanning and King Midas facts are widely documented in encyclopedias and in published research on scanning in football.

Play it safe

  • Nobody is giving away free V-Bucks. If a website, video or message offers them, it's a scam.
  • Lobby codes go into the Admin Panel inside Fortnite. A real code never needs your password.
  • Never share your password or your account with anyone, not even a friend.
  • This book never recommends cheats, hacks or glitches. ("Lobby Hacks", "Loot Hacks" and "Cheat Codes" in this book are official features of the game.)
  • Take breaks. The Rings will still be there after dinner.
