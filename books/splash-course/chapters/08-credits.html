About this book

Splash Course is an unofficial fan book made by The Chug Jug, a fan magazine about Fortnite. It turns a YouTube video, 15 Tips Every Fortnite Player Needs To Know In Chapter 7 Season 4 (Fortnite Zero Build Tips) by Evolve Jake, into a book. The tips and the quotes are his. The explanations, the "Then and now" checks, the Word Watch boxes, the quiz and the graphics were added for this book. It isn't written, approved or endorsed by Evolve Jake.

It isn't made, approved or endorsed by Epic Games either. It's free, and it will never ask you to spend money. If you ever want to buy anything in Fortnite, ask a grown-up first.

Version

This book describes Fortnite Battle Royale (Zero Build) in Chapter 7, Season 4: Override, after the update of 15 October 2026 (version 42.40). Every tip was checked on 19 October 2026. The video was recorded in late August 2026, in the first days of the season. The season is due to end on 1 November 2026 (Australian time), so some details will change after that.

Item and weapon pictures, the Island map, the location close-ups and the lobby background are by Epic Games / Fortnite, used under Epic's Fan Content Policy, via the Fortnite Wiki and fortnite-api.com. The tips and quotes: Evolve Jake's video. Quotes are what he says in the video, lightly tidied up for reading.

Item details, numbers and what changed when: Epic Games' official update notes for version 42.00 (20 August 2026); the Fortnite Wiki pages for Rings, the Llama Supply Drop, Lobby Hacks, Loot Hacks, Cheat Codes, the Pocket Crash Pad Jr., the Mega Buster, the Sonic Power Sneakers, the Shadow Sprite, the Bass Boost, the Chug Splash, the Oni Shotgun, the Midas Flopper, Stone Sanctum and Heatwave Harbor; and our earlier books Tier Pressure and The Sprite Whisperer.

Lobby codes: the Fortnite Wiki and GosuGamers, "All Fortnite Lobby Hack codes and free Admin Panel rewards in Chapter 7 Season 4".

Loot Hacks: Games.gg, "How to Unlock Loot Hacks"; Game Rant's Chapter 7 Season 4 tier list (24 August 2026); the Fortnite Wiki.

The 15 October update (Mega Buster fix, new Loot Hacks): Fragster, "Fortnite v42.40 Adds New Sprites and Loot Hacks". Only one detailed report was available when we checked, so treat the Mega Buster fix as reported, not confirmed by us.

Cheat Code colours: the Fortnite Wiki, and guides from AllThingsHow and The Spike.

The word meanings in "Words from around the world" are from standard German and French dictionaries.

Play it safe

  • Nobody is giving away free V-Bucks. If a website, video or message offers them, it's a scam.
  • Lobby codes go into the Admin Panel inside Fortnite. A real code never needs your password.
  • Never share your password or your account with anyone, not even a friend.
  • This book never recommends cheats, hacks or glitches. ("Lobby Hacks", "Loot Hacks" and "Cheat Codes" in this book are official features of the game.)
  • Take breaks. The Rings will still be there after dinner.
