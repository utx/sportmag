Chapter 3
No ramps? No problem.

Picture this. You've landed, opened every chest in the building, and found a great shotgun, some heals… and nothing at all to help you move. "One thing that is always an unfortunate circumstance in Zero Build," says Jake, "is when you loot your initial POI and you have no mobility." In Build mode you could build a ramp. In Zero Build, you're stuck on foot while the storm closes in.

These four tips are all about getting around: two you can use today, one that's changed, and one that's left the Island.

TIP8
Swap to Crash Pads
Your pocket item
STILL WORKS

In Zero Build, everybody carries one pocket item that never runs out. You use it, it goes on cooldown, and then it comes back. Most players carry the Pocket Shockwave Grenade, which blasts you through the air. It's brilliant, but you only get one at a time, and you wait a whole minute for the first recharge.

Jake's tip: if you've looted your first place and found no other mobility, swap your Shockwave for the Pocket Crash Pad Jr., a little bounce pad you throw down and jump on. You can swap at the special pocket-item vending machines around the Island.

Shockwave
  • 1 charge
  • First recharge: 60 seconds, then 40
  • Flings you a long way
  • Loud
VS
Crash Pad Jr.
  • 2 charges
  • Each one recharges in 30 seconds
  • Bounces you high
  • Almost silent

Two pads instead of one, and a faster recharge, means you can use them far more often. And when Jake made the swap, it cost him "zero gold". If you find a better mobility item later, or you just prefer the Shockwave, you can always swap back.

Jake also spotted two sneaky bonus tricks:

  • They're quiet. "The crash pads don't make really any audio at all," so you can bounce in on a fight that two other players are having without them hearing you coming. Very stealthy.
  • Shoot your pad when you escape. If you're running away, "it's a good habit to look back and shoot the crash pad. That way an enemy doesn't follow you." A pad deflates when it's shot, so nobody can bounce after you.

His verdict: the Crash Pad pocket item is "definitely underrated".

Then and now

Still works. Both pocket items are still in Zero Build, with the same charges. One small thing to watch: the vending machines can ask for Gold Bars to swap, so check the price on the button before you press it.

Charge the Mega Buster
Mega Man's arm cannon
STILL WORKSbug now fixed

The Mega Buster is the famous arm cannon from the Mega Man games. (It belongs to another company, so there's no picture.) Tap the trigger and it fires little energy shots. But hold the trigger down for two seconds and it charges up a big blast that explodes when it lands, hurting anyone nearby. Jake says it's "really good for forcing people off of cover or out of bushes."

And it has a secret second power: a boosted slide. While you're holding the Mega Buster, slide and you'll zoom forward in a dash, floating with low gravity. That makes it a mobility item and a weapon at the same time.

There's one catch, and Jake explains it carefully. You get two slides, and they only recharge while you're holding the Mega Buster. So if you use both slides to run somewhere, then switch to your shotgun straight away, your slides won't be ready when the fight starts. His advice: if you're planning to use the slides in a fight, "make sure you always have those two slides ready to go," and don't swap away from the Mega Buster while they're empty.

Then and now

Still works, after a hiccup. When the Fortnitemares update arrived on 1 October, a bug stopped the charged blast from hurting players nearby, which ruined Jake's favourite trick. Reports of the 15 October update say the blast has been fixed and works properly again.

Shoot out of a Spin Dash
Sonic Power Sneakers
CHANGED

The Sonic Power Sneakers are Sonic the Hedgehog's famous shoes. Use them and you curl up into a spinning ball and bounce around, just like Sonic. You can roll along the ground in a Spin Dash, or launch yourself at an enemy with a Spin Attack.

Here's Jake's tip. When you want to stop bouncing, swap to one of your weapons. Your character does a little animation as they uncurl, and it looks like you have to wait for it to finish. You don't. "You can just start shooting immediately," says Jake. So if you roll up to someone in a ball, swap to your shotgun and fire straight away. That split second is often the difference between winning and losing a fight.

Then and now

Changed. In Jake's video, there was also a gold Mythic version of the sneakers, which made you sprint like Sonic. It was carried by Dr. Eggman, Sonic's arch-enemy, who roamed the Island as a boss (and, Jake noticed, didn't always drop them). In the Fortnitemares update, Dr. Eggman left the Island, and the Mythic sneakers went with him. His crashed hovercraft is still there, smoking in a crater at Green Hill Zone. The normal sneakers are still around, and the shoot-straight-away trick still works.

TIP11
Crouch and blast cover
Bass Boost
GONE

The Bass Boost is a pair of gloves that fire a blast of sound. Point them down and they launch you into the air. Point them at a wall, a rock or a bush and they smash it to pieces, which is perfect for destroying the cover an enemy is hiding behind.

The trouble is that the blast also pushes you backwards. Jake's fix: crouch first. "If you shoot the Bass Boost gloves while crouched, you won't actually send yourself anywhere." You stay put, and their cover disappears.

Then and now

Gone. The Bass Boost was a Loot Hack in launch week (see Tip 5) and was vaulted on 3 September. It could come back in a future season; Fortnite brings items back all the time.

What still works: the big idea. In Zero Build, cover is everything, so anything that breaks cover or pushes people out of it is powerful. Right now, the fully charged Mega Buster (Tip 9) does that job.
