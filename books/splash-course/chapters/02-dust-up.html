Chapter 2
Hack the lobby. Crack the code.

This season's storyline is all about hacking. The villain, Geno, has built a colossal machine in the centre of the Island to rewrite reality itself, and Epic has woven that idea into the game in a clever way: there are genuine "hacks" hidden throughout Fortnite for players to use. None of them are cheating. They're legitimate features, built in on purpose. The four tips in this chapter show you how to exploit them, beginning before you've even boarded the Battle Bus.

TIP4
Start with 8,000 Sprite Dust

Sprite Dust is this season's special currency. You earn it gradually by playing, and you spend it on summoning Sprites and on Loot Hacks (which are explained in Tip 5). Earning it the slow way takes a long time. Fortunately, there's a shortcut.

In the Battle Royale lobby there's a button called the Admin Panel. Type in a secret code and the game rewards you with a prize: XP, items, or Sprite Dust. Plenty of codes give Sprite Dust, but the best place to start is with these four, which were released on the very first day of the season:

Each code can be redeemed once per account. Four codes multiplied by 2,000 Sprite Dust each makes 8,000, which is enough to unlock and upgrade several Loot Hacks before you've played a single match. Since the start of the season, Epic has released more codes, including DustInTheWind and DustySprites, which are worth 5,000 each. Add those and you're up to 18,000.

Beyond the Island: words from around the world

Why do the codes look so peculiar? Each of the four was posted by Fortnite's team in a different country, in a different language: Magilume by Fortnite Brazil, Chispambo by Fortnite Spain, Abgestaubt by Fortnite Germany and Perlimpinpin by Fortnite France. Investigate their etymology and you discover that two of them are real words, and both are about dust:

  • Abgestaubt is German for "dusted", from the verb abstauben, to dust. (In casual German it can also mean "grabbed" or "snaffled", which is exactly what you're doing with the Sprite Dust.)
  • Perlimpinpin comes from the old French phrase poudre de perlimpinpin: a pretend magic powder that travelling tricksters once sold as a cure for every illness. Today, French speakers use the phrase to describe a remedy that doesn't really work.

Magilume and Chispambo look more like invented magic words. Can you spot any clues hidden inside them?

Play it safe: Only use codes from Epic's own Fortnite accounts or a guide a grown-up trusts. A real code goes into the Admin Panel inside the game, and it never, ever needs your password. And remember: nobody is giving away free V-Bucks.

Since launch day

These codes were still on the published code lists in October. If you've already used one, the Admin Panel will tell you, and you can move on to the newer ones.

TIP5
Spend dust on Loot Hacks
The Override menu
LOBBYlist changes

So what should you do with 8,000 Sprite Dust? The smartest investment is Loot Hacks. In the lobby, open the Override section of the menu and choose Loot Hacks. You'll see a short list of items. Spend Sprite Dust on one and it gets "hacked" into your games: from then on, it has a chance of appearing in your chests, and nobody else's. Spend more to upgrade it, and it turns up more frequently and in better rarities.

Here's the detail that makes Loot Hacks so valuable: players have noticed that a hacked item appears in addition to the normal contents of a chest, rather than replacing something. So a Loot Hack never costs you loot. It's a pure bonus.

How to choose: the three questions

Every big update replaces the whole Loot Hack list with a new set of items, so there's no point memorising one perfect choice. Instead, learn how to evaluate whatever is on offer. Ask three questions about each item:

  1. Does it give me extra healing? A heal that turns up on top of a chest's normal loot means you can recover from your first fight faster than anyone else.
  2. Does it help me move? In Zero Build, a mobility item is worth its weight in Gold Bars.
  3. Does it combine with my favourite Sprite? Some items become far more powerful when paired with the right Sprite. A shotgun that only holds two shells is awkward on its own, but pair it with a Sprite that reloads your weapons for you and it becomes formidable.

Find an item that ticks one of those boxes, and you've found a good Loot Hack. Find one that ticks two, and you've found a great one.

TIP6
Read Cheat Codes the right way up
All over the Island
EARLY GAME

Cheat Codes are glowing panels concealed all over the map, and each one is secretly a Sprite chest. Walk up to one and it displays a short sequence of button arrows. Enter the arrows in exactly the right order and the code unlocks, rewarding you with a Sprite and 1,000 XP.

Plenty of players grumble that some codes are "backwards": they copy precisely what they see, and nothing happens. The explanation is surprisingly simple. They're standing on the wrong side. Every Cheat Code has a solid line, and the line is meant to sit underneath the arrows. If the line is above the arrows, you're viewing the code upside down, so the whole sequence is flipped and reversed. Walk round to the opposite side, check that the line is underneath, and try again.

Right way up
  • Line under the arrows
  • Copy what you see
VS
Wrong side
  • Line above the arrows
  • Walk round the other side

This problem mostly affects the Cheat Codes lying flat on the ground, because you can approach them from any direction. Codes mounted on walls are almost always the right way up already. It's all a question of orientation.

Beyond the Island: the most famous cheat code ever

Cheat codes are almost as old as video games. The most celebrated of all is the Konami Code: up, up, down, down, left, right, left, right, B, A. It first appeared in 1986, in a game called Gradius, after a programmer added it so that he could test the game without losing all the time. In the game Contra, it gave players thirty lives instead of three. Since then, game designers have hidden it in hundreds of games and even on websites as a joke for those in the know. Fortnite's Cheat Codes are the latest chapter in a forty-year tradition.

Since launch day

The line trick and the colours work exactly as they did in August. Since 17 September there has also been an even rarer kind of code: Mythic, the most valuable of all.

Sprites you don't have yet
ANY TIME

This one is quick, but dedicated collectors will love it. Any Sprite that you've never collected shows a small yellow exclamation mark when you see it during a match. No exclamation mark? Then that Sprite is already safely in your collection.

That tiny indicator saves an enormous amount of time. Without it, you'd have to remember every Sprite you've ever found, or open your collection screen between matches to check. With it, you can tell at a glance which Sprites are worth chasing across the map and which you can leave for somebody else. For anyone attempting to complete the entire collection, it's essential.

Chug Jug tip: Remember that a Sprite isn't really yours until you've extracted it, by taking it to an Extraction Site or using a Portable Extractor. If you spot a yellow "!", catch it, then make extracting it your top priority before anyone can eliminate you.

Since launch day

The exclamation mark arrived with the season and hasn't changed. It's more useful than ever now, because Fortnitemares has added a whole set of spooky new Sprite variants to collect.
