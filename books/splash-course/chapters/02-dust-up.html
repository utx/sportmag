Chapter 2
Hack the lobby. Crack the code.

This season's story is all about hacking the Island, and Epic has hidden real "hacks" all over the game for players to use. None of them are cheating. They're official features, built in on purpose. Jake's next four tips show you how to use them, starting before you've even left the lobby.

TIP4
Start with 8,000 Sprite Dust
The Admin Panel, in the lobby
STILL WORKS

Sprite Dust is this season's special currency. You earn it by playing, and you spend it on Sprites and on Loot Hacks (Tip 5). But Jake found a shortcut.

In the Battle Royale lobby there's a button called the Admin Panel. Type in a secret code and it gives you a prize. Lots of codes give Sprite Dust, and Jake's tip is to redeem these four first:

Type these into the Admin Panel
1MAGILUME2,000
2CHISPAMBO2,000
3ABGESTAUBT2,000
4PERLIMPINPIN2,000
=TOTAL8,000 DUST

Each code works once per account. Four codes times 2,000 Sprite Dust is 8,000, which, as Jake puts it, is "going to be helpful". Since his video, Epic has shared more codes, including DustInTheWind and DustySprites, which are worth 5,000 each.

Words from around the world

Why do the codes look so strange? Each of Jake's four was posted by Fortnite's team in a different country, in a different language: Magilume by Fortnite Brazil, Chispambo by Fortnite Spain, Abgestaubt by Fortnite Germany and Perlimpinpin by Fortnite France. Two of them are real words, and both are about dust:

  • Abgestaubt is German for "dusted", from abstauben, to dust.
  • Perlimpinpin comes from the French phrase poudre de perlimpinpin, a pretend magic powder that tricksters used to sell as a cure for everything.

Magilume and Chispambo look more like made-up magic words. Can you guess what they might mean?

Play it safe: Only use codes from Epic's own Fortnite accounts or a guide a grown-up trusts. A real code never needs your password, and nobody is giving away free V-Bucks.

Then and now

Still works. These codes were still on the code lists in October. If you've already used one, the Admin Panel will tell you, and you can move on to the newer codes.

TIP5
Spend dust on Loot Hacks
The Override menu
GONEbut the idea works

What should you do with all that dust? Jake's answer: Loot Hacks. In the lobby, open the Override section of the menu and go to Loot Hacks. There you'll see a short list of items. Spend Sprite Dust on one and it gets "hacked" into your games: from then on, it has a chance of appearing in your chests. Spend more to upgrade it, and it turns up more often and in better rarities.

Here's what Jake noticed: Loot Hack items seem to appear as well as the normal loot in a chest, not instead of it. "It doesn't take the place of anything else," he says. So a Loot Hack is pure bonus.

  • Chug Splash. Throw it and it heals everyone in the splash. If a Chug Splash pops out of a chest at the start of a match, especially on top of whatever healing the chest was already going to give you, you can heal up fast and win your first fight. (Careful: it heals anyone in the splash, including enemies.)
  • Bass Boost. A pair of glowing gloves that blast you into the air: "a really good mobility item in a season with not the most spammable mobility." More about it in Tip 11.
  • Oni Shotgun. A shotgun with only two shots before you have to reload. Jake paired it with the Shadow Sprite (a guest Sprite from the Sonic games), which reloads your weapons for you while you aren't holding them. Two big shots, then swap to another weapon while the Sprite quietly reloads the shotgun.

Then and now

Gone. Loot Hacks rotate: every big update swaps the whole list for a new one, and you get back the Sprite Dust you spent on the old one, so you never lose it. Jake's three picks were in the launch-week list, which was replaced on 3 September. Chug Splash and Bass Boost were taken out of normal matches at the same time. The newest list arrived on 15 October, so check the Loot Hacks tab to see what's in it now.

The good news: Jake's way of choosing still works on any list. Ask three questions:

  1. Does it give me extra healing on top of what's in my chests?
  2. Does it help me move? (In Zero Build, that's huge.)
  3. Does it combo with my favourite Sprite?

Find an item that ticks one of those boxes, and you've found a good Loot Hack.

TIP6
Read Cheat Codes the right way up
All over the Island
STILL WORKS

Cheat Codes are glowing panels hidden around the map, and each one is really a Sprite chest. Walk up to one and it shows a row of button arrows. Copy them in the right order and it opens, giving you a Sprite and 1,000 XP.

Some players complain that the codes are "backwards": they type in exactly what they see, and it doesn't work. Jake has the answer. "You're likely on the wrong side," he says. The secret is the line. Each code has a line, and "the line is supposed to be underneath the buttons." If the line is above the arrows, you're looking at the code upside down, so everything is flipped. Walk round to the other side and try again.

Right way up
  • Line under the arrows
  • Copy what you see
VS
Wrong side
  • Line above the arrows
  • Walk round the other side

Jake says this mostly happens with the Cheat Codes that lie flat on the ground, because you can walk up to them from any direction. Codes on walls are usually the right way up already. It's all about orientation.

The colour tells you the prize

The colour of a Cheat Code shows the rarity of the Sprite inside:

Code colourSprite you'll usually get
BlueA Rare Sprite
PurpleAn Epic Sprite
GoldA Legendary Sprite

Usually the Sprite matches the colour, but every now and then you get lucky and it's even rarer. So if you see a gold code, go and get it.

Then and now

Still works. The line trick and the colours are the same as in Jake's video. Since 17 September there's an even rarer kind of code, too: Mythic, the rarest of all.

TIP7
!
Spot the yellow "!"
Sprites you don't have yet
STILL WORKS

This one's quick, but collectors will love it. Since Season 4 began, a Sprite you've never collected shows a little yellow exclamation mark when you see it in a match. No exclamation mark? You've already got that one in your collection.

That small indicator saves you a lot of time. If you're trying to complete your collection, you know straight away which Sprites are worth chasing and which you can leave behind.

Then and now

Still works. Epic added this when the season began, and it's still there. It's even more useful now, because Fortnitemares has added lots of new spooky Sprite variants to collect.
