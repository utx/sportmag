Places: Stone Sanctum, Lifty Lodge, Heatwave Harbor, Freaky Fields.
Sprites: Spooky Dash, Vampire, Dumpster Dive, Honey.

  1. Peely landed at Lifty Lodge.
  2. Whoever landed at Heatwave Harbor found the Vampire Sprite.
  3. Jonesy didn't land at Stone Sanctum or Heatwave Harbor.

  5. Whoever landed at Freaky Fields did not find Dumpster Dive.
