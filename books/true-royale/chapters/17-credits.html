About this book

True Royale is an unofficial fan book made by The Chug Jug, a fan magazine about Fortnite. It isn't made, approved or endorsed by Epic Games. It's free, and it will never ask you to spend money. If you ever want to buy anything in Fortnite, ask a grown-up first.

Portions of the materials used are trademarks and/or copyrighted works of Epic Games, Inc. All rights reserved by Epic. This material is not official and is not endorsed by Epic.

Version

This book covers Fortnite Battle Royale in Chapter 7, Season 4: Override, with the Fortnitemares update (version 42.30) on the Island, up to 14 October 2026. Update 42.40 was due on 15 October 2026, and some items, Overrides and numbers may have changed since.

True stories and Squad Tales

Every chapter marked True Story or History File describes real events, checked against the sources listed in its Check the tape box. Where sources disagreed, or something couldn't be confirmed, we've said so in the text. When we describe a creator's video, we've used its title, its description, its chapter markers and what was published about it; we haven't printed links, so ask a grown-up before looking any of them up.

The three Squad Tales are fiction. Priya, Mo, Tilly and Jacko are made-up characters, and their matches never happened. The game mechanics in them (items, Sprites, Overrides, timings and numbers) are real, as described in each tale's Real or made up? box.

Play it safe

  • Nobody is giving away free V-Bucks. If a website, video or message offers them, it's a scam.
  • Real Lobby Hack codes come from Epic. Never give anyone your password, your account or money for a "code".
  • Never share your password or your account with anyone, not even a friend.
  • This book never recommends cheats, hacks or glitches. ("Cheat Codes" and "Lobby Hacks" are official features of the game.)
  • Take breaks. The Island will still be there after dinner.
