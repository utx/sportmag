The final circle

Eleven players left. Three squads, and somebody on their own.

The storm had squeezed what remained of the match into a small, ragged circle on the edge of Nightmare Neighborhood: a few crooked houses, an overgrown graveyard and a single grassy hill. Priya's squad was crouched behind a crumbling garden wall, and nobody had spoken for a while. Even Jacko was silent, which was how you knew the situation was serious.

And to make everything more difficult, somebody, somewhere, had activated an Override Console twenty minutes earlier and chosen Spooky Sprinter. Ever since, every player in the match had turned almost invisible whenever they sprinted.

Ghosts in the graveyard

"Someone's in the graveyard," said Tilly. "I can hear footsteps. But I can't see anyone."

That was the trouble with Spooky Sprinter. A sprinting player wasn't completely invisible. If you looked carefully you could see a faint, translucent shimmer, like heat rising off a road. But from a distance, in a graveyard full of shadows and headstones, a shimmer was almost impossible to spot.

"Leave it to me," said Priya.

She had been carrying an X-Ray Sprite since early in the match, and it had reached Level 3. Every so often, X-Ray scanned the area around her and marked any enemies it found, and a scan sees straight through invisibility. They waited. Five seconds. Ten.

The scan pulsed. Two red markers appeared in the graveyard, right behind the biggest tomb.

"Two of them," said Priya. "Behind the stone angel."

Tilly had already swapped weapons. During the match she had picked up a Bonerattler SMG, Fortnitemares' spookiest weapon. When you aim down its sights, a purple light shows the skeletons of nearby enemies straight through walls and stones, as long as they're within about 25 metres. She crept to the end of the wall and aimed. Two glowing skeletons appeared behind the tomb, crouched and waiting.

"Got them," she whispered. "They're not moving."

Up, up and away

"They've got the tomb for cover," said Priya. "If we push straight at them, they'll see us coming. We need someone behind them."

Mo grinned. For once, Mo had a plan, and it involved a Witch Broom.

The Witch Broom is one of the most peculiar items on the Island. Use it, and it launches you vertically into the sky, smashing through anything built above you, before popping your glider open so that you can drift wherever you like. Better still, if you press fire while you're in the air, you cancel the glide and drop straight down, and the broom means you take no fall damage when you land.

"I go up," said Mo. "Glide over the graveyard. Drop behind them. You three shoot from the front the moment I land. They'll have to turn round."

Priya thought about it, which Mo had learned meant she was trying to find a reason to say no. She couldn't find one.

"Go," she said. "On three. Jacko, the moment Mo lands, dash forward. Tilly, keep the skeleton-light on them. Everybody coordinate. Three, two…"

Mo shot into the sky.

From the ground it looked utterly ridiculous: a figure rocketing upwards on a broomstick like a character from a fairy tale. Then the glider snapped open, and he drifted silently over the graveyard, high above the tombs, while the two players behind the stone angel kept their eyes fixed on the wall where they knew the squad was hiding. Nobody ever looks up; it's one of the oldest rules of Fortnite, and it proves true almost every single time.

Mo pressed fire. The glider vanished, and he dropped like a stone, straight down behind the tomb, landing without losing a single point of health.

"NOW!"

Jacko jumped and dashed forward through the air with his newly extracted Spooky Dash Sprite, safe in his collection since the Quiet Way Home, now equipped and ready. Tilly opened fire with the Bonerattler. Priya, who had marked both targets, called out exactly where each one was. The other squad had a choice between facing the three players in front of them and the one behind them, and whichever way they turned, they were wrong.

The fight lasted about six seconds.

Victory Royale

After that, the momentum was entirely theirs. The solo player was caught by the storm after rotating too late from the far side of the hill. (Priya muttered something about vic0 and Malibuca.) The last squad tried to rush them, but by then the squad had found the high ground on the hill, healed up behind cover, and had Mo's broom ready for a second launch if they needed it. They didn't.

The words VICTORY ROYALE filled the screen.

For a moment, nobody said anything at all. Then everybody said everything at once, and the noise was exhilarating.

When it had calmed down a little, Jacko opened his Sprite collection. Winning a match does two wonderful things this season. First, it extracts every Sprite you're carrying automatically, so Priya's X-Ray, at Level 3, was saved. And second, it's the only way to earn the rarest trophy of all.

There, glowing gold, was a brand-new Crown Sprite.

"It's a Mythic," said Jacko, very slowly, as if he couldn't quite believe it. "Did I mention…"

"Yes," said everyone.

The broom ambush, from above

Real or made up?

Made up: the squad, the match and the stone angel.

Real: the Spooky Sprinter Override, which cloaks players while they sprint; cloaked players giving off a faint shimmer; X-Ray scans revealing cloaked players; the Bonerattler SMG's skeleton-vision through walls when you aim, up to about 25 metres; the Witch Broom launching you upwards through builds into a glide, with a cancel that drops you down with no fall damage; the Spooky Dash's mid-air dash; storm rotations; a Victory Royale extracting every Sprite you carry; and the Crown Sprite, which you can only earn by winning.

How to pull it off

  1. Information first. Use scans, sounds and skeleton-vision to find out exactly where enemies are before anybody moves.
  2. Attack from two sides. Players behind cover can only face one direction at a time. Make them choose.
  3. Look up, and remember that nobody else does. A Witch Broom launch takes you somewhere your opponents aren't watching.
  4. Cancel the glide to drop fast. With the broom, a quick press of fire drops you straight down without fall damage.
  5. Count down together. A squad that moves at the same moment is far stronger than four players acting alone.
