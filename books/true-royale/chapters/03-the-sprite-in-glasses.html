True story #3
The Sprite in glasses

In June 2026, Epic Games made an offer that sounded almost too good to be true. Anybody could design a Sprite, one of the little creatures that would be at the heart of the next season, and post it online. Epic would pick the best ones, pay each winning designer 2,500 US dollars, and build their creation into Fortnite itself.

The response was overwhelming. In two weeks, fans sent in more than 20,000 designs. Epic had planned to choose three winners, but there were so many brilliant entries that it chose five. And when the winners were announced on 7 August, one of them had a story that made a lot of people smile.

Big eyes, big glasses

According to the submission posted online, the X-Ray Sprite was designed by an eight-year-old. He had been wearing glasses ever since an eye operation when he was five, and he decided that his Sprite should wear glasses too, with big, bright eyes just like his. (We're not printing his name: he's a kid, and this is his story to tell, not ours.)

That alone would have been lovely. But the power he gave it was even better. The X-Ray Sprite helps you see. Every so often, it scans the area around you and reveals nearby enemies, marking them so you can see where they are, even through walls. The higher its level, the more often it scans and the further it reaches. A Sprite designed by a kid in glasses gives every player who equips it super-vision. It was a wonderfully perceptive idea: he noticed something about his own life that made him special, and turned it into a superpower for everyone.

The X-Ray Sprite, designed by a young fan. Picture: Epic Games / Fortnite.

X-Ray arrived on 3 September as a Legendary Sprite, one of the rarer kinds, and it quickly became one of the most respected Sprites on the Island. In Tier Pressure, our book ranking everything in the game, we put it in the very top tier. Knowing where your opponent is before they know where you are is one of the biggest advantages in Fortnite. There's a hidden bonus too: X-Ray's scan can reveal players who are using Cloak, the shimmering invisibility you get from the Blinky Sprite or the Spooky Sprinter Override. In a season full of sneaky invisible players, X-Ray is the counter.

The other winners

Online opinion is never unanimous, and fans argued happily about which winner was best. The other four fan-designed Sprites each arrived in a different update, which meant the contest's winners kept appearing in the game for more than a month:

Onigiri is a Japanese rice ball with a face. Its designer originally won with a different idea, a Sprite called Bullet, but Epic later swapped it for the same artist's rice ball, explaining simply: "so cute!" Eat or drink anything while Onigiri is equipped and you get Overdrive, a burst of unlimited sprinting. Pond lets you Super Jump just after you land, with up to three charges. Dumpster Dive, a raccoon, rewards you with food every time you leap out of a hiding spot, such as a dumpster or a haystack. And Honey, due on 15 October, sends a swarm of bees after anybody who damages you.

Sprites in sixty seconds

If you've been playing this season, you'll know the basics. If not, here's how Sprites work, because they're going to turn up again and again in this book.

You carry one Sprite in a special slot, and it rides on your back in place of your Back Bling, giving you its power. You can carry up to five more in your inventory, but only the equipped one works. Sprites start at Level 1 and grow to Level 5, which is called "Mastered". Every level makes the power stronger. Your equipped Sprite earns XP when you open containers (75 XP each), knock down or eliminate opponents, or crack Cheat Codes, and the XP is shared with your squad.

Total: 4,250 XP. At 75 XP per container, that's about 57 chests, ammo boxes and fridges, if you did nothing else.

Notice something about that chart? Each step is bigger than the last. The final level costs almost as much as the first three put together. That's a deliberate design choice, and it's why a Mastered Sprite is so valuable, which brings us to the most important rule of the season.

The golden rule: extract it or lose it

If you're eliminated, or you leave the match, while carrying Sprites you haven't extracted, you lose them, and your equipped Sprite drops back to Level 1. Worse still, the Sprites you drop keep their levels, so the player who eliminated you can pick them up and extract them for themselves.

Extracting a Sprite saves it to your collection forever. There are three ways to do it: put it in an Extraction Crate at an Extraction Site, use a Portable Extractor, or win the match. We'll look at extraction tricks properly in a Squad Tale later on.

From 8 to 78

Fortnite fans come in every age. At one end, an eight-year-old designed one of the best Sprites in the game. At the other is Cath Bowie, a grandmother from Scotland who turned 78 in September 2026. Guinness World Records named her the oldest woman streaming Fortnite, and she appears in its Gamer's Edition 2027. She started playing in 2017 after watching her grandson, and she has kept going ever since. "I call myself a 'Fortniter' more than a gamer," she told Guinness. Her streaming profile describes her as "chatty, friendly, never toxic", which is good advice for players of any age.

How to pull it off

  1. Get X-Ray. Legendary Sprites like X-Ray can come from Cheat Codes (the higher the code's rarity, the better your chances), special chests and some bosses. Legendary Cheat Codes, the orange ones, are guaranteed to give a special variant Sprite.
  2. Level it up. Open everything. Every container is 75 XP. Scans get more frequent and wider at each level.
  3. Use the scans. When X-Ray marks an enemy, you know where they are, but they don't know that you know. That's the moment to pick your angle, find high ground, or simply avoid a fight you don't want.
  4. Hunt the invisible. If the lobby has Spooky Sprinter running, or you suspect a Blinky Sprite user, X-Ray is your best counter.
  5. Extract before the final fight. A Level 4 X-Ray is too valuable to lose in a risky end game.
  6. Design your own. Grab some paper. What would your Sprite look like? What power would it have? Would it suit how you play? The next contest might be yours.
