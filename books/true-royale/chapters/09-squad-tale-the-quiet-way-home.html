The quiet way home

Jacko had been unbearable for four minutes.

It had started when he used a Lucky Locator, one of the handy little gadgets this season calls Gizmos. It had detected a buried chest somewhere ahead of them, inside the safe zone, and when Jacko excavated it, out popped the one thing he had coveted all season: a Spooky Dash Sprite, a Mythic that lets you dash through the air, straight through some objects, three times before it needs to recharge.

"It's a Mythic," he said, for perhaps the fifteenth time. "I've never had a Mythic. Do you know what the odds are? It's Mythic. Did I say it's Mythic?"

"You did," said Priya. "Now we have to get it home."

The problem with extraction

They all knew the rule, because it was the rule that shaped the entire season: a Sprite you're carrying is only borrowed. If you're eliminated before you extract it, it's gone, and the player who eliminated you can pick it up and keep it. Jacko's Mythic, until it was extracted, was in jeopardy every single second.

"Extraction Site," said Mo, pointing at the map. "Just over that ridge. Run in, call the crate, chuck it in, done."

Priya shook her head. "Calling the crate the normal way takes about forty-five seconds. And it's loud. Really loud. It rifts in with a big glowing beam, and everyone nearby can see and hear it. We'd be the most conspicuous squad on the Island, standing in one spot for nearly a minute, holding a Mythic."

"So we use a Portable Extractor," said Jacko. "You can extract from anywhere."

"I've got one," said Priya. "But it takes five seconds to use, you can't use it while you're in the air or swimming, and if we use it now, we haven't got it later. It's our contingency plan. Our backup."

"So what's the plan?" said Mo.

Priya smiled. She'd been waiting all match for somebody to ask.

Priya's precaution

Before the match, while the others were squabbling about where to land, Priya had quietly equipped an Extraction Accelerator in her Gizmo slot. It doesn't use up an inventory slot, and it does one thing exceptionally well. Used within about twenty metres of an Extraction Site, it summons the Extraction Crate almost instantly, in about three seconds, and silently. No forty-five-second wait. No beam calling every player in the area.

"You read that History File chapter, didn't you," said Tilly. It wasn't a question.

"Amundsen laid his depots before he needed them," said Priya. "Flags out, kilometres either side. Precautions in advance. That's all this is."

They crept over the ridge towards the Extraction Site, keeping low, while Tilly halted them twice to listen for footsteps. The site was deserted: just a single terminal glowing faintly in the long grass. Priya walked to within a few metres of it and activated the Accelerator.

Three seconds later, without a sound, the crate was there.

Ten seconds

Jacko threw the Spooky Dash Sprite onto the crate. (You don't even have to stand still and fiddle with menus: you can throw or drop a Sprite onto the crate and it's taken.) The crate pulsed, and a countdown appeared above it. The moment the first Sprite goes in, the crate's timer drops to ten seconds.

"Everyone!" said Priya. "Anything you're carrying, in it now!"

Mo had two Sprites in his inventory that he'd forgotten about. Tilly had one. They threw them in.

Seven seconds.

Tilly's head snapped round. "Car," she said. "Coming fast. From the road."

Five seconds. Four. Engine noise roared over the ridge, and a car smashed through the fence on the far side of the site, its doors flying open before it had even stopped. Here was the final, treacherous twist of the Extraction Crate: anybody can use it. The other squad hadn't come looking for a fight; they had come to extract their own Sprites, and the four friends were standing directly between the newcomers and the crate.

Two seconds. One.

The crate vanished in a ripple of light, taking every one of the squad's Sprites with it. Saved. Banked. Forever.

Then the shooting started.

What extraction really means

It was not, to be honest, their finest fight. Mo charged recklessly straight at the car and was eliminated almost immediately. Jacko, without his Spooky Dash, forgot that he no longer had a mid-air dash and jumped off a ledge into the open, which went exactly as badly as you'd expect. Priya and Tilly held on for longer, but in the end the other squad, angry and outnumbering them, won.

Back in the lobby, Mo sighed. "Well, that was a disaster."

"Was it?" said Priya. "Check your collection."

Jacko opened the Sprites tab, and there, safe and sound, was a Mythic Spooky Dash, his to keep. Mo's two Sprites were there too. They had lost the match, and they had lost nothing that mattered.

"That," said Priya, "is the whole point of extracting. You don't do it because you're about to lose. You do it so that losing doesn't cost you anything."

Tilly, who almost never laughed, laughed at Jacko's face. He was looking at his Mythic as if it might disappear.

Real or made up?

Made up: the squad, the car, and Jacko's dreadful jump.

Real: the Lucky Locator (it finds a buried chest 80 to 300 metres away inside the safe zone, likely to contain a Sprite you haven't found yet, and although only you see the preview, anyone can dig the chest up); the Spooky Dash (a Mythic with three dash charges); losing carried Sprites when you're eliminated; the Extraction Crate's 45-second loud arrival, its 10-second countdown once a Sprite goes in, and the fact that anybody can use it; throwing Sprites onto the crate; the Portable Extractor's five-second use and its "not in mid-air or water" rule; and the silent, three-second Extraction Accelerator, which must be used within about 20 metres of a site. Extraction Sites only start working once the first storm circle forms.

How to pull it off

  1. Equip an Extraction Accelerator before the match if you have one. Gizmos don't use an inventory slot.
  2. Get close to the Extraction Site first, within about 20 metres, then use it. The crate arrives in around three seconds, without the noise and light show.
  3. Throw everything in fast. Once the first Sprite goes in, you only have ten seconds. Make sure every squadmate knows the plan before the crate arrives.
  4. Keep a Portable Extractor as your backup. Use it on the ground, behind cover, and never in mid-air or water.
  5. Extract before the end game. The best time to extract is when you don't think you need to.
