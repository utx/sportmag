True story #4
The monster that only grows when you eat

Most Sprites level up the obvious way. You open chests, you win fights, you crack Cheat Codes, and your Sprite gets stronger. But one Sprite refuses to play by those rules, and it caused more head-scratching in the first weeks of the season than almost anything else on the Island.

Its name is Klombo. It's a big, round, pink monster with a cheerful face, based on the giant creatures that roamed the Island in an earlier chapter of Fortnite, and it's a Mythic, the rarest kind of Sprite there is. Fans quickly started calling it the rarest Sprite of the season. And it has one very unusual appetite: Klombo only gains XP when you eat or drink something that heals you. Chests? Nothing. Eliminations? Nothing. Med Kits, Shield Potions, fish, apples? Delicious.

The Klombo Sprite. Picture: Epic Games / Fortnite.

Every time Klombo levels up, it rewards you with a random item, and the higher its level, the better the item. So the more you heal, the more you get. Simple, right?

Not quite. Because there's a catch hiding inside that rule, and it's the kind of catch that makes you stop and frown.

The healing paradox

You can't heal if you're not hurt. Drink a Shield Potion with full shields and nothing happens: it just won't let you. And according to the Fortnite Wiki, Klombo's XP depends on how much health or shield you actually restore, so a heal that restores nothing earns nothing.

Which leads to a genuine paradox. To make your Klombo stronger, you have to get hurt. To keep yourself safe, you have to avoid getting hurt. The two goals point in completely opposite directions. A careful player, the kind who avoids damage, will have a Klombo that barely grows. A reckless player, the kind who takes lots of damage, will grow their Klombo quickly, right up until they're eliminated and lose it.

Two creators, two approaches

Within days of the season starting, Fortnite creators were taking on the Klombo puzzle in videos.

On 23 August, the YouTube channel Tomato, known for its challenge videos, set itself a strict rule in "The KLOMBO SPRITE ONLY Challenge in Fortnite": win a match using only the items Klombo hands out. The video's chapter titles tell the story of how hard that turned out to be: "Struggling to level up", then "Extracting the sprite", then "Starting a new attempt", before finally "Reaching level five" and "Winning the final showdown". It took more than one match, but the challenge was beaten.

Five days later, the Canadian creator Nick Eh 30, famous for keeping his videos completely free of bad language, posted "The struggle of trying to master Klombo…", which has been watched almost half a million times. The word "struggle" was not an exaggeration. According to the video's own chapter markers, the mastering section alone runs for about twenty minutes, as he and a friend worked their way up the levels, one heal at a time. Some viewers even chimed in with suggestions in the comments, such as hunting for ways to take a little damage safely so that there's always something to heal.

That's the real lesson of Klombo: it rewards creative thinking. If the only way to grow your Sprite is to heal, you need a steady, safe supply of small amounts of damage. Experienced players and guide-writers worked out a few:

  • Fall damage. Jump off something a little too high, like a small cliff or a roof. You'll lose a few health points, which you can then heal straight back. Guides at the gaming site Insider Gaming recommended exactly this.
  • The storm's edge. Late in a match, stepping briefly into the storm takes a small, predictable amount of health. Step out, heal, repeat. Careful, though: the storm hurts more in later circles.
  • Fights you're already winning. Any damage you take in a fight counts, so heal up afterwards, even if you're only a little bit hurt. Don't waste the opportunity.

Epic speeds things up

Klombo's levelling was famously slow. Doing the same heal-hurt-heal routine for twenty minutes can feel tedious, and, as Nick Eh 30's video title suggests, even the most popular creators found it a struggle. So, in a hotfix on 2 October, Epic changed the numbers: according to the Fortnite Wiki, each point of healing now earns Klombo twice as much XP as before. Epic also made Klombo more generous with bonus items in the Fortnitemares update. A Sprite that once took ages to master now takes roughly half as long.

This happens a lot in game design. Designers make a guess about how hard something should be, millions of players try it, and then the designers adjust. Klombo is a good example of a "balance change": not a new feature, just a number nudged to make the game feel fairer and more fun.

One Klombo, a hundred players

A rare Sprite makes the perfect prize for a giant game of keep-away. In September, Australian creator Loserfruit, from Melbourne, ran a custom match called "100 Players vs 1 Rare Sprite", in which players fought to hold on to a single Mythic Sprite until a thirty-minute timer ran out. Because of the Sprite rules, whoever was carrying it was the most hunted person on the Island. The eventual winner left a comment thanking the two friends who had helped protect them, adding: "so in reality all of us three won".

How to pull it off

  1. Find a Klombo. It's Mythic, so it's rare. Your best chances are from higher-rarity Cheat Codes and special chests.
  2. Never waste a heal. Don't drink potions at full shield. Wait until you've actually lost something, so every heal counts.
  3. Take damage deliberately, but only a little. A small fall is the safest method. Never do it when enemies are nearby.
  4. Carry lots of small heals. Fish, fruit and Small Shield Potions are perfect: quick to consume, and they turn tiny bits of damage into XP.
  5. Extract early and often. If you're eliminated, your Klombo goes back to Level 1. After a level-up you're proud of, find an Extraction Site.
