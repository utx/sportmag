Everything in this book really happened (mostly)

Every season, millions of matches of Fortnite are played, and almost all of them vanish without a trace. Somebody wins, everybody else heads back to the lobby, and the Island resets as if nothing ever happened. But every so often, something happens that's too good to forget: a hiding place so clever that nobody spots it, a puzzle that thousands of people race to crack, a championship decided by two points out of more than five hundred.

This book collects those moments. Every True Story in it happened during Chapter 7, Season 4: Override, between August and October 2026, and every one has been checked against at least one written source: news reports, tournament results, Epic's own update notes, or the creators' own videos. Where the sources disagreed, or where something couldn't be confirmed, we say so. That's what separates a true story from a rumour.

Two kinds of story

Most chapters are True Stories. They're marked at the top, and they come with a Check the tape box at the end that tells you exactly where the facts came from, so you can verify them yourself.

A few chapters are different. They're called Squad Tales, and they follow a squad of four friends who don't exist: Priya, Mo, Tilly and Jacko. Their adventures are fiction, which means we made them up. But here's the rule we set ourselves: every trick they use is real. Every item, every timer and every number in a Squad Tale works the way it does in the real game this season. The characters are invented; the mechanics aren't.

Somewhere in the middle of the book there's also a History File: a true story from more than a hundred years ago that turns out to be surprisingly similar to one of this season's biggest ideas. We won't spoil it here.

How to steal a trick

Reading about a brilliant play is fun. Pulling it off yourself is better. So every chapter finishes with a How to pull it off section that breaks the mechanic down step by step, and a Steal-ability rating, out of five Chug Jugs, that tells you how easy it would be to copy in your own matches.

Five Chug Jugs means anyone could do it next match. One Chug Jug means you'd need a lot of luck, a lot of skill, or a time machine (because some of these tricks have already been switched off by Epic).

A note on the words

You'll notice that this book doesn't talk down to you. Some of the words are big, and some of the sentences are long, because some of the stories are complicated and deserve to be told properly. When a tricky word turns up, we'll usually explain it right there in the sentence, or you'll find it in a Word Watch box nearby. Every Word Watch word is collected in the word bank at the back, the Jug of Words. Try sneaking a few of them into conversation. "That was a remarkably audacious rotation" sounds a lot more impressive than "nice".

The small print (it's important)

This book covers Fortnite Battle Royale in Chapter 7, Season 4: Override, up to 14 October 2026. Update 42.40 arrives on 15 October, and Epic changes things all the time, so some details may be different by the time you read this. If something doesn't match, the game has changed, not the story.

The players and creators in this book are grown-ups or professional players. Where we've mentioned videos, we've only described what's in them; we haven't included links, so ask a grown-up before you go looking. Nothing in this book asks you to spend money, and this book never recommends cheats, hacks or glitches. (The "Cheat Codes" and "Lobby Hacks" you'll read about are official parts of the game, made by Epic.)
