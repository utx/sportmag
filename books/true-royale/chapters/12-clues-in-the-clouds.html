True story #9
Clues in the clouds

Long before the Override season began, Fortnite fans knew something big was coming. They just didn't know what. So they did what Fortnite fans always do: they turned detective.

This is the story of how thousands of players worked out the secrets of a whole season before it started, using nothing but sharp eyes, sharp ears and a lot of arguing. It's also the story of a clue that fooled almost everyone.

The tune at Wonkeeland

The first clue was a sound. Around 1 August, near the end of the previous season, two players exploring Wonkeeland noticed something odd. Faintly, among the usual noises, a tune was playing: the famous jingle from the start of Pac-Man, the arcade game that's been gobbling dots since 1980. The well-known Fortnite data-miner ShiinaBR, someone who studies the game's files for hidden content, shared the discovery online, crediting the two players who had heard it first.

The gaming news site Insider Gaming called it "the biggest hint yet" that Pac-Man was coming to Fortnite. Then, by 6 August, the sound had been quietly removed. Nobody knows for certain why; Epic didn't say. The likeliest explanation is that it had been switched on prematurely, before Epic was ready to reveal anything. But it was too late. People had heard it.

And they were right. When the season began, three Pac-Man-themed islands appeared off the coast. In the first weekend alone, according to Epic, players dropped onto them 3.5 million times.

Shapes in the sky

The next clues came on 10 August, in a ten-second teaser video. It showed Peely, Fortnite's famous banana, racing through a loop-the-loop and collecting golden rings. Anyone who'd ever played a Sonic the Hedgehog game recognised it instantly, and the official Sonic account confirmed the hint, posting that the new zone "feels oddly familiar…"

But the eagle-eyed viewers weren't looking at Peely. They were looking at the sky. Some of the clouds behind the loop had straight edges and square corners. One of them looked exactly like the Z-shaped piece from Tetris. When Tetris was officially confirmed a couple of days later, people went back to the new teaser and found more cloud shapes hidden in that one too: Pac-Man's ghosts, and the moustached face of Sonic's arch-enemy, Dr Eggman.

The secret phrases

At the same time, Epic started a game inside its official Discord chat server. It was what's called an alternate reality game, or ARG: a puzzle that plays out in the real world, across websites and social media, rather than inside a game. Players typed secret phrases into a chat bot, and each phrase was a clue to one of the season's guest stars. Fans compiled the list as fast as they could find them:

  • GottaGoFast: obviously Sonic, whose catchphrase it is.
  • TakeYourHeart: the Phantom Thieves from the game Persona 5.
  • PerfectOrder: thought to be connected to The Order, Geno's daughter.
  • SurviveTheNight: …?

That last one caused a sensation. Thousands of fans decided that "SurviveTheNight" must mean Minecraft, a game famous for its dangerous nights full of monsters. It made sense. It fitted. And, according to the news site TechTimes, that theory held for more than twenty-four hours, with excitement building the whole time. Minecraft in Fortnite! It would be enormous!

It was wrong. On 11 August, Epic revealed that the clue actually pointed to 99 Nights in the Forest, a survival game made on Roblox. It was the first time a character from a Roblox game had ever come to Fortnite, which was big news in itself, just not the news people had convinced themselves of.

The Minecraft mix-up is a perfect example of something that happens to everyone, not just gamers. When we want something to be true, we notice the evidence that fits and ignore the evidence that doesn't. "Survive the night" fitted Minecraft, so people stopped looking for other answers. A good detective keeps asking: what else could this mean? Is there any evidence that corroborates my theory, or am I just hoping? Speculation is fun. But it isn't the same as knowing.

The clues became codes

When the season started on 20 August, Epic rewarded the detectives. The ARG phrases turned into real Lobby Hack codes for the new Admin Panel. GottaGoFast gave a special Sonic Sprite. PerfectOrder gave four Spicy Tacos. And SurviveTheNight gave two Cheat Code Locators. Even the cloud shapes stayed in the sky for a while. They were finally cleared away in the Fortnitemares update on 1 October.

Listen to the Island

The Pac-Man tune was found by players who were listening carefully, and that's a skill worth practising, because sound matters more in Fortnite this season than ever. Here are a few auditory clues the Island gives away:

  • Cheat Codes hum. Since the Fortnitemares update, Epic says Cheat Codes "can now be heard from farther away".
  • Vault keys pulse. At Stone Sanctum, the containers holding the Temple Vault Keys pulse and make a sound you can track.
  • Hiding spots rattle. Players hiding in dumpsters, haystacks or portable toilets give themselves away: the hiding spot shakes every few seconds.
  • Footsteps, chests and gliders all make noise, and every one of them tells you something.
Visualize Sound Effects
Turn this on in your audio settings. A ring appears around the middle of your screen, and icons show you what's making a noise nearby and which direction it's coming from. Guides this season pointed out that it helps you find Cheat Codes too. It's useful for everyone, and especially helpful for players who are deaf or hard of hearing.

How to pull it off

  1. Switch on Visualize Sound Effects. It's in the audio settings, and it's one of the most useful options in the whole game.
  2. Play with headphones if you can, so you can tell which direction sounds come from.
  3. Stop and listen. Before you open a door or crest a hill, pause for a second. Sound travels further than sight in a building.
  4. Be a sceptical detective. When you see a leak or a "100% confirmed" rumour online, ask: who says so, and what's their evidence?
  5. Watch the sky in teasers. Epic loves hiding clues in the background.
