Hoist the Jug!

A treasure chest the size of a house appeared on a beach. It filled up like a loading bar. It sang. Then one Saturday it cracked open, and out of the fog sailed a pirate ship.

That ship is carrying Fortnite's next season, Treasure Hunters: a short "mini-season" starting on 1 November 2026, in which rival pirate crews race to find the greatest treasure of all. Its headline guests are the crew of One Piece, the most popular pirate story on the planet.

This book is your primer. First, it throws a farewell party for Override, the season that's ending. Then it hands you a telescope and points it at everything sailing in. And because a pirate season deserves real pirates, there's a chapter of genuine pirate history in the middle, plus "Real Pirates" boxes all the way through.

A map drawn before the voyage

We wrote this three days before launch, so some of it is guesswork. Old map-makers had the same problem: they drew the coastlines they knew and doodled sea monsters in the gaps. So every fact about the new season wears a badge:

How sure are we?
Look for these badges next to every new-season fact.
Confirmed
Epic has announced it. As solid as a chest full of gold.
Spotted
Seen in Epic's own trailer or artwork. It's real, but nobody knows exactly how it works yet.
Leak
Dug out of the game's files by dataminers, or let slip by insiders. Usually right.
Rumour
Internet chatter. A sailor's yarn. Fun, but don't bet your doubloons on it.

In the text they look like this: Confirmed Spotted Leak Rumour. Most leaks come from people who datamine the game; a rumour is closer to a sailor's yarn. If the game and this book disagree after 1 November, trust the game.

Word Watch

Primernoun
A short book that teaches you the basics before you dive in. (Say it "PRIM-er".)
Datamineverb
To dig through a game's files for hidden things that haven't been announced. The diggers are called dataminers.
Yarnnoun
Wool for knitting, but also a long, exciting story that might not be entirely true. Sailors were famous for spinning yarns.

How to read this book

  • Chapter 1 is a party for Override. Bring snacks.
  • Chapters 2 and 3 tell how the new season was revealed and show the new island.
  • Chapter 4 is real pirate history, including a Sydney coin with a hole punched through it.
  • Chapters 5 to 9 cover the crews, the characters, the weapons and the mysteries.
  • Chapter 10 tells you when you can play, and how to be a leak detective.
  • Word Watch boxes explain the trickiest words, and the Treasure Chest of Words at the back collects them all. Then there's a quiz, to sort the captains from the landlubbers.

The small print

This is an unofficial fan book. It isn't made or approved by Epic Games, the makers of One Piece, or YouTuber PWR Lachlan, whose video gave us the new-season screenshots (used with permission). Names and details may change on 1 November.

A note for grown-ups

Fortnite carries an age rating on every platform, so check the one on yours. Nothing in this book asks anyone to spend money. The guest stars include One Piece characters and two rappers; we mention the musicians but don't recommend their music, and the One Piece anime is aimed at slightly older viewers. The pirate history keeps the fighting to a minimum and the fun to a maximum.
