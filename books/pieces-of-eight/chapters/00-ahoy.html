Hoist the Jug!

Something strange is happening on the Island. A gigantic treasure chest has appeared on a beach. It filled up slowly, like a loading bar, and it sang songs to anyone who swam close enough to hear. Then, one Saturday, it cracked open, and out of the fog sailed a pirate ship.

That ship is carrying the next season of Fortnite. It's called Treasure Hunters, it arrives on 1 November 2026, and it's a short "mini-season" about pirates racing each other to find the greatest treasure of all. Its headline guests are the crew of One Piece, the most popular pirate story on the planet.

This book is your primer. It does two jobs. First, it waves goodbye to Override, the season that's ending, with all its best bits in one place. Then it hands you a telescope and points it at the horizon, so you know exactly what's sailing towards you before the voyage begins. And because a pirate season deserves real pirates, there's a whole chapter of genuine pirate history in the middle, plus little "Real Pirates" boxes scattered all the way through.

Word Watch

Primernoun
A short book that teaches you the basics of a subject before you dive in. (Say it "PRIM-er", like "prim and proper".)
Horizonnoun
The line far away where the sea seems to meet the sky. Something "on the horizon" is coming soon.
Voyagenoun
A long journey, especially by sea or through space.
Yarnnoun
Wool for knitting, but also a long, exciting story that might not be completely true. Sailors were famous for spinning yarns.

A map drawn before the voyage

Here's the tricky part. We wrote this book on 29 October, three days before Treasure Hunters launches. Epic has shown a trailer and a handful of teasers, but it hasn't published the full details yet. Old sea charts had the same problem: map-makers drew the coastlines they knew, then guessed the rest, and sometimes doodled a sea monster in the empty bits.

So every fact about the new season in this book wears a little badge, telling you how sure we are:

How sure are we?
Look for these badges next to every new-season fact.
Confirmed
Epic has announced it. As solid as a chest full of gold.
Spotted
Seen in Epic's own trailer or artwork, so it's real, but nobody knows exactly how it works yet.
Leak
Found by dataminers digging through game files, or by insiders. Usually right, but Epic hasn't said so.
Rumour
Internet chatter and guesswork. A sailor's yarn. Fun to think about, but don't bet your doubloons on it.

In the text, they look like this: Confirmed Spotted Leak Rumour. Most leaks come from people who datamine the game's files, and they're usually right. A rumour is more like a sailor's yarn: fun to hear, but all anyone can do is speculate. When Fortnite and this book disagree after 1 November, trust the game. It means the map has been redrawn, not that your eyes are wrong.

Word Watch

Datamineverb
To dig through a game's files looking for hidden things that haven't been announced yet. The people who do it are called dataminers.
Speculateverb
To guess about something when you don't have all the facts. A guess like this is called speculation.
Chartnoun
A sailor's word for a map of the sea, showing coastlines, rocks and safe routes.

How to read this book

  • Chapter 1 is a farewell to Override. Read it for the memories.
  • Chapters 2 and 3 tell the story of how the new season was revealed, and take a first look at the new island.
  • Chapter 4 is real pirate history: famous pirates, real treasure and a coin from old Sydney with a hole punched through the middle.
  • Chapters 5 to 9 cover the crews, the characters, the weapons and the mysteries of Treasure Hunters.
  • Chapter 10 tells you when you can actually play it, and how to be a leak detective.
  • Word Watch boxes explain the trickier words, and there are lots of salty sea words this time. Every one of them is collected at the back in The Treasure Chest of Words.
  • The quiz at the back will show whether you're a true treasure hunter or a landlubber.

A note for grown-ups

Fortnite carries an age rating on every platform, so check the one on yours. Nothing in this book asks anyone to spend money. The new season's guest stars include characters from the One Piece anime and two well-known rappers; we mention the musicians but don't recommend their music, and the One Piece anime is aimed at a slightly older audience, so it's worth a look together before watching. The pirate history chapter keeps the real-life fighting to a minimum and the fun facts to a maximum.
