Something inside wants to be found

Epic almost never just tells players what's coming next. It drops cryptic clues into the game and lets millions of fans crack the code together. The build-up to Treasure Hunters was one of the cleverest teases in years, and it started with a box.

A chest appears

In mid-October, a gigantic treasure chest appeared on a beach on the south coast, east of Sunken Shores. Golden chains. Glittering gems. A grinning skull for a lock. And a health bar above it, slowly creeping upwards, as if the chest were downloading.

Every time the bar jumped, players stampeded to the beach. And strangest of all, the chest sang. Swim close and you'd hear music drifting out of it, a different song each time, as if several bands were taking turns inside. The anticipation was enormous.

Six flags, six songs

Then a picture in the game's news feed showed six pirate flags carved into a wooden board Spotted. Dataminers worked out that each one belonged to a different crew, and that the songs from the chest matched them.

The six crew flags from Epic's teaser. Can you spot the one with a straw hat? Picture: Epic Games / Fortnite.

Fans spotted that one flag was almost exactly the Jolly Roger of One Piece's Straw Hat Pirates, minus the hat. Others were linked to the virtual singers Hatsune Miku and Kasane Teto, and to rappers A$AP Rocky and Lil Uzi Vert, who all posted tantalising previews of their own. Chapter 5 has the full crew list.

The night the chest opened

On Saturday 24 October at 2pm in New York (5am Sunday in Sydney, so most of Australia slept right through it), the bar hit 100% and the chest cracked open. Out of a moonlit fog sailed a little wooden ship with a sheep's head on the front, followed by two logos side by side: Fortnite and One Piece Confirmed.

The Going Merry, the Straw Hats' first ship, from Epic's trailer. Notice anything odd? It's hanging from a balloon. Picture: Epic Games / Fortnite (trailer).

One Piece fans knew it instantly: the Going Merry, Luffy's first ship. Epic posted the season's name with the words "The greatest adventure of all!", and the next morning Fortnite's official account posted nothing but three straw hat emojis. Within three hours, according to leaker HYPEX, the teaser had more than 115,000 likes, making it one of Fortnite's most-liked posts ever.

Wanted, all over the world

Meanwhile, real WANTED posters began popping up on city walls, in the style of One Piece's famous bounty posters. A fan in London photographed a set showing Luffy, his brother Ace, and Fortnite's Jonesy and Hope Spotted.

Posters on a London wall: Ace, Jonesy, Hope and Luffy. Photo shared by a fan on Reddit.

"Let the hunt begin"

Finally, on 27 October, came the trailer: 28 seconds, packed to bursting. Luffy's arm stretched across the sand. Zoro drew his swords. Cannons boomed from pirate ships. And a narrator growled four words to herald the new season: "Let the hunt begin."

The trailer in six frames. Pictures: Epic Games / Fortnite (trailer).

Look closely at the title card: the name is written twice, in English and in Japanese katakana (Torejā Hantāzu), a nod to One Piece's home country.

Word Watch

Crypticadjective
Mysterious and puzzling, with a hidden meaning you have to work out.
Anticipationnoun
The excited feeling of waiting for something you know is coming.
Tantalisingadjective
Teasing you with something you want but can't have yet. (From Tantalus, a king in Greek myth who was punished by being kept forever just out of reach of food and water.)
Heraldverb
To announce that something is coming. (A herald was once a royal messenger.)
