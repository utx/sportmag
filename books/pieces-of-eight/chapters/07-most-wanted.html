Fortnite goes anime

Look closely at the trailer and you'll notice that everyone, even Fortnite's own heroes, looks like they've stepped out of a Japanese cartoon: bold outlines, flat blocks of colour, huge expressive eyes. That style is called cel-shading, and leakers say Fortnite's classic characters are getting anime "remix" outfits for the season Leak.

So who's in the Battle Pass?

Here's where it gets messy. Epic hasn't published the Battle Pass yet, and the leaks contradict each other:

ReportSays the Battle Pass includes…
BeebomNico Robin, Ace, Charlotte, Cuddle Team Leader, Jonesy and a "Captain X"
VICEJonesy, Hope, Charlotte and Cuddle Team Leader, maybe Oscar
Future of the ForceProbably Luffy, Zoro and Buggy
TimesaverAnime Hope, Evie, Syd and Jonesy, maybe Oscar

Spot the pattern? Jonesy is in nearly every list, and Charlotte and Cuddle Team Leader in most. When several separate sources agree, a leak is probably right. When only one source says something (hello, "Captain X"), it's dubious. One report says the pass will cost 800 V-Bucks, the same as Override's Leak.

A word about V-Bucks

The Item Shop will be full of shiny pirate outfits, but you don't need to buy anything to have a brilliant time: the new island, the weapons and the free rewards are for everyone. V-Bucks cost real money, so talk to a grown-up before spending any. And nobody, anywhere, gives away free V-Bucks. That's always a scam.

Word Watch

Cel-shadingnoun
A way of drawing 3D computer graphics so they look like a flat, hand-drawn cartoon. (A "cel" was a clear sheet that old cartoon animators painted on.)
Debutnoun
Someone's first appearance. (Say it "DAY-byoo".)
Contradictverb
To say the opposite of something. If two reports contradict each other, at least one must be wrong.
Dubiousadjective
Doubtful; not to be trusted until proven. (Say it "DYOO-bee-us".)
