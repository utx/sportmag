Fortnite goes anime

The Straw Hats aren't the only pirates sailing in. Fortnite's own heroes have had a makeover too, and they look like they've stepped straight out of a cartoon.

Fortnite, anime style

Look at the trailer and you'll notice that everyone, even Fortnite's own characters, is drawn in the style of Japanese anime: bold outlines, flat blocks of colour, big expressive eyes. It's an art technique called cel-shading, and it makes 3D characters look as if they've been hand-drawn. Leakers say Fortnite's classic heroes are getting special anime "remix" versions for this season Leak.

Word Watch

Cel-shadingnoun
A way of drawing 3D computer graphics so they look like a flat, hand-drawn cartoon. (A "cel" was a clear sheet that old cartoon animators painted on.)
Remixnoun
A new version of something that already exists, changed in an exciting way. Usually used for songs; in Fortnite, for outfits.
Debutnoun
Someone's first appearance. (Say it "DAY-byoo".) Kasane Teto makes her Fortnite debut this season.

Hatsune Miku and Kasane Teto are both "virtual singers": voices made by computer software, which musicians all over the world use to create songs, each with a cartoon character as its face. Miku has been in Fortnite before, but this time she's got a pirate hat and coat. The creator of our video was clearly impressed: "There's the Hatsune Miku pirate. It's crazy." Teto, with her red hair twisted into curls like drill bits, makes her Fortnite debut.

The rappers A$AP Rocky and Lil Uzi Vert also appear in the trailer as the captains of their own crews. As we said in Chapter 5, their music is for grown-ups, so we'll leave them there.

So who's in the Battle Pass?

This is where things get messy. Epic hasn't published the Battle Pass yet, and the leaks contradict each other. Here's what four different reports said, all published before launch:

ReportSays the Battle Pass includes…

VICEJonesy, Hope, Charlotte and Cuddle Team Leader, plus possibly Oscar
Future of the ForceProbably Luffy, Zoro and Buggy, because they're on the key art

Notice what they agree on: Jonesy appears in nearly every list, and Charlotte and Cuddle Team Leader in most. When several independent sources say the same thing, it's more likely to be true. When only one says it, be sceptical. (Chapter 10 has more leak-detective tips.) One report also says the pass will cost 800 V-Bucks, the same as Override's Leak.

A word about V-Bucks

New seasons are exciting, and the Item Shop will be full of shiny pirate outfits. But you don't need to buy anything to have a brilliant time: the free rewards, the new map and the new weapons are for everyone. If you're thinking of spending real money (V-Bucks cost real money), talk to a grown-up first. And remember: nobody, anywhere, gives away free V-Bucks. That's always a scam.
