Chapter 9
Here be monsters

Every good treasure map has blank spaces, and every blank space has a monster in it. Grab your magnifying glass: Treasure Hunters has mysteries nobody has solved yet.

Real Pirates: monsters on the map

Part of the Carta Marina, a map of the northern seas from 1539. Spot the sea serpent wrapped around a ship. Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons.

Five hundred years ago, the open ocean was a terrifying, uncharted unknown, so map-makers filled the empty seas with monsters: sea serpents coiled around ships, whales with tusks, fish with faces. The Carta Marina, drawn in 1539 by a Swedish priest called Olaus Magnus, is swarming with them. Sailors also told tales of the Kraken, a giant beast from Norway that dragged whole ships down to Davy Jones' Locker. It's a legend, but with a grain of truth: the real giant squid can grow about as long as a bus.

The Lord of the Coast

The Lord of the Coast, from One Piece, as shared by a leaker.

Here's a monster Fortnite might actually let us meet. Leaker HYPEX says the Lord of the Coast will be "present next season" Leak. In One Piece, it's a gigantic, eel-like leviathan with a mouth full of teeth that lurks near Luffy's home village in the very first chapter, and it plays a big part in how Luffy got his straw hat. A boss? Part of the map? Lachlan reckons it "might be some kind of boss", adding: "It's been a while since we had some kind of, like, crazy NPC like that." If you see a long grey shape under the water on launch day, swim the other way.

Sprites ahoy!

Good news for Sprite fans: the little helpers look set to stick around. The key art shows what looks like a new Sprite, a mouse-like creature living in a treasure chest Spotted, and leakers found a combined Miku and Teto Sprite Leak. Remember the end of Override's story? A voice revealed that the Sliver of the Zero Point was locked inside the chest. Is this the same voice? Someone who wants the Sliver for themselves?

The masked figure from the trailer. Friend? Foe? Game-show host? Picture: Epic Games / Fortnite (trailer).

Then there's the masked figure in the trailer: smooth golden mask, spiky armour, glowing screen behind Spotted. A complete enigma. Some fans link the mask to the voice, and to the sixth, mystery crew. Lachlan got "vibes of, like, someone hosting this whole season", like the presenter of a giant game show. One leaker even claimed the figure is voiced by a world-famous YouTuber, based on a cast list that was later changed Rumour, which is about as reliable as a map drawn by Zoro.

For now, the truth remains elusive, and that's half the fun. Write your own theory down, seal it in an envelope (or a bottle), and open it when the story is revealed. If you're right, you get bragging rights for life.
