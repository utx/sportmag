Here be monsters

Every good treasure map has blank spaces, and every blank space has a monster in it. Treasure Hunters has plenty of mysteries that nobody has solved yet. Grab your magnifying glass.

Real Pirates: monsters on the map

Part of the Carta Marina, a map of the northern seas made in 1539 by Olaus Magnus. Spot the sea serpent wrapped around a ship. Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons.

Five hundred years ago, the open ocean was a terrifying, uncharted unknown. Map-makers filled the empty seas on their charts with drawings of monsters: sea serpents coiled around ships, whales with tusks, enormous lobsters and fish with faces. The most famous example is the Carta Marina, a huge map of the northern seas drawn in 1539 by a Swedish priest called Olaus Magnus. It's swarming with beasts.

You may have heard that old maps had the words "Here be dragons" written on them. In fact, the phrase (in Latin, hic sunt dracones) is known from just two old globes, both made in the early 1500s, and hardly ever appeared on maps at all. But the idea stuck, and "here be dragons" now means "danger: unknown territory ahead".

Sailors also told tales of the Kraken, a gigantic sea monster from Norway that could drag whole ships down to Davy Jones' Locker. It's a legend, but there might be a grain of truth in it: the real giant squid can grow about as long as a bus, and for centuries sailors only ever saw pieces of them floating in the sea.

The Lord of the Coast

The Lord of the Coast, from One Piece, as shared by a leaker. Picture via leaks shown in the video.

Here's a monster Fortnite might actually let us meet. Leaker HYPEX posted that the Lord of the Coast will be "present next season" Leak. In One Piece, the Lord of the Coast is a gigantic sea monster (a kind of beast called a Sea King) that lurks in the waters near Luffy's home village in the very first chapter of the story. It's a long, grey, eel-like leviathan with a mouth full of teeth, and it plays a big part in the story of how Luffy got his straw hat.

What will it do in Fortnite? Nobody knows. It might be a boss. It might be part of the map, swimming around the island. The creator of the video we watched guessed it "might be some kind of boss", and added: "It's been a while since we had some kind of, like, crazy NPC like that." If you see a long grey shape under the water on launch day, swim the other way.

The mystery voice

Now for the strangest mystery of all. In the days before the chest opened, a whispering voice was heard on the Island. Leakers collected its lines Leak:

Clue 3"Feel that? Something inside wants to be found."

Whose voice is it? Somebody talking about what's inside the chest, clearly. Remember the end of Override's story: a voice revealed that the Sliver of the Zero Point was locked inside. Is this the same voice? Is it someone who wants the Sliver for themselves?

The masked figure from the trailer. Friend? Foe? Host? Picture: Epic Games / Fortnite (trailer).

The trailer also shows a masked figure in a smooth golden mask, standing in front of a glowing screen Spotted. The figure is a complete enigma. Some fans have linked the mask to the voice, and to the sixth, mystery crew. The creator of our video said the voice gave him "vibes of, like, someone hosting this whole season", like the presenter of a giant game show.

And then there's the wildest theory of all. One leaker claimed that the masked figure is voiced by a famous YouTuber, based on a cast list posted on a film-and-TV website, which was later changed Rumour. There's no official word at all, and a cast list that keeps changing is about as reliable as a map drawn by Zoro. File it under "sailor's yarn" until Epic says otherwise.
