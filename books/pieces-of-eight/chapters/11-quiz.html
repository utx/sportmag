How many pieces of eight did you collect?

14 or 15: King of the Pirates. Luffy would be jealous.
10 to 13: Quartermaster. The crew trusts you with the loot.
5 to 9: Deckhand. Solid work. A quick re-read and you'll be promoted.
0 to 4: Landlubber! Walk the plank (it's mostly a myth, so you'll be fine) and try again.

Word Watch

Landlubbernoun
A sailor's insult for someone who knows nothing about the sea and is clumsy on a ship.
Deckhandnoun
A sailor who does the everyday work on a ship's deck: scrubbing, hauling ropes and keeping things tidy.
