About this book

Pieces of Eight is an unofficial fan book made by The Chug Jug, a fan magazine about Fortnite. It was written on 29 and 30 October 2026, just before the Treasure Hunters mini-season launched, as a farewell to Override and a first look at what was coming next. Once the season is live, much of it will be out of date. That's the deal with treasure maps drawn before the voyage.

It isn't made, approved or endorsed by Epic Games, by Eiichiro Oda, Shueisha or Toei Animation (the makers of One Piece), by any of the musicians mentioned, or by the YouTube creator whose video helped us. It's free, and it will never ask you to spend money. If you ever want to buy anything in Fortnite, ask a grown-up first.
