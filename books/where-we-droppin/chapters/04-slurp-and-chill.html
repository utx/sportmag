Chapter 4 · The quiet east island
Slurp and chill

Not every great drop is a famous one. Off the east coast, between WonkeeLand and Golden Grove, there's a little island that doesn't even have a name on the map. It has a wooden dock, a building, some orange autumn trees, a few rocks and a lot of sea. Watch the Battle Bus fly over it and you'll see dozens of players glide straight past on their way to somewhere louder.

That's exactly why it's on our list. One guide says it "barely gets any traffic", and a look at the map shows why: it's out on its own, away from every road. If WonkeeLand is a rock concert, this island is a library.

A little lonely, a lot lucky

Don't expect a city. This isn't a barren rock, but it's close to the edge of the world, and on a grey evening, with the wind on the water and nobody else in sight, it can feel almost desolate. Some players find that a bit melancholy. We think it's a gift. Every second nobody is shooting at you is a second you can spend getting ready.

Why land here?

Here's what's waiting for you, according to the guide that tested it:

  • Chests along the dock and inside a boat moored there.
  • Barrels and vegetables scattered about: handy for building materials and a little healing.
  • Fishing spots in the water around the island.
  • Two ziplines that whisk you back to the mainland, one heading north-west and one heading south. You can see them on the map.

Loot is scarcer here than in a big town, so you won't leave with a backpack full of gold. That's the trade-off. What you will leave with is time, health and a plan.

Secret 1: golden fishing

Grab a Fishing Rod and look for ripples on the water. A normal fishing spot gives you a better chance of rare fish and weapons than fishing in plain water. But the real prize is a golden fishing spot, which sparkles gold. According to the Fortnite Wiki, you can fish a golden spot twice, and every catch guarantees a weapon (Epic rarity or better, sometimes even an Exotic) plus a fish. Occasionally, you'll reel in an entire chest.

Now the honest bit. Early in the season, golden fishing spots appeared in every match. Then, on 26 August, Epic made them much rarer: each one now only has about a one-in-five chance of turning up. So the bonus has diminished, but it hasn't disappeared. If you see gold, drop everything and cast.

Fishing can feel a little monotonous: cast, wait, reel, repeat. Use the waiting time well. Stand still, contemplate where the storm will go next, and plan your route to the circle.

Secret 2: the Slurp Truck trick

Our second secret isn't on the island itself, but it belongs in this chapter because it's all about starting a fight at full strength. Around the Island sit a few Slurp Trucks: tanker trucks full of Slurp, the glowing blue juice that heals both health and shield. Destroy one and the Slurp inside tops you up.

The guide from Dexerto says a Slurp Truck gives you a free 100 shield, and lists three of them this season:

  • East of Sunken Shores, on a little island near an extraction site, close to Mega Maze (Chapter 5).
  • North-west of Shaken Sanctuary, with chests and a Cheat Code nearby.
  • Pac-Man Island, where the truck sits in Pac-Man's mouth. (Pac-Man is a guest from another company's game. Note that the spooky Freddy Fazbear's Pizzeria is not far away, so this one isn't for everyone.)

Let's be sceptical for a moment. We could only find one source that gives the exact amount, so we can't ascertain that it's always 100. What we can confirm is that Slurp heals: the Fortnite Wiki says each smashed Slurp Barrel heals 10 health or shield. Either way, it's free, it's fast, and it's a brilliant way to start.

The calm-start plan

Land on the dock. Open the boat chest and the dock chest. Check for gold on the water and fish if you see it. Smash barrels for materials. Then ride a zipline back to the mainland, fully loaded, while the players who landed at WonkeeLand are still patching up their wounds.

Players who start like this tend to be resilient. They don't get knocked out in the first minute, so they get more practice in the later stages of the game, where matches are actually won.
