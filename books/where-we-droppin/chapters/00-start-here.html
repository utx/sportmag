Somewhere on board, a hundred players are asking themselves the same question, and it's so famous that Fortnite named a weapon after it. (In Zero Build, everyone lands holding the Where We Droppin' Pistol.)

Where are we dropping?

It's the most important decision of the match, and you have about thirty seconds to make it. Land somewhere packed and you might be back in the lobby before you've found a gun. Land somewhere empty and you'll be safe, but you may spend the rest of the game running away from the storm.

This book takes you to five real places on the Island right now. Each one has a secret: a mini-game, a hidden vault, a trick that most players fly straight past. Learn all five and you'll never sit on the Battle Bus wondering where to go again.

Rumour patrol: how we checked

The internet is overflowing with "secret Fortnite tricks". Some are brilliant. Many are out of date, because Fortnite rebuilds its map every few months and the secrets disappear with it. And a few are simply made up. You may have heard about a barbell that only lifts after you've eaten four steaks, or a bedroom drawer that secretly works as a treasure chest. They sound fun. We couldn't find a shred of evidence that either one exists on the Island today.

So we were sceptical about everything. For every secret in this book, we tried to ascertain the facts from at least two separate sources: the Fortnite Wiki, Epic's own update notes, the map data, and guides from gaming websites. Being plausible wasn't enough to get a trick into this book; it had to be confirmed. On the rare occasions when only one source mentioned a detail, we've said so.

Sometimes the sources didn't agree. One guide would say a key was hidden in a tower; another would contradict it and say the rooftops. When the evidence was ambiguous like that, we haven't pretended to know. We've been candid and told you what each source said, so you can find out for yourself in the game.

Word Watch

Scepticaladjective
Doubting; not easily convinced. A sceptical reader wants proof before believing a claim.
Ascertainverb
To find something out for certain, by checking or investigating.
Plausibleadjective
Seeming reasonable or believable, though not yet proven. A plausible story might still turn out to be false.
Contradictverb
To say the opposite of something; to clash with other evidence.
Ambiguousadjective
Unclear, or open to more than one meaning. "Meet me at the tower" is ambiguous on an island with ten towers.
Candidadjective
Honest and direct, even when the truth isn't what people want to hear.

Try this: Next time a video promises a "100% guaranteed secret", ask three questions. When was it made? Does anyone else say the same thing? Does the game you're actually playing match it? That's what a sceptical reader does, and it works on news stories as well as Fortnite tips.
