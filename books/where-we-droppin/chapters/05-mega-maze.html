Chapter 5 · Mega Maze
A-maze-ing loot

South-east of Stone Sanctum, tucked into the jungle, is a giant hedge maze. Seen from the Battle Bus, it's a beautiful, intricate pattern of green walls and narrow paths, like a puzzle printed on the ground. Seen from inside, it's a lot more confusing.

Mega Maze isn't a big named town. It's a landmark, built on 20 August where a petrol station used to be, so it doesn't attract crowds. Most players never come here at all. The ones who do know something the others don't: hidden under this maze is a second Temple Vault, and it only needs one key.

The secret: the runaway key

At Stone Sanctum, the key containers sit still on their plinths. At Mega Maze, the key runs away. According to the Fortnite Wiki, the key is inside a small breakable container that moves around the maze on its own, and freezes when you get close. Destroy it and the key is yours.

Here's how to catch it:

  1. Spot it from above. The container is hard to see at ground level, but it follows a fixed route and leaves a trail. The best view is from the sky as you glide in, or from standing on top of the maze walls.
  2. Cut it off. If you miss it, don't chase it round in circles. It's on a loop, so it'll come back. Wait at a corner on its route.
  3. Smash it. When you get close, it stops. Hit it and grab the key.
  4. Take the key west. Climb the stairs on the west side of the maze to the pedestal there. Use the key, and a hidden door opens in the wall behind it.
  5. Go down. The passage leads to an underground chamber with two Rare Chests and two ordinary chests. One of the Rare Chests can hold one of the four Exotics from Chapter 2.

One container, many descriptions

Guides agree that the key moves, but they can't agree what it looks like. One calls it a glowing urn. Another says a floating beam of light. A third says a floating orb, and a fourth talks about moving braziers that you shoot. One guide even describes a completely different method, with the container hidden behind a wall that turns see-through at a dead end. Don't be gullible and believe the first guide you read. Go and look, and decide for yourself what you saw.

Watch your step

The maze has a nasty extra. Hidden on its paths are trap buttons. According to the Fortnite Wiki, standing on a button for two seconds switches on a trap, and it stays on until the player on the button steps off. Anyone standing in the trap takes 5 damage, again and again, so your health deteriorates fast if you don't get out.

Here's the clever part: the buttons work for anyone. That includes you. If another player is chasing the key through the maze, a well-timed trap can stop them in their tracks.

The key container is obstinate, too. It won't stop wandering until you're right next to it. Be just as obstinate back: if you miss it, wait for it to come round again rather than giving up.

The exit (yes, it's a toilet)

This is our favourite secret in the whole book. When you've emptied the vault, you don't have to walk back past the pedestal, where someone might be waiting. The vault has an emergency exit, and it's a portable toilet. Step inside and it carries you out to the maze's south entrance. Two separate sources confirm it, so we're confident it's real, as ridiculous as it sounds.

Now picture the player who spotted you going in, crouched at the top of the stairs, ready to ambush you on your way out. They wait. And wait. You never come back up. They'd have every right to feel indignant, but you'll be long gone, with two Rare Chests' worth of loot.

The mystery next door

Since the update on 15 October, a giant pirate chest has appeared on the coast east of Sunken Shores, not far from the maze. Nobody knows yet what it's for. Some players think it's a hint about what's coming next season. Keep an eye on it.

Pro Tip: Remember that Temple Vault Keys only work in their own vault. A key from Stone Sanctum won't open Mega Maze, and the other way round. But nothing stops you doing both in one match: the two vaults are close together, and a player who opens both will see their loadout flourish into something extraordinary.

One last thought. Fortnite's seasons don't last forever: this one ends at the start of November, and when the map changes, places like Mega Maze can vanish overnight. In a few months, you may feel quite nostalgic about the day a runaway key and a magic toilet won you a match. So go and enjoy it while it's here.
