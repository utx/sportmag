The squadmate everyone wants

If you play in a duo or squad, your character matters even more. The best teammates aren't always the best shots. They're the ones who make the whole team better.

  • A benevolent teammate shares. They hand over a spare Shield Potion when you're low, and they let the player with no weapon take the first one they find.
  • An affable teammate keeps everyone calm. Even when the storm is closing in and the squad is down to one player, their voice stays friendly.
  • A candid teammate tells the truth: "I'm on 20 health, I can't take that fight." Honest callouts save matches.

And the teammate nobody wants? The arrogant one who charges in alone because they're sure they're the best, or the gullible one who leads the squad across the map to a "secret" from a video that turned out to be made up. Don't be either of those.

From drop to Victory Royale

A great drop gives you a head start, but it doesn't win the match by itself. Three rules for what comes next:

  1. Leave on time. The moment the first storm circle appears, work out how far away you are. The players who loot "just one more chest" are the ones the storm catches.
  2. Heal before you move. Never walk into the next fight with half a shield if you have potions in your pocket.
  3. Take the high ground. Whoever is higher up can see more and is harder to hit. It's been true since the very first season.

Pro Tip: Try all five drops at least twice before you pick a favourite. One match can be bad luck. Two matches are evidence. (That's a sceptical scientist's rule, and it works for Fortnite too.)
