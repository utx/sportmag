Chapter 2 · Heatwave Harbor
Get your ducks in a row

Picture a sunny seaside city: pastel apartment blocks, palm trees, neon signs and a long bridge glittering over turquoise water. That's Heatwave Harbor, and it's clearly inspired by Miami in Florida. It's built on two islands of an archipelago, joined by a bridge, with shops called Flying Fish Tacos, Electric Flamingo and Crabby Cabana.

Between the buildings run quiet canals. Before the Battle Bus arrives, the water is perfectly tranquil, with little boats bobbing at their moorings. It won't stay that way for long, because Heatwave Harbor has one of the best-loved secrets on the Island.

Why land here?

Heatwave Harbor is one of the most well-rounded drops on the map. It has 37 chests, 36 ammo boxes, three vending machines and a Reboot Van. There are cars and water everywhere, so it's easy to get away. And there are two Override Consoles close by, one to the north and one to the east.

It isn't as wild as WonkeeLand, but it isn't sleepy either. It suits a shrewd player: someone who lands with a plan, loots quickly, and is ready to grab the big prize when the moment comes. And the big prize here is a race.

The secret: the Duck Race

Yes, really. On a small bridge at the north-east edge of Heatwave Harbor, four rubber ducks sit in a neat little row on the southern side. They look like decoration. They're not. Here's how the race works, according to the guides that have tested it:

The Duck Race in four steps
1
FIND THE DUCKS
Four rubber ducks on the little bridge at the north-east edge of town.
2
HOLD TO START
Hold the interact button. The ducks splash into the water and they're off.
3
FOLLOW THEM
They crash through three stacks of boxes on the way, and each one spills loot.
4
CATCH THE PRIZE
At the finish, in the south of town, cannons blast the rewards into the air.
The race route (our sketch, not to scale)

What do you win?

This is where the race has changed over time. Last season the prize was a Mythic shotgun. Epic took that away when this season began on 20 August, and on 17 September it brought in something new. According to the Fortnite Wiki, finishing the race now rewards you with one of four Exotic weapons: normal weapons with a superpower added.

The possible prizes

One guide is a little more cautious and says an Exotic "may drop" at the finish, so don't be shocked if the cannons are stingy once in a while. Either way, the boxes along the route are worth it on their own.

Racing like a pro

The ducks are completely indifferent to your plans. They don't care whether you've got a weapon, whether you're on low health, or whether a squad is creeping up behind you. They just float. So the planning is up to you.

  • Arm yourself first. The race is a magnet for other players, because everyone knows what's at the finish. If you start it empty-handed, you may inadvertently be throwing a party for every player nearby, with you as the snack.
  • Grab the box loot as you go. Each crash spills items into the water. Scoop them up while you keep pace.
  • Be at the finish before the ducks are. If other players have spotted the race, their arrival may coincide with the cannons firing. Get into position early and make them come to you.
  • Don't let traffic stop you. Boats, cars and other players will all impede you in the narrow streets. Swimming along the canal is often the clearest path.
