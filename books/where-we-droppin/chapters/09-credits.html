About this book

Where We Droppin'? is an unofficial fan book made by The Chug Jug, a fan magazine about Fortnite. It isn't made, approved or endorsed by Epic Games. It's free, and it will never ask you to spend money. If you ever want to buy anything in Fortnite, ask a grown-up first.

Version

This book describes Fortnite Battle Royale in Chapter 7, Season 4: Override, during Fortnitemares, as the Island was on 17 October 2026 (game version 42.40). The season ends at the start of November, and the map, the vaults and the prizes may all change after that.

Pictures

The Island map, the close-ups of each drop (cut from that map), the item and weapon icons, the gliders, the Override Console and the lobby background are by Epic Games / Fortnite, used under Epic's Fan Content Policy, via fortnite-api.com. The drop cards, step boards, route sketches, vault diagrams, charts and cover design were made for this book by The Chug Jug. We haven't shown pictures of characters that belong to other companies.

Where the facts came from

Chest counts, vault contents, the Duck Race prizes, the maze traps, the toilet exit, Override Console rules and golden fishing: the Fortnite Wiki (fortnite.weirdgloop.org), checked against the guides below.

The Temple Vaults: Nintendo Life, "Fortnite Vault locations in Chapter 7 Season 4: Override"; Beebom, "All Fortnite Vaults in Chapter 7 Season 4 and How to Open Them"; AllThingsHow and The Click.

The Duck Race: Games.gg and The Click (how the race works); AllThingsHow, "Fortnite Override: All Exotic Weapon Locations" (the Season 4 prize).

WonkeeLand: the Fortnite Wiki; TechWiser, "6 Best Landing Spots in Fortnite Chapter 7 Season 4 Override"; Epic's own post about WonkeeLand's roller-coaster (the Chapter Seven Playground update); Timesaver.gg (Fortnitemares changes).

The quiet east island and the Slurp Trucks: Dexerto, "Best landing spots in Fortnite Chapter 7 Season 4", and the Island map itself. The Slurp Truck shield amount comes from Dexerto alone.

The v42.40 update and the pirate chest: Beebom, "Fortnite Update 42.40 Patch Notes".

Landing numbers from tournaments, and item stats: our earlier book Tier Pressure, which drew on Elite Esports, Dexerto, Beebom and the Fortnite Wiki.

Hedge mazes (Training Ground, Round 2): general reference sources on Hampton Court Maze and on maze-solving.

What we left out, and why

Some drop guides promise secrets that we couldn't find any evidence for on the current Island: a barbell you can only lift after eating four steaks, a "Cats Only" vault door, a bedroom drawer that acts as a chest, and loot cannons that fire out stacks of weapons at WonkeeLand. Several of the places those secrets were said to be (Ripped Tides, Classified Canyon, Clawsy Lodge and Frigid Fortress) were on earlier versions of the Island and aren't on the map this season. We've left all of them out. If any of them turn up in a future season, we'll be the first to check.

Play it safe

  • Nobody is giving away free V-Bucks. If a website, video or message offers them, it's a scam.
  • Never share your password or your account with anyone, not even a friend.
  • This book never recommends cheats, hacks or glitches. ("Cheat Codes" in this book are an official feature of the game.)
  • Take breaks. The Island will still be there after dinner.
