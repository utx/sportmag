Chapter 9
Sprite fight

They're small. They're cute. Some of them have sunglasses. And they might be the most important thing you carry. Sprites are this season's collectable creatures, and each one gives you a special power while it's equipped.

Sprites in 60 seconds

  • Equip one and it rides on your back (it replaces your Back Bling), giving you its power. You can carry up to five more in your inventory, but only the equipped one works.
  • Level it up from Level 1 to Level 5 ("Mastered") by opening containers, knocking and eliminating opponents, and cracking Cheat Codes. Each level makes its power stronger.
  • Don't lose it. If you're eliminated, you lose every Sprite you're carrying, unless you've extracted it first. Extracting saves a Sprite forever. You can do it at an Extraction Site, with a Portable Extractor, or by winning the match.
  • Find them in chests, Pumpkin Chests, Cheat Codes, vaults, and from bosses.
  • Variants are special versions with an extra bonus. During Fortnitemares there are Trick or Treat variants (find candy in containers), plus Bounty Hunter, Gold, Cheat Master and Loot Hacker ones.
  • Sprites aren't allowed in Ranked at the moment. Epic took them out in August.

The S tier

Every time you deal damage, you siphon some of it back as health. The higher the level, the more you heal. It turns every fight you're winning into a fight you'll win on full health, and it's especially strong in Zero Build, where long fights are common.

Simple and brilliant: it gives you an extra layer of Overshield, 10 points per level, up to a whole extra 50 when it's Mastered. More shield means you survive more fights. No buttons, no thinking: it's a passive power that's always working.

Every so often, it marks nearby enemies so you can see where they are. As it levels up, it reaches further and pings more often. Knowing where your enemy is before they know where you are is one of the biggest advantages in Fortnite. Designed by a fan in the #DesignASprite competition.

The A tier

The most coveted Sprite on the Island. Jump, then jump again in mid-air, and you dash forward, phasing straight through some objects. You get up to three charges, which refill over time (faster as it levels up). In a game where moving well is everything, a dash you can use three times is enormous: dodge shots, close the gap on an enemy, or vanish round a corner.

So why only A tier? Because some experts think dashes are a trap in fights. Evolve Jake points out that you can't shoot while you're dashing, and the dash uses the same button as your glider and some other Sprites' powers, so it's easy to set it off by accident at the worst moment. Use it for moving around, not for fighting.

Shortly after you take damage, you get some health or shield back, and the amount grows as it levels up. It's one of the three Sprites you can pick when you start, and it's still excellent: free healing, every fight.

Jump just after you land for a Super Jump, with up to three charges. Higher jumps mean high ground, and high ground wins fights. Another fan design from #DesignASprite.

Aim while you're in the air and you enter Hangtime: you hang there for a moment with better accuracy. Perfect for jumping over a wall and landing a shot before you come down.

A big pink monster who gives you a random item every time it levels up. The catch: it only levels up when you eat or drink healing items (or collect Rings at Green Hill Zone). Heal a lot, get a lot.

The B tier

A raccoon who loves rubbish. Food heals you extra, and you'll find food when you jump out of hiding spots (like the Scare Crows at Freaky Fields) and sometimes in containers, including Golden Apples at higher levels.

Gives you Overdrive (unlimited sprint and extra speed) after you take storm damage. When Mastered, it shows you where the future storm circles will be, which is extremely useful, but only once you've levelled it all the way.

A rice ball with a face. Eat or drink anything and you get Overdrive for a few seconds. Lovely with a pocketful of candy.

Upgrades a random item in your inventory each time it levels up. When it's Mastered, it resets to Level 1 and is saved automatically. A solid starter.

The C tier

Every so often, a bush appears on you, so you can hide. At max level, you get a bush after each elimination too. Sneaky, but not very powerful.

Chests sometimes drop slices of birthday cake (10 health and 10 shield). At max level, eliminations drop cake too. Happy birthday to you, but not much help.

Guarantees an 8-Bit Shotgun in your next chest, and gives 8-Bit Shotguns a score multiplier. Fine if you love the 8-Bit; otherwise, there are better powers.

The rarest-looking Sprite, and the least useful in a fight. It only levels up when you win a match, and it gives you extra Crown Wins. A trophy, not a tool. (A very nice trophy, though.)

The golden rule of Sprites

Extract before you risk it. Levelled a Spooky Dash up to Level 4? Don't take it into the final fight unextracted. Find an Extraction Site or use a Portable Extractor first. Losing a Mastered Sprite hurts more than losing the match.
