Chapter 13
The Great Debates

Here's something surprising about ranking things: the experts don't agree. We read tier lists from big gaming websites and listened to famous Fortnite creators, and on some items they're miles apart. One website's S tier is another's C tier.

That isn't because some of them are wrong. It's because they play in different ways, in different modes, at different times in the season. A good debater listens to both sides, weighs up the evidence, and then makes up their own mind. Here are the biggest arguments of the season, and our verdict on each. You're the judge.

Debate 1: Pump or 8-Bit?

Shotgun showdown
Pump
  • 119 per shot (gold)
  • Headshot can one-shot anyone
  • Slow: miss and you wait
  • Best in Build
VS
8-Bit
  • 108 per shot (gold)
  • Fully automatic, 10 shells
  • Miss? Fire again
  • Loved in Zero Build

Team Pump: GameRant says the Pump is the best shotgun for Build mode, because a gold Pump headshot does 205 damage and eliminates anyone in one go. Dexerto ranks the Pump higher too, and dismisses the 8-Bit as "the guy who finished second in a two-man race".

Team 8-Bit: Beebom puts the 8-Bit in S tier, above the Pump, "for one specific reason: it's automatic". Evolve Jake mostly uses the 8-Bit (or the Sweeper) in Zero Build, because missing a shot or two "is not the end of the world".

Our verdict: They're both right, about different modes. Pump in Build, 8-Bit in Zero Build, and 8-Bit for anyone still learning. If you're good at aiming, the Pump's single huge shot is the most satisfying thing in Fortnite.

Debate 2: Is the Bonerattler actually good?

For: Evolve Jake says it's "incredible" even without its X-ray trick, and that its accuracy lets it go "toe-to-toe" with the Assault Rifle. Dexerto ranks it A and calls the wall-reveal "a huge edge". SypherPK tried it on launch day and said simply, "I love this SMG."

Against: Beebom's October tier list puts it all the way down in B tier, calling it merely "decent at mid-range", and suggests hunting for the Exotic version instead.

Our verdict: S tier. Its numbers are the best of any normal weapon, and seeing through walls is a superpower. We think Beebom is underrating it, but it's a good reminder that even experts disagree.

Debate 3: The Tactical Pistol: keep it or dump it?

Dump it: Beebom says "dump the Tactical Pistol as soon as you land", ranking it C.

Keep it: GameRant and Dexerto both rank it A tier. It's fully automatic, does double damage on headshots, and its DPS is almost as high as the Bonerattler's.

Our verdict: Keep it, at least until you find a Bonerattler. Look closely at Beebom's reason: it says the Drum Gun is "dominating mid-range". But the Drum Gun was vaulted on 1 October! When you weigh up evidence, always check whether it's still up to date.

Debate 4: Witch Broom: classic or clunky?

Classic: It's back for Fortnitemares, it smashes through anything above you, and it lets you glide anywhere on the map.

Clunky: Evolve Jake calls it "kind of an outdated mobility item". Weapons are so accurate now that floating slowly through the air can make you "a sitting duck", and in Zero Build you can already redeploy your glider.

Our verdict: A tier. Brilliant for long journeys and storm escapes; risky in the middle of a fight.

Debate 5: Should you land at Green Hill Zone?

Yes: At the start of the season, every big landing guide picked it first, thanks to its huge chest count and the Rings that give you free Overshield.

No: TG Plays says it's fun, "but if you want to win the game, you probably shouldn't land there". It's busy, and since the Fortnitemares update it no longer has a boss with a Mythic.

Our verdict: A tier. Go for the Rings if you're playing Zero Build; go somewhere quieter if you just want to win.

Debate 6: Spooky Dash: superpower or trap?

Superpower: It's the only Mythic Sprite with a power you can use in a fight, and three dashes through walls sounds unbeatable.

Trap: Evolve Jake says that when players use a dash mid-fight, it "hurts them more than it actually helps them", because you can't shoot while dashing and the dash shares a button with your glider.

Our verdict: A tier. Use it to move, not to fight.

Debate 7: Slap Candy Corn or Chug Jug?

Chug Jug: 200 points of healing, both bars, in one item. It's a legend for a reason.

Candy Corn: Evolve Jake says six Slap Candy Corn (180 healing, eaten in seconds, with unlimited sprint) is the healing he most wants right now, "a lot of people are sleeping on" it.

Our verdict: We're The Chug Jug, so we're biased. But honestly? In a hurry, take the candy. When you have time to hide and heal properly, nothing beats a Chug.

How to win any debate

The most persuasive arguments use three things: facts (numbers you can check), experience (what actually happens when you play), and expert opinion (what people who play all day say). And the best debaters change their mind when the evidence changes. Even when there's no consensus, you can still decide which argument is strongest.
