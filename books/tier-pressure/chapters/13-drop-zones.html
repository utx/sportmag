Landing spots
Where should you jump?

The bus horn honks. The doors open. A hundred players stare at the same map, and every one of them has about thirty seconds to make the most important decision of the match: where to land.

Land somewhere packed and you might be back in the lobby before you've found a gun. Land somewhere empty and you'll have loot, but the storm might chase you across the whole Island. The perfect drop is a compromise: good loot, a manageable number of enemies, and a short run to wherever the circle ends up.

We've ranked every named location on the map, plus the best smaller landmarks. To decide, we looked at how many chests each place has, what special rewards are there (vaults, bosses, Override Consoles), how busy it gets, and how close it is to the middle of the map.

How busy is busy?

One esports website studied more than 350 pro tournament matches from this season and counted where every player landed. Some of what they found was surprising: the places the guides called "hot drops" weren't always the busiest. Remember, though, that pros and ordinary players think differently. In public matches, the newest, most talked-about places are usually the most crowded. During Fortnitemares, that means Nightmare Neighborhood.

The S tier

An ancient temple with a maze behind it, where walls and platforms appear and vanish as you approach. It's lucrative: about 32 chests, plenty of ammo and vending machines. But the real treasure is underneath.

Find the two Temple Vault Keys and use them to open the Temple Vault. The fountain drains away to reveal a secret underground room full of rare chests, with a chance of something Exotic. Guides disagree about exactly where the keys turn up, so search the high towers and look out for anything glowing.

Here's the clever bit: even though it's one of the quickest places to reach from the Battle Bus, it's often surprisingly quiet. In pro matches, nobody landed there at all in more than a third of games. And because it sits near the centre, you're rarely far from the next circle.

Chug Jug tip: Land on a tower, not in the courtyard. You'll see who else is coming, and you'll have the high ground when they arrive.

The A tier

The headline spot of Fortnitemares: a haunted suburb of purple-lit streets, tombstones and jack-o'-lanterns, which replaced The Battlewoods. It has the most Pumpkin Chests of anywhere on the Island (each gives you a Sprite and a weapon), a graveyard where you can dig up keys, and locked rooms in a mansion basement.

It's also home to a boss borrowed from an old horror film. On loot alone this would be S tier. We've knocked it down for two reasons: it is the most contested spot in the game right now, so it's chaos, and it's genuinely spooky. If jump-scares aren't your thing, there are lots of brilliant places to land that don't have a villain in them.

Its secret weapon is the Rings. Each one you collect gives you 5 health, or 5 shield if your health is full, and they keep stacking up into extra Overshield. That's a huge advantage, especially in Zero Build, where you can't build walls to protect yourself.

A sunny, Miami-style city on two islands joined by a bridge. One guide called it "one of the most well-rounded landing spots", and we agree. It has good loot, two Override Consoles nearby, a chance of a boss, and water to escape into if things go wrong.

Look out for the Duck Race: win it, and you get an Exotic weapon as your prize.

A ski lodge with a cable car, high in the snowy north-west. It has one of the biggest chest counts on the map and more vending machines than anywhere else. The catch: pros love it, so you'll have company, and it's a long run from the middle if the circle goes south.

A beautiful orange-and-gold autumn forest with a research base hidden inside. More than 50 chests make it one of the richest spots in the east. It was the second busiest place in pro matches, so don't expect it to be secluded.

Geno's fortress, built to look like the inside of a gaming computer, with towers shaped like graphics cards, giant fans and glowing cooling pipes. It's central, quick to reach, has an Override Console next door and sometimes a boss. Because it's in the middle, players pass through all match long, so loot fast and be ready for visitors.

The B tier

A haunted farm with a cornfield maze and a giant pumpkin sitting in the sea. It replaced Latte Landing in the Fortnitemares update. It's packed with Pumpkin Chests and full of character, but it's so new that nobody has counted its chests yet. It could easily climb to A tier when they do.

An amusement park with a Ferris wheel, a roller-coaster and a water slide, now spookily haunted with water turned an unpleasant shade of green. One guide calls it "the chaos drop"; another says it's not very popular. Our advice: ride the Ferris wheel for a free lookout over the whole area.

A half-flooded town that's been squashed by giant falling Tetris blocks. It's calm, and the blocks make brilliant ready-made cover in Zero Build. The downside: it's short on ammo boxes, so you might run low.

A garage with a small racetrack on a desert cliff. Only a few buildings, but always a decent number of chests and some Pumpkin Chests nearby. It's a safe, quiet start, and its only real problem is that it's a long way from the middle.

The C tier

A fancy beach club with a duck-shaped yacht and a secret loot room. It has the fewest chests of any named location and a very long glide to get there. Only worth it if you're hunting Cheat Codes, which pop up here a lot.

Scare level warning

Two places on the map belong to Fortnitemares' horror guests: Nightmare Neighborhood (a horror-film villain) and Freddy Fazbear's Pizzeria, a restaurant full of creepy animatronic robots from the Five Nights at Freddy's games, which roam the Island as bosses. In pure loot terms, the Pizzeria is one of the richest landmarks. But if spooky isn't your thing, skip both. You'll still win plenty of games.

The Chug Jug landing plan

Want an easy start? Mega Maze or the little east island. Loot in peace, then head for the circle.

Want the best loot? Stone Sanctum. Learn where the vault keys are and you'll be the richest player on the Island.

Want a fight? Nightmare Neighborhood or Green Hill Zone. Grab a weapon the moment you land, and don't stop moving.

Playing Zero Build? Green Hill Zone's Rings and their free Overshield are worth the risk.
