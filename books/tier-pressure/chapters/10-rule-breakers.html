Overrides: change the rules

Imagine if, halfway through a football match, someone could run to the side of the pitch, press a button, and announce: "From now on, every goal counts double!" That's what an Override does to a game of Fortnite. It's the big idea of this whole season, and it makes every match a little bit unpredictable.

How Overrides work

An Override Console, waiting for someone to take control. Picture: Epic Games / Fortnite.

  1. Consoles switch on during the match. There are seven Override Consoles around the Island (they're on the map in the Drop It Like It's Hot chapter). Each one has a chance to switch on when the first, second or third storm circle begins. They're guarded by robot NITE Unit guards, and each has a chest.
  2. The first player to reach it picks. They're offered three random Overrides and choose one. The other two disappear.
  3. It changes the rules for everybody. Most Overrides affect every player in the match, for the rest of the match. And everyone finds out who did it, because the chooser's name appears around the console.
  4. Squad Overrides are different. Rarer "Squad" versions only help your team. The consequence: your whole squad gets marked as "Hackers" on everybody's map for the rest of the game. That's a gamble.

Overrides are free: no Gold Bars, no Sprite Dust. They're switched off in Ranked.

Just for fun

Big Head Mode gives everyone an enormous head. It doesn't help you win. It is, however, very funny. Spooky Prop Hunt lets players crouch, stay still, and turn into a Halloween prop. Every pumpkin you see is now a suspect.

Should you even touch the console?

Activating a console tells the whole lobby where you are, and other players often rush to consoles to fight whoever's there. Here's our rule: only take an Override if you're healed up, you've got good loot, and you can leave quickly afterwards. Grab it, then get moving. Don't hang around admiring your handiwork.
