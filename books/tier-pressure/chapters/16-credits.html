About this book

Tier Pressure is an unofficial fan book made by The Chug Jug, a fan magazine about Fortnite. It isn't made, approved or endorsed by Epic Games. It's free, and it will never ask you to spend money. If you ever want to buy anything in Fortnite, ask a grown-up first.

Portions of the materials used are trademarks and/or copyrighted works of Epic Games, Inc. All rights reserved by Epic. This material is not official and is not endorsed by Epic.

Version

This book describes Fortnite Battle Royale in Chapter 7, Season 4: Override, with the Fortnitemares update (version 42.30) on the Island, as it was on 12 October 2026. Fortnite changes often. The next update was due on 15 October 2026, and some items, stats and rankings may change after that.

Pictures

Item icons, outfit pictures, the Island map, the Override Console and the lobby backgrounds are by Epic Games / Fortnite, used under Epic's Fan Content Policy. They came from the game's own art, via fortnite-api.com, Epic's content service, and the Fortnite Wiki's copies of the in-game icons. Tier boards, charts, cards, the tier map and the cover design were made for this book by The Chug Jug.

We haven't shown pictures of characters or items that belong to other companies (from crossovers with other games, films and TV shows).

Where the facts came from

Stats and patch details: Epic Games' official update notes (v42.00, v42.10, v42.20 and v42.30); the Fortnite Wiki (fortnite.weirdgloop.org) and the Fortnite Fandom wiki; fortnite-api.com.

Tier lists and guides (opinions quoted in this book): Beebom (weapon tier list, 8 October 2026), Dexerto (weapon tier list, 1 October 2026), GameRant (Override meta tier list), plus guides from TechWiser, Sportskeeda, DigitalTQ, The Click, Nintendo Life, Technerdiness and Elite Esports (landing data from more than 350 pro matches).

Creators (videos made since the season began): Evolve Jake, SypherPK, TG Plays and Top5Gaming. Quotes are taken from what they said in their videos.

The tiers are our own opinion, based on all of the above. Where the experts disagreed, we said so.
