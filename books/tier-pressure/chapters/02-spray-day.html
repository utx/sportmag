Chapter 2
Assault rifles

If you could only carry one weapon for a whole match, it would probably be an assault rifle. They're the all-rounders of Fortnite: accurate enough for medium range, fast enough for close fights, and reliable everywhere in between. Before we rank them, here's how to read a weapon's stats like a pro.

How to read weapon stats

StatWhat it means
DamageHow much one bullet takes off an enemy's shield and health, if it hits their body.
HeadshotHow much one bullet does if it hits their head. Usually 1.5 to 2 times as much. Aim high!
Fire rateHow many shots the weapon fires each second.
MagazineHow many shots before you have to reload.
DPS"Damage per second": damage × fire rate. It tells you how quickly a weapon can wear an enemy down if every shot hits.

The numbers in this book are for the Legendary (gold) version of each weapon, unless we say otherwise. Lower rarities do a little less damage and reload a little more slowly. A Common (grey) Assault Rifle does 30 damage per shot; the Legendary does 36.

Wait, the Pump Shotgun is last?

Yes, and that's why DPS isn't everything. The Pump fires slowly, so its damage per second is low. But its damage per shot is enormous: up to 119 to the body, and 205 to the head. One perfect Pump shot can do more damage than a whole second of Assault Rifle fire. Different weapons are good at different jobs. Keep reading.

Fortnite's oldest weapon is still one of its best. It's accurate (the first shot goes exactly where you aim), it fires quickly, and it holds 25 rounds. It's good up close, good at medium range, and you'll find it everywhere. Several big guides rank it as one of the top picks of the season.

In an update in early September it got slightly weaker (smaller headshot bonus, slower reload), but it's still the most reliable gun on the Island.

Chug Jug tip: Don't hold the trigger down at long range. Tap it, one shot at a time, and every bullet goes where you aim.

The bigger, slower, harder-hitting cousin of the Assault Rifle. Each bullet does more damage (44 at Legendary), and a headshot does a formidable 70 or so, but it fires more slowly and has more recoil. It came back for Fortnitemares with some tweaks, including less kick when you aim down the sights. (Sources disagree on a couple of its exact numbers, so we have rounded them.)

If you're good at aiming for heads, it might be even better than the normal AR. If you're still practising, the normal AR is more forgiving.

A semi-automatic rifle (one shot per trigger press) that loses hardly any damage at long range, and its headshots do double damage. With no sniper rifles in the game right now, this is the best long-range weapon there is. The catch: you can only find it if you've unlocked it as a Loot Hack in the lobby, and the current Loot Hacks change on 15 October.

Sixty bullets before you reload, and no damage lost at long range at all. Brilliant for spraying at someone hiding behind a wall, or for knocking down builds. But each bullet is weak, and the reload takes ages (3.6 seconds, even at Legendary). Another Loot Hack, so you'll only see it if you've unlocked it.
