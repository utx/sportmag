Every Fortnite player has opinions. Strong ones. Loud ones. The kind that start arguments in the lobby and finish them in the storm. Which shotgun is best? Is the Bonerattler secretly amazing or secretly rubbish? Should you drop at the hottest spot on the map, or somewhere so quiet you can hear the birds?

This book settles those arguments. Well, most of them. We've taken every weapon, healing item, gadget, Sprite, Override, vehicle and landing spot in Battle Royale right now and put each one in its place, from the best of the best to the stuff you should leave on the floor for somebody else.

That's the pressure part. Every item in this book had to prove itself. Some rose to the top. Some crumbled. Welcome to Tier Pressure.

How the tiers work

How we decided

Rankings can be subjective: one player's favourite is another player's rubbish. So we didn't just go with our gut. Every ranking in this book is based on three kinds of evidence:

  1. The numbers. Damage, fire rate, magazine size, how much an item heals, how long it takes to use. Numbers don't have opinions.
  2. The experts. We read and listened to dozens of guides, tier lists and videos from well-known Fortnite creators and gaming websites, made since this season started.
  3. The players. We looked at what thousands of ordinary players say in Fortnite communities online, because they play it every day.

When all three agree, the verdict is easy. When they don't, we tell you, and there's a whole chapter near the end called The Great Debates about the biggest arguments.

Build or Zero Build?

Fortnite has two main ways to play Battle Royale. In Build, you can put up walls, ramps and floors in a split second. In Zero Build, you can't, so you hide behind trees, rocks and buildings instead. Some items are much better in one mode than the other: a wall you can build in half a second changes everything. Where it matters, we'll say so.

The small print (it's important)

Fortnite changes all the time. This book covers Chapter 7, Season 4: Override, as it was on 12 October 2026, with the Fortnitemares update (version 42.30) on the Island. The next update, 42.40, is due on 15 October, and Epic can add, remove, strengthen or weaken anything whenever it likes. If something in the game doesn't match the book, the game has changed, not your eyes.

Stats in this book come from the game's files and patch notes, as collected by Fortnite fans online. The tiers are our opinion, backed by evidence. You're allowed to disagree. In fact, we'd be disappointed if you didn't, at least once.
