Chapter 5
Explosives and oddities

Some weapons don't fit in a neat box. They explode, they swing, they shoot pumpkins, or they belong to a famous character from another game. This chapter is for the oddballs, and one of them is among the most destructive weapons on the Island.

A rocket launcher that fires a grinning jack-o'-lantern. It's slow, it holds one shot at a time, and the projectile takes a moment to arrive. But when it lands, it does 137 damage to anyone caught in the blast and an enormous 330 damage to builds. In Build mode, that means you can blow a hole in someone's fort in one shot.

Beebom's October tier list puts it in S tier, and Evolve Jake says it's useful "every single time it comes into the loot pool". We'd agree, except for one thing: right now, you can only find it if you've unlocked it as a Loot Hack. It's also less useful in Zero Build, where there are no builds to destroy.

Chug Jug tip: Aim at the ground near your enemy's feet, not at their body. Splash damage still hurts, and the ground is much harder to miss.

The giant key-shaped sword from the Kingdom Hearts games arrived in the mid-season update. You can swing it in a three-hit combo, attack from the air, and cast "Thundaga", a lightning spell, every 16 seconds. While you hold it you float with low gravity, and every elimination with it drops healing orbs.

It's huge fun. But a sword against a shotgun is a brave choice, and most experts call it a novelty: Dexerto ranks it C, Beebom B. The Mythic version is better, and you get one for each squad member from the Squad Level Up Override.

Mega Man's famous arm cannon fires quick shots or a big charged blast, and you can shoot while sprinting. Normally it's handy for blasting players hiding behind cover. But at the moment it has a malfunction: since the Fortnitemares update, its explosion doesn't spread damage to players nearby. Evolve Jake recommends avoiding it until it's fixed, and he expects the fix to arrive with the 15 October update.

Left out on purpose

Two more special weapons are in the game right now, but they come from horror films, so we haven't ranked them or recommended them:

  • Freddy's Nightmare: claws from A Nightmare on Elm Street, dropped by a horror-film villain boss at Nightmare Neighborhood. It's Legendary (some guides wrongly call it Mythic). SypherPK called it "the most powerful thing they've ever added"; Dexerto thinks its damage is too low to be a real threat.
  • Last Call: a knife from the Scream films, available as a Loot Hack. Some players use it to scan for nearby enemies.

If you see them in a match, you'll know what they are. You'll also know you can win perfectly well without them.
