Chapter 4
SMGs and pistols

SMGs (submachine guns) and pistols are the sprinters of the weapon world. Each bullet is small, but they fire so fast that, up close, they shred shields in a blink. They're perfect for chasing someone who's trying to escape, or for finishing a fight after a shotgun blast.

This season the category has a brand-new star, and it might be the most fun weapon in the game.

The star of Fortnitemares, and it has an ingenious trick. Aim down its sights and it switches on a purple flashlight that shows enemies' skeletons through walls, if they're close: Evolve Jake measured the range at about 13.5 metres in normal matches (it's much shorter in Ranked). It's eerie, it's brilliant, and it means nobody can hide from you behind a door.

It would be a great SMG even without the X-ray vision. It has the highest DPS of any normal weapon in the game (212 at Legendary), it's agile, and a hotfix the day after it launched made it fire even faster. With the Drum Gun vaulted, it's the only regular SMG in the loot pool right now, and it's earned its place.

Chug Jug tip: Before you push into a house, aim down sights at the wall. If you see a skeleton, you know exactly where they're hiding.

Don't let the word "pistol" fool you. This is fully automatic and plays like a little SMG, with double damage on headshots, a speedy reload, and the ability to aim while reloading. At Legendary, its DPS (207) is almost as high as the Bonerattler's. A fantastic early-game pick-up and an excellent supplement to a shotgun.

A pair of crossbow-pistols, one in each hand. Every press of the trigger fires both. They look fantastic and hit fairly hard, but they're harder to aim well than the Tactical Pistol, and they fire more slowly. SypherPK tried them on launch day and decided they have "never been good", and Beebom ranks them C. We agree. On 3 October, everybody got a pair for free during a special "Bounty Hunter" Power Hour. Sources disagree on their exact damage after a quick tweak, so we've rounded it.

In Zero Build, everybody lands with this little pistol, so you're never completely defenceless. It's weak, and it disappears if you drop it, but it can win you the first fight of the match if the other player landed on an empty floor. Swap it out the moment you find anything better.

Want an even spookier Bonerattler? The Exotic Marker Bonerattler also marks the enemies you hit so your whole team can see them. It's in Mythic Proportions, along with the Exotic Accelerant Drum Gun and the Mythic Midas' Masterpiece golden pistol.
