Chapter 8
Gadgets, gizmos and getaways

Not everything worth carrying shoots bullets. This chapter is about the items that move you, save you, find you treasure, or bring you back from the dead. Pros call them utility: things that are useful in lots of different ways.

Why do they matter so much? Because the storm never stops closing in. The player who can move fastest, and get out of trouble most easily, survives the longest. In Fortnite, a good escape is worth more than a good gun.

The S tier

An extra life, like in an old arcade game. Carry one and, when you get eliminated, you're resurrected: you come back through a rift, with all your loot and 100 health. It doesn't even take up an inventory slot. You'll find them in Llama Supply Drops, at the top of the Tetris Tower rift, and in chests during Saturday Power Hours.

In Zero Build, you can't build ramps to escape, so this is your ramp. It blasts you (and anyone nearby) through the air, and because it's a "pocket" item, it recharges instead of running out. The first recharge takes 60 seconds, then 40.

Throw it and it knocks everyone nearby flying. Anyone caught in it also gets Overdrive for ten seconds: unlimited sprinting, a little extra speed, and no fall damage until they land. Throw it at your own feet to escape; throw it at an enemy to fling them off a cliff. Wonderfully versatile. GameRant calls it "by far the best mobility item in the game", and Evolve Jake agrees. You can even buy them from vending machines.

The A tier

Back for Fortnitemares, and the best way to get around the Island. Use the broom and it blasts you high into the sky (smashing anything in the way), then lets you glide wherever you like. Press fire and you drop straight to the ground with no fall damage.

That makes it perfect for escaping a fight that's going wrong, crossing the map when the storm is coming, or swooping down on someone from above. It has a 20-second cooldown and only a limited number of uses, so don't waste it on sightseeing.

Why not S tier? Not everyone's a fan. Evolve Jake thinks it's "kind of an outdated mobility item": weapons are so accurate now that hovering slowly through the air can make you "a sitting duck". It's still great for crossing the map; just don't fly it straight over a crowd.

Throw it down and a bubble appears that bullets and explosions can't get through, for ten seconds. In Zero Build, that's ten seconds to heal, reload or think. There's also a pocket version that recharges.

Throw it, and a little metal bunker builds itself around you. It's the closest thing Zero Build players get to building, and it's brilliant for the final circles.

Use it to call down a loot llama on a balloon, packed with high-tier weapons, healing, ammo and Gold Bars. Shoot the balloon to make it land faster. You get these from quests (three on Thursdays!) rather than chests.

Lets you extract your Sprite anywhere, in five seconds, so you keep it forever even if you're eliminated afterwards. If you've just levelled up a rare Sprite, this is how you make sure you don't lose it.

The best vehicle on the Island, and it's a boat. Four seats, lots of health, and a missile launcher on the front. It can even drive on land, though it slowly takes damage while it does. With lakes, rivers and an ocean all around, a boat is a fast, safe way to reposition.

A pocket bounce pad. Throw it, jump on it, and spring high into the air. It recharges after 30 seconds, which is quick, and it's Evolve Jake's favourite pocket item for getting around the map.

The B tier

Opens the secret vaults under Stone Sanctum and Mega Maze, full of rare chests that can contain Exotic weapons. You can only carry one at a time. Not useful on its own, but it unlocks some of the best loot in the game.

Found at Nightmare Neighborhood, where it opens secret locked rooms in the mansion. Spooky, and rewarding.

Fishing is one of the calmest ways to get healing, and golden fishing spots can even give weapons. You can also hook items, or players, and reel them towards you. The Pro version (the Rare one) catches better fish.

Creates a Cheat Code somewhere nearby, inside the safe zone, and shows you where it is. Cheat Codes give you Sprites, so this is a Sprite-finding machine.

Buries a Sprite Chest nearby, and it's guaranteed to contain a Sprite you haven't found yet. A collector's dream.

A sleek sports car. Very fast on roads, not so great off them, and it only fits two. It needs fuel, so keep an eye on the gauge.

A chunky four-seater SUV. Slower than the Whiplash but much better on rough ground, and the whole squad fits in.

A Fortnitemares pumpkin bucket. Throw it and it smashes open, spilling a pile of candy (including the excellent Slap Candy Corn). You earn these from Fortnitemares quests.

Sonic's famous shoes, borrowed from the Sonic games. They let you spin-dash into enemies and attack in mid-air. Fun, but the even better Mythic version left with the Fortnitemares update. No picture: it belongs to another company.

The C tier

Makes a cloud of smoke for 15 seconds. Handy for crossing open ground, but enemies can still spray into it.

Wear a bush and become a bush. It's the oldest trick in Fortnite, and some players still fall for it. The disguise breaks the moment you take damage.

Makes the Extraction Crate arrive almost instantly at an Extraction Site, instead of after about 45 seconds. Useful, but a Portable Extractor does the same job anywhere.

What happened to the vans?

Reboot Vans, where you bring eliminated teammates back, used to be driveable. In the Fortnitemares update they were parked for good. They now stay in one place, and only appear in team modes.
