Chapter 7
Heals and shields

Here's a secret the best players know: matches aren't won by the player who shoots best. They're won by the player who heals best. Win a fight with 12 health left and you're one stray bullet from the lobby. Win it, then calmly top yourself back up to 100 health and 100 shield, and you're ready for whatever comes next.

In Fortnite you have two bars. Health (green) goes up to 100. Shield (blue) sits on top of it, up to another 100, and takes damage first. Some items fix one bar, some fix the other, and the very best fix both. We've ranked them all, by how much they heal, how fast they work, and how easy they are to find.

The S tier

What else was going to be at the top of a book made by The Chug Jug? But we promise it isn't favouritism. The Chug Jug is the most potent healing item in the game: it replenishes both your health and your shield, all the way to full, from almost nothing.

The catch is time. After a two-second pause, it pours in 10 points at a time for ten seconds, so a full chug takes about 12 seconds. The good news is you can keep moving while you drink, and it stops as soon as you're full, so you don't waste time.

Chug Jug tip: Never drink it in the open. Get behind a wall, a rock, or (in Build) inside a little box of walls first. Twelve seconds is a long time to be vulnerable.

The big blue potion. It gives you 50 shield over about five seconds, and you can carry three, which is enough to go from no shield to a full 100 and still have one spare. Shield is the first thing an enemy has to chew through, so starting every fight with full shield is the single best habit you can build.

Fun fact: right now there's a bug that can leave you glowing blue for the rest of the match after you drink one. Epic says it's fixing it in the next update. Until then, enjoy your glow-up.

"The snack that powers your back!" These crunchy duck-shaped treats returned for Fortnitemares, and they're sneakily brilliant. Fifty health or shield in just two seconds is already very good, but each one also levels up your equipped Sprite by one level. That's like getting a free power-up with your snack.

The surprise star of Fortnitemares. Each piece heals 30 in under a second and gives you "Slap": unlimited stamina, so you can sprint for 20 seconds without slowing down. That sounds small, but they come in stacks of six: 180 healing in one inventory slot, eaten almost as fast as you can press the button.

Zero Build expert Evolve Jake calls it "actually incredible" and says it's the healing item he most wants to carry right now, even over a Chug Jug. You'll find it in Golden Rifts, from the Snack-O'-Lantern, and as one of this fortnight's Loot Hacks.

The A tier

The classic first-aid kit restores a full 100 health. It starts slowly and speeds up as it goes, and you can walk while it works. If you get interrupted, you keep whatever it had already healed. It only fixes health, not shield, which is why it sits just below the potions.

The "mini". Each one gives 25 shield in two seconds, but only up to 50 shield. Drink two minis first, then a big Shield Potion on top, and you'll reach a full 100 in the fastest possible time. Minis are also the quickest heal to sneak in during a fight.

Heals 25, gives you a speed boost and unlimited sprinting for 20 seconds. Eat one with your whole squad at the same time for a "Taco Time party". You earn these from quests and special rewards rather than finding them in chests.

The best thing you can catch with a fishing rod. Forty points of health or shield (it fills whichever you need) in one second. Fishing for a few of these is a sneaky way to stock up before the final circles.

The B tier

A fish made of shield. Forty shield in a single second is excellent, but you need a fishing rod and some patience to get one.

The orange fish with the googly eyes. Forty health in one gulp. Faster than a Med Kit, but it heals less in total.

Heals you for 20, and also heals any teammates standing next to you. Small, but generous.

A shiny golden apple that heals 50 health and makes you float like you're on the Moon for nine seconds. Rare, and great fun.

A Zero Build pocket item. Plonk it down and it sprinkles shield over anyone standing close to it, for 30 seconds. Brilliant for squads who need to top up together, as long as nobody shoots the keg.

A treat from the Kingdom Hearts games. It makes a golden circle that heals you and your squad by 35. It's from another company's game, so we can't show you a picture.

The C tier

Candy Corn, Jelly Beans and Hop Drops pour out of Pumpkin Chests, Trick-or-Treat Cheat Codes and Snack-O'-Lanterns. Each piece only heals 10 (Hop Drops just 5, but they make you float). They add up, though, and with the Sugar Rush Override switched on, candy suddenly becomes far more powerful.

Apples (5 health), Peppers (5 health and a burst of speed) and Cabbages (10 health, slowly) are the healthy option, found in produce boxes. Not much healing, but it's free sustenance. With the Dumpster Dive Sprite equipped, food heals extra.

A little fish that heals 25 health, but only up to 75. Better than nothing.

Ten health and ten shield. You can't carry it: you eat it straight off the floor. Delicious, but small.

The D tier

Poor old bandages. They take three and a half seconds to heal just 15 health, and they can't take you above 75. You can at least throw one to a teammate. Pick them up if your pockets are empty; drop them the moment you find anything else.

The perfect heal routine

Got space and a moment to breathe? Do it in this order:

  1. Two Small Shield Potions (fast, and they only work up to 50, so use them first).
  2. One big Shield Potion on top, taking you to 100 shield.
  3. Med Kit or fish to fix your health.
  4. Save the Chug Jug for an emergency, when both bars are nearly empty.
