Loadout lab

You've got five inventory slots. That's it. Five. Every item in this book is fighting for one of them, and choosing well is a skill all of its own. A good loadout (the set of items you carry) covers every situation: something for close range, something for further away, a way to heal, and a way to escape.

The five-slot rule

Almost every great loadout follows the same pattern:

  1. A shotgun for close fights.
  2. A spray weapon (SMG, pistol or assault rifle) for finishing people off and for medium range.
  3. Something for longer range (an assault rifle does the job this season, because there are no sniper rifles).
  4. Healing: ideally shields and health.
  5. Mobility or a flex slot: Overdrive Grenades, a launcher, extra heals, whatever suits you.

The best loadouts have synergy. Evolve Jake points out that the new Bonerattler SMG has made the Pump Shotgun better: you fire one big Pump shot, then switch to the Bonerattler to keep the damage coming while the Pump gets ready again. Two good weapons that make each other great.

How to choose when your pockets are full

You'll constantly find new things, and you'll have to decide what to drop. Prioritise like this:

  • Never drop your only shotgun for a second spray weapon.
  • Higher rarity usually wins when it's the same kind of weapon. A gold 8-Bit beats a green Pump for most players.
  • Always keep at least one healing item, even if it's only a few bandages.
  • Mobility wins games. If you're carrying three guns and no way to escape, swap one for Overdrive Grenades.
  • Adapt as the match goes on. Early on, take whatever you find. By the final circles, you want your favourite five.
