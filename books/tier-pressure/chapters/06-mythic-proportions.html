Chapter 6
Exotics, Mythics and bosses

Every weapon so far comes in the usual colours: grey, green, blue, purple and gold. This chapter is about the two rarest kinds of all. Exotics are normal weapons with a superpower added. Mythics are the rarest of the rare, usually carried by a boss or hidden somewhere special. Find one and the whole lobby should be apprehensive.

Where to find Exotics

You can't buy Exotics this season. They're elusive: each of these places has a chance of giving you one.

  • Vaults: the rare chests inside the secret vaults under Stone Sanctum and Mega Maze. You'll need a Temple Vault Key.
  • The Duck Race at Heatwave Harbor: win it and the prize is an Exotic.
  • The Tetris Tower: a special event that appears in some matches. Climb to the top for a loot rift.
  • Two special chests: one in the attic of a green-roofed house at Nightmare Neighborhood, and one on top of the giant pumpkin off the coast at Freaky Fields.

The S tier

An exquisite solid gold pistol that belonged to Midas, one of Fortnite's most legendary characters. It fires one golden bullet at a time, and a headshot does 313 damage, which eliminates anyone, even with full health and shield. It also does ten times damage to bosses. The catch: you only get seven bullets, and then it disappears.

Usually there's only one in a match. It arrives in a special event called the Midas' Masterpiece Drop, a loot rift that appears inside the circle. When you see it on the map, everyone else has seen it too. Dexerto ranks it S and says it "has no business being in a battle royale". We agree on both counts.

Take the best shotgun in the game and make it grow stronger. Every time you hit someone, the next shot does 5 more damage, up to 25 extra. Keep the hits coming and it goes from 119 to 144 per shot. The bonus fades if you stop dealing damage for five seconds or switch weapons.

Everything great about the Bonerattler (see Small but Mighty), plus one more trick: every enemy you hit gets marked, so you and your teammates can see them. Beebom even suggests hunting for this one instead of the normal version. One of these waits in a special chest in the attic of a green-roofed house at Nightmare Neighborhood.

The A tier

A souped-up Assault Rifle with a little more damage and a faster reload, and Dexerto calls it the one gun "that just works at basically any range". On stats alone it's S tier. So why A? Because it may be almost impossible to get right now. It was carried by a boss called Hush, and the Fortnite Wiki says she left the Island in the Fortnitemares update, although some guides still list her. If you ever see a Boss Drop event on your map, it might be worth a look.

The automatic 8-Bit Shotgun with a stylish bonus: hit someone while you're in the air or sliding (a "trick shot") and you get some health or shield back. Pull off tricks and it keeps you alive.

The normal Drum Gun was vaulted for Fortnitemares, but this Exotic version may still turn up. The longer you hold the trigger, the faster it fires (up to a third faster) and the faster you run (up to half as fast again). Terrifying up close. We couldn't find anyone who has confirmed seeing one since 1 October, so treat it as very rare.

A Mythic Wood Stake Shotgun, with more damage and a faster reload. It's a fine weapon, but there's a big catch: it's dropped by the creepy animatronic robot bosses from Five Nights at Freddy's. Scare level: high.

The B tier

The silliest weapon in the game, and we love it. The 1st, 12th and 24th shots of every magazine burst into balloons, which tie themselves to anyone nearby. One balloon makes you floaty. Two make you floatier. Three lift you clean off the ground. Brilliant for comedy; slightly less brilliant for winning, because it does less damage than a Legendary Assault Rifle.

A pumpkin rocket that starts slowly, slowly enough for you to jump on and ride it, before speeding up. It's a tribute to the rocket-riding of Fortnite's earliest days. Great fun, and handy for a surprise entrance. Look for it in the chest on top of the giant pumpkin at Freaky Fields.

Who are the bosses?

Bosses are computer-controlled characters with lots of health, who guard their treasure. The Fortnitemares update changed the line-up: the two bosses who were Fortnite's own characters (Hush and Dr. Eggman) have gone, and every boss left on the Island is a guest from another company's game, film or TV show.

How to beat a boss

  1. Don't go alone if you can help it. Bosses have hundreds or even thousands of health.
  2. Keep your distance. Most of them are strongest up close. An Assault Rifle from medium range is safer than a shotgun.
  3. Use cover. Adam Smasher fires swarms of homing missiles; get behind something solid.
  4. Watch your back. Other players hear the fight and come to steal the loot.
