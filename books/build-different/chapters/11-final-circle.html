Chapter 11
The final circle

The player count reads 12. The circle is tiny, gunfire echoes from every direction, your hands are slightly sweaty and you can feel your heart thumping. Welcome to the endgame: the final few minutes of a match, when the Victory Royale is won or lost.

Endgames feel chaotic, but the players who win them aren't simply lucky. They prepare earlier in the match, they get into position before everyone else, and they stay calm while others panic.

Pack for the endgame

You can't win an endgame with an empty backpack. From the middle of the match onwards, start thinking about what you'll need at the very end. A strong endgame loadout looks something like this:

Pack for the endgame
1ShotgunClose fights
2Rifle or SMGEverything else
3ShieldsAs many as you can
4More healsHealth or both
5MobilityFor moving zones
Build mode: also fill up on materials (aim for 300+ of something). Zero Build: mobility matters even more.

In Build mode, materials are your most important resource in the endgame. The final circles often turn into a forest of player-made walls and ramps, and running out of mats leaves you unable either to protect yourself or to move. Use the quieter stretches of the match to gather extra, and collect mats from every player you eliminate.

Get there first

Chapter 9's advice to "rotate early" becomes even more important in the endgame. Once the circle is small, every good position has been claimed by somebody. The players who arrive first choose the best spots (high ground, solid buildings, big rocks), while those who arrive last have to fight their way in past everyone else.

The best endgame spots offer three things:

  • Cover on several sides, so you can't be shot from every direction.
  • Height, so you can see what's happening around you.
  • An escape route, in case the next circle moves the other way.

The circle moves

Here's something that catches out a great many players: during the last few phases, the safe zone doesn't merely shrink. It slides across the map. A circle that was centred right on top of you might drift away, forcing you to pack up and move, possibly straight through other players. That's precisely why a mobility item is so valuable in the endgame, and why your escape route matters. Always ask yourself, "Where would I go if the circle moved away from me?"

Remember, too, that by this stage the storm is lethal, removing about 10 health every second in the final phases (Chapter 9). You can't survive in it for long, so when the circle moves, move with it.

Let them fight

When only a few players remain, every fight is noisy and everyone is close together. If two other players start fighting, there's no need to join in. Let them wear each other down, then deal with the winner (the third-party tactic from Chapter 6). The fewer fights you take, the more health and resources you'll have left for the final one.

However, don't hide so well that you stop paying attention. Keep watching the player count and the storm timer, and look out for players who may be planning exactly the same trick against you.

Build mode endgames

Build endgames are the most chaotic situations in Fortnite, with dozens of walls and ramps and everyone trying to climb higher than everyone else. A few tips will help you keep your head:

  • Don't build a giant tower too early. It consumes your mats and advertises your location to every player in the lobby. Stay low and protected until you actually need to move.
  • Protect your ramps. When you rotate, build walls on the side facing the enemies, and floors beneath your ramps, so nobody can shoot your path out from under you.
  • Height is powerful, but expensive. Being highest is wonderful until you run out of mats or the circle moves away, so make sure you can afford to stay up there.

Zero Build endgames

Without building, endgames revolve around the land itself:

  • Claim a building or a hill inside the circle as early as possible.
  • Move from cover to cover whenever the circle shifts, avoiding open ground if you possibly can.
  • Save a mobility item for the final circles, because it may be your only way out of a bad spot.
  • Make the most of your Overshield. Remember that it refills if you avoid damage for a few seconds, so break line of sight whenever you can.

The last fight

Two players left: just you and them. This is when the tension becomes intense, and when many players make hasty decisions, such as rushing in, wasting shots or forgetting to heal. Instead, try this:

  1. Breathe. Seriously: one slow breath helps steady your hands.
  2. Heal to full before the fight begins, if you can.
  3. Let them come to you, if the storm allows it, because the player who is forced to move is usually at a disadvantage.
  4. Use everything you've learned: crosshair at head height, shoot when they're exposed, and push when they're cracked.

And if you don't win? Finishing second out of a hundred is a fantastic result. Watch the replay (Chapter 12), work out what happened, and claim the next one.

Common mistake: the victory dance

You eliminate someone in the endgame, and your character is still dancing an emote when a third player knocks you out. Save the celebrations for the lobby! Until the Victory Royale screen actually appears, keep looking and listening.

Try this drill: the endgame diary

Every time you reach the top 10, write down three things immediately afterwards: how many mats or heals you had, where you were standing when the last circle closed, and what eliminated you (or how you won). After ten endgames, read your notes back. You'll almost certainly notice a pattern, such as "always low on shields" or "always caught moving late", and that pattern tells you exactly what to fix.
