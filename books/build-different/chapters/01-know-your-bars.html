Chapter 1
Know your bars

Look at the bottom of your screen in any Battle Royale match and you'll see two bars. They're the most important numbers in the game, and good players always know exactly what's in them. Before we get to the clever stuff, let's make sure you do too.

Two bars, 200 points

The green bar is your health. You start every match with 100 of it. When it reaches zero, you're out (or, in Duos, Trios and Squads, you're knocked down and need a teammate to revive you).

The blue bar is your shield. You start with none, but you can fill it up to 100 by drinking shield items. Shield sits on top of your health and absorbs damage first. So a player with full shield and full health has 200 points to lose before they go down. A player with no shield has only 100. That's why the first thing good players do after landing is look for shields: without them, you're twice as easy to knock.

There's an important exception. Storm damage and fall damage ignore your shield and go straight to your health. So a full shield won't protect you if you sit in the storm, or jump off a cliff. You'll find out much more about the storm in Chapter 9.

Zero Build bonus: Overshield

In Zero Build, you usually get a third bar, called Overshield, worth up to 50 extra points. It sits on top of everything else and takes damage first. The clever part: it refills by itself if you avoid getting hit for a few seconds. So in Zero Build, breaking line of sight and hiding for a moment is like a free heal.

Reading the damage numbers

Every time you hit someone, a number pops up showing how much damage you did. The colour tells you a story:

  • Blue numbers mean you hit their shield. They've still got some protection left.
  • White numbers mean you hit their health. Their shield is gone. They're vulnerable.
  • Yellow numbers mean a headshot (a "critical hit"), which does extra damage.

Good players watch those colours closely. The moment the numbers turn white, the enemy is "cracked" (their shield is broken), and that's often the perfect moment to push in and finish the fight before they can heal.

Count the damage

Try to add up the damage you've done in a fight. If you've hit someone for 150 and they had full shield and health (200), you know they have just 50 left, even if you can't see their bars. If you've taken 150 yourself, it's time to back off and heal.

Healing the smart way

Healing items replenish your bars, but they aren't all the same. Some heal health, some heal shield, and some take ages. Using them in the right order is one of the easiest ways to become a better player, and it costs nothing but a little thought.

Shield up in the right order
1stSmall ShieldsOnly work up to 50, so use them first
2ndBig ShieldsTake you from 50 to 100
3rdMed KitHealth, after the fight
LastChug JugFills both, but slow

Here's why the order matters. A Small Shield Potion only works up to 50 shield. If you drink a big Shield Potion first and reach 50, your small ones become useless until you get hit again. Drink the small ones first, then use the big ones to go from 50 to 100. No wasted potions. That's efficient.

A few more healing rules every good player follows:

  • Heal behind cover. Most healing items make you stand still (or move very slowly) while you use them. Out in the open, that's an invitation to get shot.
  • Shield before health in a fight, because shield is usually quicker to drink. Top up health when things calm down.
  • Save the Chug Jug. It fills everything, but it takes about 12 seconds. It's for when you're safe and badly hurt, not for the middle of a gunfight.
  • Don't carry a full stack of something you can't use. Five slots is not a lot. If you're full on shield, don't let bandages take up a spot your shotgun needs.

Common mistake: healing in the open

You win a fight, your shield is gone, and you immediately start drinking potions right where you're standing. Then someone else, who heard the whole fight, finishes you off. Before you heal, move: duck into a building, behind a rock, or (in Build mode) put walls up around yourself. Ten seconds of safety beats a full shield bar you never get to use.

Try this drill: the bar check

For your next five matches, every time you finish a fight, say your numbers out loud before doing anything else: "Shield 25, health 80." Then decide: heal, move, or keep looting? It sounds silly, but it trains your brain to always know what's in your bars, which is exactly what pros do without thinking.
