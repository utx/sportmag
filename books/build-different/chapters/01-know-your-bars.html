Chapter 1
Know your bars

Glance at the bottom of your screen during any Battle Royale match and you'll see two bars. They're the most important numbers in the game, and experienced players always know precisely what's in them, even in the middle of a chaotic fight. Before we move on to cleverer tactics, let's make sure you understand them completely.

Two bars, 200 points

The green bar is your health. You begin every match with 100 of it, and when it reaches zero you're eliminated (or, in Duos, Trios and Squads, you're knocked down and need a teammate to revive you).

The blue bar is your shield. You start with none, but you can fill it up to 100 by drinking shield items. Your shield sits on top of your health and absorbs incoming damage first, so a player with full shield and full health has 200 points to lose before going down, while a player with no shield has only 100. That simple piece of arithmetic explains why good players hunt for shields immediately after landing: without them, they're twice as easy to knock out.

There's one important exception. Storm damage and fall damage ignore your shield entirely and go straight to your health. A full shield therefore won't protect you if you linger in the storm or leap off a cliff. You'll learn much more about the storm in Chapter 9.

Zero Build bonus: Overshield

In Zero Build, you usually get a third bar, called Overshield, worth up to 50 extra points. It sits on top of everything else and takes damage first. The clever part is that it refills automatically if you avoid being hit for a few seconds, so in Zero Build, breaking line of sight and hiding briefly works like a free heal.

Reading the damage numbers

Every time you hit someone, a number appears showing how much damage you dealt. Its colour tells a story, and learning to read it gives you information your opponent would rather keep secret:

  • Blue numbers mean you hit their shield, so they still have some protection left.
  • White numbers mean you hit their health: their shield has gone, and they're vulnerable.
  • Yellow numbers mean a headshot (a "critical hit"), which deals extra damage.

Skilled players watch those colours closely. The instant the numbers turn white, the enemy is "cracked" (their shield is broken), which is often the ideal moment to push forward and finish the fight before they can recover.

Count the damage

Try to keep a running total of the damage you've dealt in a fight. If you've hit someone for 150 and they started with full shield and health (200), you can work out that they have only 50 left, even though you can't see their bars. Equally, if you've taken 150 yourself, it's time to back away and heal.

Healing the smart way

Healing items replenish your bars, but they differ enormously. Some restore health, some restore shield, and some take an agonisingly long time. Using them in the correct order is one of the simplest ways to improve, and it costs nothing except a little thought.

Shield up in the right order
1stSmall ShieldsOnly work up to 50, so use them first
2ndBig ShieldsTake you from 50 to 100
3rdMed KitHealth, after the fight
LastChug JugFills both, but slow

Here's why the order matters. A Small Shield Potion only works up to 50 shield. If you drink a big Shield Potion first and reach 50, your small potions become useless until you take more damage. Drinking the small ones first, then using the big ones to climb from 50 to 100, means no potion is ever wasted. That's what being efficient looks like.

A few more healing rules that good players follow:

  • Heal behind cover. Most healing items force you to stand still (or shuffle very slowly) while you use them, which, out in the open, is practically an invitation to be shot.
  • Shield before health during a fight, because shield items are usually quicker to drink. Top up your health once things have calmed down.
  • Save the Chug Jug. It refills everything, but it takes about 12 seconds, so it belongs in safe moments when you're badly hurt, not in the middle of a gunfight.
  • Don't carry a full stack of something you can't use. Five slots isn't many. If your shield is already full, don't let bandages occupy a space your shotgun needs.

Common mistake: healing in the open

You win a fight, your shield has gone, and you immediately start drinking potions exactly where you're standing. Then another player, who heard the whole fight, arrives and finishes you off. Before you heal, move: duck into a building, crouch behind a rock, or (in Build mode) put walls up around yourself. Ten seconds of safety is worth more than a full shield bar you never get to use.

Try this drill: the bar check

For your next five matches, whenever you finish a fight, say your numbers out loud before doing anything else: "Shield 25, health 80." Then make a decision: heal, move, or keep looting? It might feel silly at first, but it trains your brain to track your bars constantly, which is exactly what professionals do without thinking.
