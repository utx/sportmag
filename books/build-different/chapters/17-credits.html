About this book

Build Different is an unofficial fan book made by The Chug Jug, a fan magazine about Fortnite. It isn't made, approved or endorsed by Epic Games. It's free, and it will never ask you to spend money. If you ever want to buy anything in Fortnite, ask a grown-up first.

Portions of the materials used are trademarks and/or copyrighted works of Epic Games, Inc. All rights reserved by Epic. This material is not official and is not endorsed by Epic.

Version

This book was written in October 2026, during Fortnite Battle Royale Chapter 7, Season 4: Override (version 42.30). Most of it is about skills that work in any season. Where we give numbers that can change (such as storm damage, revive and reboot times, and the Ranked rules), they are correct as far as we could check on 14 October 2026. Epic changes things often, so if the game doesn't match the book, trust the game.

Pictures

Outfit pictures (Jonesy the First, Wrixel, Mali, Peely, Fishstick, Lynx, Raven, Brite Bomber, Skye, Drift, Meowscles, Renegade Raider and Midas), item icons, the Island map and the lobby backgrounds are by Epic Games / Fortnite, used under Epic's Fan Content Policy. They came from the game's own art, via fortnite-api.com and the Fortnite Wiki's copies of the in-game icons. All of them are Epic's own characters: we haven't shown characters or items that belong to other companies.

All the diagrams, charts and the cover design were drawn for this book by The Chug Jug. The storm-rotation map is an example we drew on top of this season's map, not a real match.

Where the facts came from

Game mechanics (health, shield and Overshield, the knocked state and reviving, materials, building, movement, the storm phases, Storm Surge and storm sickness, Reboot Vans, settings and Ranked): the Fortnite Wiki (fortnite.weirdgloop.org, a copy of the Fortnite Fandom wiki), checked in October 2026 (its pages are based on Epic Games' update notes).

Chapter 7 changes (Simple Build, saved reload progress, knocked-player movement and Reboot Vans): Video Games Chronicle (30 November 2025), GameRant (1 December 2025) and Beebom (Simple Build guide, February 2026).

Replays: guides from Prima Games and TheSpike.gg. Ranked: the Fortnite Wiki. Sprites in Ranked: Epic's in-game news (via fortnite-api.com, October 2026).

Advice and drills are our own, based on the game's mechanics and on common practice advice from coaches and experienced players. They're suggestions, not rules: if something else works better for you, do that.

Play it safe

  • Nobody is giving away free V-Bucks. If a website, video or message offers them, it's a scam.
  • Never share your password or your account with anyone, not even a friend.
  • This book never recommends cheats, hacks or glitches. They get accounts banned, and many of them are scams.
  • Check the age rating of any Creative island before you play it.
  • Take breaks. The Island will still be there after dinner.
