Chapter 8
Zero Build hero

In Zero Build, nobody can put up a wall to save themselves. That changes everything. The Island itself becomes your shield: every tree, rock, car, hill and house is a place to hide. And the way you move between them becomes your biggest weapon.

Zero Build players sometimes get told that it's the "easy" mode. Don't believe it. The best Zero Build players move like parkour athletes, read the map like a chess board, and have aim that's as good as anybody's. Here's how to join them.

Your movement toolkit

Fortnite has added lots of movement moves over the years. In Zero Build, you need all of them. Here are the ones every player has, without needing any special items:

A few extra details make these moves much more useful:

  • Sprinting uses stamina. When your stamina runs out, you slow down until it refills. Don't waste it running round in circles. Save it for crossing open ground or escaping.
  • Sliding is faster downhill and slower uphill, and you can shoot while you slide. Sliding into a fight down a slope is a great way to arrive fast and hard to hit. Slide into an opponent and you'll even kick them backwards.
  • Mantling cancels fall damage. If you're falling from a height and grab a ledge on the way down, you're safe.
  • You can do up to three wall kicks in a row, each a little weaker than the last. Chaining kicks between two walls close together is a sneaky way to get onto a roof.
  • Ledge jumps: jump right at the edge of a drop while you're moving fast, and you'll leap further and higher than a normal jump.
  • Roll landings also give you back some stamina, so a big drop followed by a roll keeps you sprinting.

Put them together and you become agile: slide down a hill, hurdle a fence, wall kick onto a roof, and you've crossed a fight zone in a few seconds.

Cover is everything

The golden rule of Zero Build is simple: never stand where you can be shot from lots of directions. Before you move anywhere, look at where your next bit of cover is. Then move from cover to cover, like stepping stones across a river.

When you reach each piece of cover, stop for a moment. Look and listen (Chapter 4). Then pick the next one. It feels slower than sprinting straight to where you want to go, but you'll arrive with your health. Players who sprint across open fields are exposed the whole way, and they're the easiest targets in the game.

Use the land

Zero Build is all about the terrain:

  • Hills are free high ground. In Build mode you can make height. In Zero Build, you have to find it. Hilltops, cliffs and tall buildings are prime real estate, especially later in the match.
  • Valleys and rivers are traps. Anyone above you can see you. Cross them quickly, or go round.
  • Buildings are fortresses. Thick walls, doors that make noise when enemies open them, and windows to shoot from. In the late game, getting inside a good building can win you the match.
  • Behind the hill, not on top. When you're on a hill, stay just behind the top edge, peeking over to shoot. Standing right on top shows your whole body to everyone.

Mobility items are your ramps

In Build mode, you escape by building. In Zero Build, you escape with mobility items: launch pads, grenades that fling you into the air, gliders, vehicles and anything that lets you move fast. Each season has different ones (this season, the Overdrive Grenade and the Crash Pad Jr. are favourites). Whatever they're called, try to carry one. A mobility item can get you out of a lost fight, away from the storm, or onto the high ground in the final circle.

Zero Build bonus: Overshield

As we saw in Chapter 1, Zero Build usually gives you up to 50 extra Overshield, which refills by itself if you avoid damage for a few seconds. That means breaking line of sight is a kind of healing. Take some damage, duck behind a rock, count to a few seconds, and some of your protection comes back for free.

Pro move: slide-jump

Start a slide down a slope, then jump at the end of it. You'll carry your momentum into the jump and fly much further than a normal jump. Chain slide, jump, slide, jump down a long hill, and you'll cover ground very quickly.

Common mistake: peeking the same spot twice

You peek over a rock and shoot. You duck down. Then you peek from exactly the same place again, and your opponent, who has been aiming at that spot the whole time, hits you straight away. After each peek, move: pop up from the other side of the rock, or slide to a different piece of cover.

Try this drill: the parkour run

Pick a route through a town on the Island (or a Creative parkour island) and try to cross it using as many different moves as you can: slide, hurdle, mantle, wall kick, roll landing. Then try to do it without ever stopping. You'll learn which walls you can kick off, which roofs you can mantle onto, and how far a slide-jump takes you, all of which you'll use in real fights.
