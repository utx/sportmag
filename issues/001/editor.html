Welcome to Touchline

Hello, and welcome to the very first issue. Here's the idea: every week we'll bring you the biggest stories in football, from Old Trafford to the Bernabéu to Moore Park, plus the best of the rest of sport and the games worth playing. We'll explain what happened and why it matters, and we won't talk down to you, because you're clever enough to keep up.

You'll notice some words are highlighted. Those are our Word Watch words: interesting, useful and occasionally ferocious. Tap one to see what it means, then find them all again in the Word Bank at the back.

One more thing. We try very hard to be fair. We do not always succeed when Manchester United are involved. Consider yourself warned.

— The editors
