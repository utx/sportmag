Reading the table

P = played, W = won, D = drawn, L = lost, GD = goal difference (goals scored minus goals conceded), Pts = points: three for a win, one for a draw. When teams are level on points, goal difference separates them. Notice something? Only four points separate 12th-placed United from fourth place. Four. That's one win and a draw. We're just saying.
