Every October, Fortnite puts on its Halloween costume. Fortnitemares 2026 arrived on 1 October with update 42.30, and it has given Chapter 7, Season 4 ("Override") a spooky makeover: new locations, a terrifying boss and a bag of brand-new weapons. The event runs until around the start of November, so you've got a few weeks to master it. Here's your guide.

What's newThe spooky stuff

  • Nightmare Neighborhood has replaced The Battlewoods. It's home to a boss borrowed from a famous horror movie. Beat him and he drops a Nightmare item that lets you teleport close to your next opponent for a surprise slash.
  • Freaky Fields is the other new Fortnitemares location. Expect pumpkins. Lots of pumpkins.
  • Fortnite Reload got its own Halloween season on 8 October, Nitemare Island: Kado's Curse, with special Siphon loot that heals you whenever you deal damage.

How to surviveSix pro tips

Tip 1
Overrides are a trade

Grabbing an Override Console can give you a big advantage for the rest of the match, but everyone knows you've got it. Be ready for company.

Tip 2
Think before Squad Overrides

The buffs are tempting, but they broadcast your squad's location. Only take one if your team is healed, stocked and ready for a fight.

Tip 3
Vault hunting

At Stone Sanctum, find the two Vault Keys hidden around the area and open the Temple Vault for rare weapons and Sprites.

Tip 4
Fish for gold

Golden fishing spots, like the one on Pac-Man Island, are a quiet way to pick up good items without a gunfight.

Tip 5
Recon first

Use the Bonerattler's wall-vision to check a building before you push in. Walking into an ambush is the quickest way back to the lobby.

Tip 6
Bosses can wait

Boss loot is great, but bosses attract crowds. Sometimes the smartest move is to let two squads fight over it, then tidy up.

The battle pass

This season's pass costs 800 V-Bucks. The headline is Sonic the Hedgehog, who unlocks as soon as you buy it, with no levelling needed. Other outfits include Bastian, Grace Crowne, K1tty W1ns, Wrixel, Mali, Phantom and Geno. Fortnite Crew members get the pass as part of their subscription. Gift the pass to a friend and you both get a bonus outfit. (Always ask a grown-up before buying anything.)

Not just FortniteThree more worth your time

Football · Out now
EA Sports FC 27

Released on 25 September. The big changes are on the pitch: more manual passing, cleverer curved attacking runs, off-balance dribbling and less help when defending. It's harder, which means it rewards real skill. Ultimate Team adds a new FUT Gallery that remembers every player who has passed through your club.

Touchline tip: the best way to learn the new passing is a few slow games with a team you know well. We can't think of one.

Building · Out now
Minecraft

The autumn update, Wilderness Bound, adds a new dappled forest biome, filled with poplar trees whose leaves come in three different colours, plus a new wood type to build with. Perfect for camouflage builds and autumn-themed houses.
