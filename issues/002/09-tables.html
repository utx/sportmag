Reading the table

P = played, W = won, D = drawn, L = lost, GD = goal difference (goals scored minus goals conceded), Pts = points: three for a win, one for a draw. United are 13th with six points, but look closer: Leeds, seventh, have only three more. Win at Elland Road on Sunday and United could climb as high as seventh, depending on other results. That's how tight it is in October.
