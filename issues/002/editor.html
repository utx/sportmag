Welcome to Issue 2

We'll be honest: it has not been a vintage week to be a Manchester United supporter. A goal conceded in 47 seconds, a red card that nobody (well, nobody in red) thought was a red card, and a trip to Elland Road to come. If you'd like to skip straight to the happier pages, we understand.

But football is much bigger than one bad week, and this issue is full of reasons to love it. There's a chart that sorts every footballer in Europe into scorers and creators, and shows that United's captain is the best chance-maker of the lot. There's Barcelona's chase for a record that has stood since 1968, a Swedish match that went from 0–0 to 5–6 in one half, and proof that Sydney FC have won more trophies than any other club in the A-League era.

As always, the highlighted words are your Word Watch words, and they're all waiting for you in the Word Bank at the back. And remember: stay until the final whistle. You never know.
